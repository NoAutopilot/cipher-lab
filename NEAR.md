# NEAR.md: the near-solve register

Owner's concern, 25 Sept 2026 (18:25 UTC clock read): "make sure we're not being shortsighted with these near solves ...
don't want a near solve to be marked as such and then logged no and forgotten." Instituted by parent 7c the same hour
(UPDATES.md). Both parents read this file at every check-in; every retrospective reviews it; the board shows it.

**What goes here.** Any target where a solver beat its matched control by a reproducible margin, or a control showed
the negative was not a real test (transcription noise, a mismatched corpus, a control below its gate), or a first
test put the text with the language controls -- and no verifier class has settled it. A row enters when the lane
posts the numbers; it leaves only when (a) the named next step has run with its control and the numbers are in the
row, or (b) a verifier classes the target in AUDIT.md. A target with a row here may be `partial` or `open`, never
`closed-negative` (CLAUDE.md rule 5 as amended 25 Sept 2026): closing needs every family in its ladder logged with a
passed control. Rows are edited in place; the history stays in git.

**Review rule.** A row untouched for 48 hours is a flag for the parent, not a reason to drop it: the parent names a
worker or a blocker (ASKS row) in the row. `tools/near_check.py` (to be built) exits nonzero when a NEAR target's
NOTES.md reads `closed-negative` or a row is stale.
