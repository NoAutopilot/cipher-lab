Enciphered letters that a held key (or a published one) could read but that no cloud route reaches. One row per order
unit, ranked for the owner: free tier first, then (unread cipher units x key weight) / price. The machine-readable copy,
with every tariff source quoted in full, is `COPY-ORDERS-2026-10.tsv`. Nothing below is ordered, emailed or filled in.

Source rows: KEYHUNT-2026-10-07.tsv lines 7, 16, 36, 58, 82-86, 104 (fr.2988 lead), 117, 162-165, 172, 199-202,
218/230, 219/231, 239, 243, 245, 248-251, 285, 305, 322-330. Merged duplicates (162+243, 163+245, 218+230, 219+231,
322-330 as one row) and split line 58 into its MHi and NYPL halves: **19 order rows covering about 50 letter items, 10
dropped with a reason.** The brief's "~33" counted items, not orders. The count is lower because the italien 1583
letters and the Clairambault pages are each one order per volume.

**Key weight:** 1 = a published or period key for that channel; 0.75-0.5 = the right office, but which key or which
date is unproven; 0.25 = key fit unknown, or no working key (pool material only); 0 = a crib, not a cipher unit.
**Units** = letters (or fragments) with unread cipher; estimates are marked. **Images** = an estimate at about 2 pages
per letter. **Price** is the archive's own tariff for those images. GBP is converted at a rough 1.15 for ranking only.
This is not a quote.

## Tariff pages read (one per archive, 7 Oct 2026 unless stated)

- **BnF**: https://www.bnf.fr/fr/commander-une-reproduction-sans-utilisation-commerciale, read 21:5x UTC. A manuscript
  digitised by the page for reading use (PDF) is EUR 0.70 HT a page. A whole manuscript volume is EUR 130 HT. The minimum
  order is EUR 3 HT. Non-commercial and academic use pays no licence fee. No free tier.
- **Archives nationales**: https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/quelles-sont-les-modalites-de-reproduction-et-de-reutilisation
  and its "Décision tarifaire reproduction 2026" PDF (signed 16 Jul 2026), read 21:5x UTC. A4/A3 digitisation is
  **free for 1-19 views**, EUR 10 flat for 20 views and EUR 0.30 a view beyond that. Email or download delivery is free.
  Remote requests go only through the SIV personal space and need the exact cote. The host loaded from the cloud this
  time, although earlier notes said it was blocked.
- **Archives diplomatiques**: tarifs_reproduction_2026_06_15.pdf, read 6 Oct 2026 by OUT-CHECK-REQ2678 (cited in
  `ciphers/decode-2678-bnf-colbert127-gravel-1665/REQUEST.md`; not re-fetched). Hand-held photos are **free for 1-19
  views**, EUR 10 for 20 views and EUR 0.50 a view beyond. Orders go on the form only, with cote and folios.
- **Massachusetts Historical Society**: https://www.masshist.org/library/reference-reproductions, read 21:5x UTC. Up to
  **25 reference images are free**. Beyond that the charge is USD 25 a folder or volume. Turnaround is 12-16 weeks.
- **British Library**: rate card from the BL's own reply of 6 Oct 2026 (`outreach/QUOTES.md`): GBP 27.70 for the first
  image, then GBP 7.25 for each further image of the same manuscript. No free tier.
- **TNA**: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/record-copying/fees/, read 21:4x UTC. A page check
  is GBP 9.92 per record. A digital copy up to A3 is GBP 1.52.
- **ANTT**: https://antt.dglab.gov.pt/servicos/reprografia/ leads to Despacho 6852/2015, Anexo I, read 21:5x UTC.
  Partial digitisation from the original is EUR 0.75 an image, plus EUR 2 for fragile originals. The column alignment
  was read from a PDF text extraction, so confirm it on the quote. No free tier found.
- **BNE**: tariff page unreachable from the cloud (HTTP 403; Cloudflare to the headless browser on the one retry after
  60 s). A 2022 Wayback capture lists the products but no prices.
- **NYPL**: tariff page unreachable from the cloud (Incapsula; the browser retry returned an error page).
