How we know

The Abbé Pareti's letter of 13 Sept 1728 to the Earl of Marchmont (Huntington mssBLA 186), on Ripperda's escape, was printed in English extract by Rose in 1831 (Marchmont Papers ii, 414-15, dated there 3 Sept), with its cipher line omitted. Its two cipher lines read in part (20 of 24 groups) with a key set out from contemporary decipherments in the same collection. No prior decipherment of those lines was located in the sources listed in AUDIT.md (N3).
