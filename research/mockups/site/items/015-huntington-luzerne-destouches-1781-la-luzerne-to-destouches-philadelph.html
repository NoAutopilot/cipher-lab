La Luzerne to Destouches, Philadelphia 16 Jan 1781 (Huntington mssDE 108(A)): partial reading with a key rebuilt from three sibling letters' contemporary decipherments

The claim

mssDE 108(A), La Luzerne to Destouches, 16 Jan 1781, is the original of a letter whose duplicate in the same collection (mssDE 108(B)) carries Destouches's own contemporary decipherment; a copy is in AAE, CP États-Unis vol. 15 (Idzerda, Lafayette Papers 3:319). Our partial key reading of 108(A) (C 347, M 157, U 215 of 719) agrees with it where checked and is superseded by it (N0).

How the reading regenerates
  • Transcription: ciphertext.tsv, ciphertext_decipher.tsv
  • Key: key.tsv, key_conflicts.tsv, key_tomokiyo.tsv, key_yale_crosscheck.tsv
  • Reading: https://github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/blob/main/ciphers/huntington-luzerne-destouches-1781/reading_108A.txt

Run python3 tools/decode_key.py ciphers/huntington-luzerne-destouches-1781 --check from a clone of the repository: it rebuilds the reading from the transcription and the key, and exits with an error if the committed reading is stale.
