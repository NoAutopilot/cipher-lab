The claim

Read with War Department Cipher No. 1; the plain text is known from the period clear copy in the Eckert Papers (Huntington pointer <n>, in the holder's public transcription) [or: printed in <OR/Butler page>]; our reading is an independent re-decipherment of the Fort Monroe cipher copy.

The verifier's safe sentence, quoted from AUDIT.md.

At 10 PM on 22 Apr 1864 Meigs tells Major Van Vliet, quartermaster at New York, to complete the supply of tugs, ferry boats, barges and schooners for both Fort Monroe and Washington, says that steamers enough to move the troops are now engaged, and orders him to devote himself to hastening the vessels' arrival, since delay would be most injurious.
