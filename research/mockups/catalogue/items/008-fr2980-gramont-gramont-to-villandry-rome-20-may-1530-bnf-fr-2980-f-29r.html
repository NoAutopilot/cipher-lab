Gramont to Villandry, Rome, 20 May 1530 (BnF fr. 2980 f.29r)

The claim

A 569-sign cipher passage read with the published Gramont 1530 key: 533 signs valued after a blind second reader's corrections, several lines continuous French. No printed plaintext or decipherment located after two logged searches. The second leaf (f.30, 1,973 signs) now reads with the same key on 22 of 55 lines and is with the verifier.

From the results register; no matching safe sentence was found in AUDIT.md for this entry, so read the audit before quoting it.

Gramont tells Villandry that he has given the bearer an article set apart, addressed to Villandry although it is for the King, and calls it 'le total fondement'. (interpretation: the verifier's English paraphrase of the read passage)
