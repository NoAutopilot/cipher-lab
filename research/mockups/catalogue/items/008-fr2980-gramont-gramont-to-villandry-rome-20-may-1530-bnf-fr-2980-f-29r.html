Gramont to Villandry, Rome, 20 May 1530 (BnF fr. 2980 f.29r)

Gramont tells Villandry that he has given the bearer an article set apart, addressed to Villandry although it is for the King, and calls it 'le total fondement'. (interpretation of the reading, in English)
