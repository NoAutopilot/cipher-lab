Eckert 1864: General-in-Chief to Hunter, Cedar Creek, 22 May 1864 10.30 PM, not sent: no reinforcements without Grant's orders (E106)

On the night of 22 May 1864 Halleck's office drafted, and did not send, a telegram telling Hunter at Cedar Creek that no reinforcements could go to his department without Grant's special orders and that all available troops had been ordered elsewhere by Grant; the reply sent the next morning (E9) instead offered him brigadiers.
