Gustav II Adolf to Axel Oxenstierna, Nürnberg 23 July 1632 (printed cipher, Oxenstierna series letter 602): found solved

The claim

Letter 602 (Gustav II Adolf to Oxenstierna, 23 July 1632) was deciphered and printed by R. Torpadie in Historisk tidskrift 8 (1888), pp. 376-383. Our cryptanalytic re-reading of the letter layer (grades H0 C0 S665 M29 I0 U77, with a matched control at 98.0%) agrees with his, but it is not a new result.

How the reading regenerates
  • Transcription: ciphertext.txt, ciphertext_key.tsv, ciphertext_verified.tsv
  • Key: key.tsv
  • Reading: https://github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/blob/main/ciphers/oxenstierna-gustav-adolf-1632/reading.txt

Run python3 tools/decode_key.py ciphers/oxenstierna-gustav-adolf-1632 --check from a clone of the repository: it rebuilds the reading from the transcription and the key, and exits with an error if the committed reading is stale.
