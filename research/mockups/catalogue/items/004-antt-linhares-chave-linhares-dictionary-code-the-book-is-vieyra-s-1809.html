Register line

The key sheet in the Condes de Linhares papers (ANTT PT/TT/CLNH/0086/11) says each number points to a page, column and word of 'o Diccionario'. tested free scans against its worked example and found the book: A New Pocket Dictionary of the Portuguese and English Languages, abridged from Vieyra, Part I, London 1809; all twelve example groups land on the exact word. The two-page mid-letter fragment on m0002 then decodes 25 of 26 groups at grade H, one at M; pages 1 and 4 of the letter are not in the item, so no connected gloss is offered yet. A separate verifier classes both the reading and the identification N3. Safe sentence in AUDIT.md. On 25 Sept the Portuguese language judge, re-run on a corpus of the letter's own period (Correio Braziliense and O Investigador Portuguez, 1808-1819, 3.2 million letters), passes the reading (-1.044 against a real-text floor of -1.100; 192 of 200 genuine period windows pass the same gate), so the reading now counts; it stays below N4 because the edition most likely to print this letter, Coutinho's Textos Políticos, Económicos e Financeiros (Banco de Portugal, 1993), has not yet been read; we is on it.
