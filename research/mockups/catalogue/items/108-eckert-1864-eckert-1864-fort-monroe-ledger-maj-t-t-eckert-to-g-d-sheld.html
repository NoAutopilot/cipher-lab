Eckert 1864 (Fort Monroe ledger): Maj. T. T. Eckert to G. D. Sheldon, Fort Monroe, 27 May 1864, Bickford's party and material for 20 miles of line to West Point (E214)

On 27 May 1864 Eckert sent the operator Bickford's line party to West Point with material for twenty miles of telegraph toward Grant's new base. (interpretation of the reading, in English)

What can be said

Read at grade H with War Department Cipher No. 1: on 27 May 1864 Eckert told G. D. Sheldon at Fort Monroe that Bickford had 10 miles of wire, 3 miles of insulators and 18 miles of spikes, to send him to West Point with enough material to make 20 miles, and that operators were ordered to report. Sheldon's reply of 29 May is printed in the Official Records (ser. I vol. 36 pt 3 pp.321-322); this telegram was not located there or in Plum's Military Telegraph (searched 9 Oct 2026).
