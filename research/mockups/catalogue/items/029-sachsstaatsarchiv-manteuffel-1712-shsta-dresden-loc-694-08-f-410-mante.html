SHStA Dresden Loc. 694/08 f.410, Manteuffel to Flemming, Berlin, Nov 1712: unglossed cipher P.S. read with Dr. Krauske's 1893 manuscript key table (Loc. 694/10, unprinted)

In this postscript the Queen of England is named three times: once in a phrase reading 'touchant le prince', and once followed, after two unread groups, by 'ne fera rien pour lui, mais luy...'. (interpretation of the reading, in English)
