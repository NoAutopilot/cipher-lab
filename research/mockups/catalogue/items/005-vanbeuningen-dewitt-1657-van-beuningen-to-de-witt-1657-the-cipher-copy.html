Van Beuningen to De Witt, 1657: the cipher copy the 1919 edition called 'unsolved' now has a key

Van Beuningen writes that the King of Denmark's agent in London reports hopes that the English ambassador will be instructed to negotiate an alliance. (interpretation of the reading, in English)

What can be said

Nationaal Archief 3.01.17 inv.nr 1538 holds the plain copy (ff.208-209) and the cipher copy (ff.210-211) of the 19/29 Sept 1657 letter printed in Brieven aan Johan de Witt I (1919) pp.405-406, whose footnote calls the cipher 'onopgelost'. Aligning the pair rebuilt 53 codes; a fresh re-derivation agrees on every code it found evidence for. A separate verifier classes the key N3; the letter's text is N1, since it is already printed. Named gap: Postma's 2006 dissertation on this correspondence was not reachable.
