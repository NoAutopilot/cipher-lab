How the reading regenerates

    Run python3 tools/decode_key.py ciphers/fr3621-dinteville-1592 --check from a clone of the repository: it rebuilds the reading from the transcription and the key, and exits with an error if the committed reading is stale.

    The control it had to beat
    • 527 tokens: C 177 M 311 U 39, no H, no S (strict rule). f.130 decode fr16 -1.271 vs 0/3000 free and 0/3000 frequency-banded shuffles
    • wrong-text control 0/20
    • word division fails its pre-registered control

    A reading counts only beside a matched control: the same method run on shuffled keys or a synthetic text of the same kind, with both numbers reported.
