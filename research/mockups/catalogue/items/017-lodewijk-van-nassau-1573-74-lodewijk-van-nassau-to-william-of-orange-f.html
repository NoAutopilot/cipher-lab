Lodewijk van Nassau to William of Orange, four cipher letters 1573-1574 (WVO 4610, 4611, 4612, 4616): partial reading with a key aligned from two sibling letters' decipherments

The claim

System: letters, five numbers each in blocks from n; numbers above 120 are names and nulls. Key aligned from WVO 4613 and 4615 against their contemporary decipherments. 4610, 4611 and 4616 read as French in stretches; most of 4612's keyed runs do not yet read as French. No letter reads straight through. 4610, 4611, 4616 N4: no prior decipherment located in the principal editions (Groen, Blok 1887 and 1889, Gachard, Kervyn, La Huguerye), the KHA inventory or WVO; Orange's printed replies (Groen IV, Lettres CDXXVII, CDXXXIII, CDLXXXIV) answer them. 4612 N3 until it has a reading.
