## What actually follows from the cipher
The key-derived runs read 'fur wenig tagen ... durch seine ... stadtliche gesanndthenn bei uns suchen lasen seinen sohn [König] ma[x]imilianum noch bei seinem des [Keiser]s leben ... romischen [König]e zu erwelen ... bei den andern [Churfurs]ten ... auch gesucht werden ... inn geheim ...'.
Thus the reported request to elect Maximilian King of the Romans during his father's lifetime, and the direction to keep the news secret, are strongly supported.
Five token exceptions are contextual rather than independently supplied by WVO 74:
- OQ -> wir, once;
- THE -> Keiser, twice;
- G1h -> x in Maximilianum, once;
- BOX -> Churfurs before 'ten', once.
The two additional M tokens concern an unusual tent in 'vertrawen' and an extra upright in 'seinee'. The generated text also retains COL markers, including within 'eiCOLem'; normalised prose should not pretend that every character is mechanically settled.
'Emperor' is therefore a strong contextual interpretation of a repeated word sign, not an independently recovered word-sign value. 'Maximilianum' has an inferred x. The recipient's expected action is election of a King of the Romans, not immediate election as Emperor.
