Specimens of Louis XIV's Great Cipher (1691)

Seven letters in code of Louis XIV and his war minister Louvois addressed to General Catinat in 1691 were decoded by Commandant Bazeries (see another article). The code has a code number (27) to delete the preceding number. John Wallis also reported use of a similar symbol in French ciphers of the same period (see another article).

The present article describes usage of the deleting symbol in the letters decoded by Bazeries, followed by decoding of the whole letter of 14 September 1691.
