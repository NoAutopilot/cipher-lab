# Folger manuscript sources (29 Sept 2026)

- `bennett-an-unsolved-puzzle-solved.pdf`: D. H. Bennett, "An Unsolved Puzzle Solved", NSA Cryptologic Spectrum
  (UNCLASSIFIED, US government work), 12 pages, from https://media.defense.gov/2021/Jun/30/2002752891/-1/-1/0/UNSOLVED%20PUZZLE.PDF
  (403 to our sessions; supplied by the project on 29 Sept 2026). Bennett's assumptions: English, monoalphabetic,
  uniliteral, clusters between spaces are words (about 12 strokes per cluster), and he recovers the cipher alphabet.
- S. Brent Morris, "The Folger Manuscript - A Lecture" (CMRC, 8 Sept 1999), text and figures 1-5 and 7 at
  http://www.themasonictrowel.com/articles/manuscripts/manuscripts/folger_manuscript/the_folger_manuscript_lecture.htm
  (reachable from the cloud by curl with a browser user agent; WebFetch got 503). Not copied here (copyright);
  read it at the link. It summarises Morris's book *The Folger Manuscript* (Masonic Book Club, 1992) and the
  earlier solution by Baden, and says the enciphered key phrase came from p.84 of the Macoy Book.
