2. **Searched.** Gallica IIIF manifests: 19 requests (fr.16039-16046, 16104-16110, 16142, 16144, NAF 22048, 22070), all 200. ROOM.md, QUEUE.md, NEAR.md, NEXT-STEPS.tsv, POOLS.tsv, `ls ciphers`: no folder or claim for Vivonne, Longlée, Noailles-Constantinople, Savary, Ferrals. SRU and the Gallica texteBrut were not used.
3. **Sample method and its limit.** Four evenly spaced canvases per volume (n=4-12 per pool), one vision look at a 2x2 sheet, no transcription. A full cipher page is about 1,000 signs (20-22 lines of 45-55). Cipher-bearing canvases were 2 of 4 in fr.16110, 16142, 16144, 1 of 4 in 16109, 16045, and 0 of 4 in fr.16040 and NAF 22048. The `est_signs` figures extrapolate from n=4-12 and are marked low confidence; only the "at least" lower bounds are measured.
