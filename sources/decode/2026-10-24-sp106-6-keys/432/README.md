# DECODE record 432, TNA_SP106/6_CharlesII_(0040-0042) -- NOT a match for Tomokiyo's no.31/32/35

Fetched as the strongest candidate from the cache (shelfmark matched: French plaintext, blank cleartext field --
a decoding-table shape -- and the only SP106/6 Key record with the exact date range 1662-1662, matching the
Chauran/Wickfort correspondence year). Full-size images came back real (PNG, not the `035489a0...` forbidden
placeholder), 3 pages, 1614-2300 px long side.

**DECODE's own "Additional Information" field (by Satoshi Tomokiyo, 21 Jan 2024), quoted verbatim:**

> A list of 31 pseudonyms in French. Names ans places suggest a colonial intrigue of some kind, related to North
> America, involving the Dutch and the highest levels of state, e.g. the King and Lord Arlington. (Satoshi
> Tomokiyo, 21 January 2024) The author "Sir George Downing" in metadata seems to belong to R433 (see the
> endorsement and the handwritten entry in the nomenclature therein).

This settles it: the "31" in DECODE's description is a count of pseudonyms on this sheet, not TNA's catalogue
item number "no. 31" from `chauran.htm`. The subject (a North America/colonial intrigue involving the Dutch,
the King and Lord Arlington) has nothing to do with the Chauran/Wickfort/De Bacquoy Spanish-ambassador traffic
this job is chasing. **Record 432 is not SP 106/6 no. 31, 32 or 35.**

No transcription attempted (per brief). At a glance: page 1 (`IMG_R432_I3545_P1.png`) is the dorse/endorsement
side of a folded document, rotated ~90 degrees in the scan -- a docket reading roughly "No 2" and a date "1660"
in a secretary hand, consistent with a cover/endorsement leaf rather than the pseudonym list itself (pages 2-3
presumably carry the list; not individually opened this pass). Language of the endorsement: French/English
mixed secretary hand, as expected for an SP 106/6 wrapper page.

Held here as a documented negative so a future worker does not re-spend a login fetching this same id again
under the same hypothesis.
