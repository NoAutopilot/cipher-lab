adds no new information a session could act on sooner. What the record actually shows killed the handoff, not the
work: LANE R5's own brief (the one actually used) already told it to check `rate_limit_info` every 30-45 minutes
and write the handoff only on `rejected` — a **terminal**, not incremental, requirement. Each of the three lane

## Proposal 2: generalize the matched-control lesson in CLAUDE.md rule 3 to a judge corpus's era, not only its language

Rule 3 already carries two instances of "same category, wrong specific instance invalidates the comparison" (design
at the right N/K; transcription convention). A judge corpus is a third: `pt17` is the right *language* for Linhares
but the wrong *era* (modern/canonical Portuguese vs. this letter's 1780s-1830s register), and that alone produced a
false FAIL that a fresh, era-matched corpus (`pt18`) reversed with a matching improvement in the control's own
false-negative rate (8.5% to 4.0%), not just the reading's score — evidence the fix was a real calibration
correction, not a lucky threshold shift.

```diff
--- a/CLAUDE.md
+++ b/CLAUDE.md
@@ rule 3
    A matched design is not enough if the two texts being diffed use different transcription conventions.
    Lesson of 25 Sept 2026 (PX-BRODEC): a leave-one-out control diffed a fresh body decode against the
    appendix's own period gloss and returned 50-68% against an 80% gate, apparently a failure -- but the gloss
    abbreviates ("S.d±" for "Snr D.") while the decode spells the word out, so most of the gap was notation, not
    error. Before diffing two renderings of the same underlying text for a gate, normalize both to one
    convention (expand abbreviations, one case, one punctuation set) or the gate measures whichever side
    transcribes more literally, not correctness.
+   A language corpus can be the wrong *era* even when it is the right language. Lesson of 25 Sept 2026
+   (V6-PTCORP, antt-linhares-chave): `tools/judge_plaintext.py`'s `pt17` corpus is modern/canonical Portuguese;
+   the target is an 1808-1819 letter. Under pt17 the reading FAILed (-1.145 vs real_p05 -1.101) and real prose
+   itself false-negatived 8.5% of the time; swapping to a corpus built from the letter's own period and register
+   (pt18: Correio Braziliense + O Investigador Portuguez, 1808-1819) PASSed the same reading (-1.051 vs real_p05
+   -1.122) and dropped the real-prose false-negative rate to 4.0% -- both sides of the gate moved together, which
+   is what a genuine calibration fix looks like, not threshold-shopping. Before running a language judge on a
+   target with a specific date and register, check whether tools/data's corpus for that language is era-matched;
+   if not, building one is a cheap job (12 minutes, V6-PTCORP) worth doing before trusting a FAIL or a PASS.
```

Motivating rows: LEDGER.md:677 (V6-PTCORP); ROOM.md 15:53 25 Sept (V6-PTCORP's own done line with both corpora's
numbers side by side).
