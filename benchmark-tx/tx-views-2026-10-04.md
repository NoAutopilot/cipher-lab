# TX-VIEWS: multi-view voting on Birago no.87 (4 Oct 2026, account 2 dispatched for the account-3 orchestrator)

## Pre-registration (written and pushed before any view read was made, 05:16 UTC)

- **Unit and scope.** f.178r + f.179r of no.87 (18 crops, 164 scored signs of the 803; benchmark item birago1572-no87,
  eval). This is the unit the original passes A/B were read in (one Sonnet call per reader, 18 crops; NOTES.md "Blind
  passes"). f.178v (639 scored signs, 69 crops, two calls per reader) is not read in this job: at three views it would
  add six calls beyond the cap. The result is therefore a pilot on 164 signs, and the sign test below is unlikely to
  reach p < 0.05 unless the effect is large; a miss is "untested at this N", not a refutation.
- **Views read** (3 calls, one per view, Sonnet, value-blind, the unchanged `harvest/blind_pass_brief_1572.md` and
  `sign_sheet_blind_1572.png`): `pad`, `s125`, `warp` from `tools/iiif_lines.py --views` (all five views are written to
  `harvest/views87/views/`; `s080` and `contrast` are not read). Voters: passA, passB (the existing plain-view reads) +
  the 3 views = 5, odd.
- **Primary test.** `tools/reconcile_passes.py passA passB pad s125 warp --vote` (star alignment on passA; a column
  emits its plurality sign when more passes have a sign there than a gap; a sign tie goes to the earliest pass), scored
  with `tools/tx_bench.py` against the 164 scored signs, compared with the **current reconcile** (passC, the committed
  transcription, which also had a third adjudicating reader and a sloped re-crop of f.178r L03 -- a home advantage)
  as a paired count on the same truth positions: fixed (wrong in passC, right in the vote) vs broken (right in passC,
  wrong in the vote). Adopt only if fixed > broken with a two-sided exact sign test p < 0.05.
- **Secondary (reported, not gating).** The same paired count vs passA alone and vs a plain 2-pass A+B reconcile
  (no adjudication); err_true of each single view read; pairwise error correlation (phi on per-position error
  indicators, and the share of one pass's errors another repeats with the same wrong sign) for every pair of the 5
  passes, to name the views whose errors are least correlated with A and B.
- **Cost.** 3 Sonnet subagent calls (views x 1 batch); per-pass estimate 1.5 (CLAUDE.md Usage 6 and the README floor).

## Result (4 Oct 2026, scored after all three reads landed)

Regenerate: `sh benchmark-tx/build_views87.sh`, then
`python3 tools/reconcile_passes.py $B/passA_rr.tsv $B/passB_rr.tsv $B/pad.tsv $B/s125.tsv $B/warp.tsv --vote --err-truth benchmark-tx/birago1572-no87.truth.tsv --out-dir $B/vote5`
and `python3 tools/tx_bench.py $B/vote5/vote.tsv --paired $B/passC_rr.tsv` (B = benchmark-tx/outputs/birago1572-no87/views).

**Primary gate: FAIL.** Vote vs passC, paired on the same 164 signs: fixed 0, broken 6, sign test p = 0.031 -- the vote is
worse than the current reconcile, not better. Not adopted. Vote vs passA: fixed 0, broken 0 (the vote reproduces pass A's
errors exactly); vs the 2-pass vote: identical.

Why, from the numbers:
- **A crop error no view can vote away.** Seven f.178r L03 signs (pos 24-25, 30-34) are deleted by all five readers: the
  fixed-band crop cuts the sloping L03 tail off, which passC recovered from the sloped re-crop (NOTES.md "Blind passes").
  A view of the same crop cannot restore pixels the crop lacks. Excluding those 7, vote vs passC is fixed 0, broken 1
  (157 signs, C wrong 6, vote wrong 7, p = 1.0) -- a tie at the level of one sign.
- **The views' errors repeat A's.** Every one of pass A's 14 errors is repeated by pad and by warp, and 13 of 14 by s125
  (repeat 0.93-1.00; phi with A 0.71-0.76, higher than A-B's 0.65). The views are worse readers alone (0.14-0.17 vs A's
  0.085) and their extra errors are scattered, so the majority falls back on A. Least-correlated pairs: B-s125 phi 0.415,
  B-pad 0.429, B-warp 0.49; among the views s125 is the least correlated with A (0.712) and with the other views
  (0.546 pad, 0.562 warp); pad-warp is the most correlated pair (0.876, 18 same-wrong signs). The arXiv 2509.09722
  pattern (the least-correlated view helps the vote) does not show here because no view is decorrelated from the best
  reader on its errors: at this hand the errors sit in the glyphs, not in the presentation.
- Reader note (pad and s125 readers): f.178r crops show neighbouring lines' rows at top and bottom and the L01/L02 tails
  spill into the next crop; both read tails across crops at M/L. The pad reader disclosed that a count loop printed the
  warp files' per-line counts (no signs read); the pass is kept and this is logged.

What this does and does not say: 164 signs is a pilot (pre-registered as such); f.178v (639 signs) was not read. The
result is a FAIL of the pre-registered gate on this unit, and a clear pattern (views repeat the strongest reader's
errors) -- not a refutation of view voting in general. Next instrument, not a further view knob: a different reader
(another model tier, research #1/#10) or the sloped crops for every leaf (the one error class here that all readers
shared is the crop). Cost: 3 Sonnet subagent calls (about 106-115k subagent tokens each).
