Oracle boxes (luzerne): verify the cuts

Verify the machine-proposed sign boxes and their reading order on these lines (one hand). For each box: accept it, or mark it a bad cut and move, split or merge it with Fix the cut; add a box where a sign has none. Never read a value. Time the first 100 signs and write minutes per 100 signs in the result before continuing; stop at 1,500 signs. The two piles are only the machine's cut kind (a sign box, or a small mark with no sign in reach), not a reading.

Step 1 · Clean each pileLook along a pile. Tap any sign that is not like the others: it moves to the "Taken out" tray at the bottom. Tap it there to put it back. When a pile looks right, press "This pile is done".
See a sign largerPress and hold a sign (or press "View larger" on a pile): it opens on its line, the sign in brackets, with Next and Previous.
Step 2 · Place the taken-out signsEach taken-out sign is shown large with every other pile below it, closest-looking first. Drag it onto its pile, or just tap the pile. "None of these: new sign" starts a new pile; nothing to type.

How to fix a cut

Drag an edge of the box, or drag inside it to move the whole box. Drag a round corner handle to move that corner alone (a slanted or uneven box for a wide looped sign). On a phone, the arrows move one edge or the chosen corner 2 pixels a tap. "Erase stray ink": paint over a neighbour's ink inside the box and it is left out of the tile (shown as paper); "Undo stroke" takes back the last stroke. A sign with no tile of its own: move the box onto it and press "Add as a missed sign" (this tile's cut stays as it was). A box that holds two signs: fit it to one of them and press "Split: keep this part, add the rest"; the rest of the box becomes a sign of its own. Added signs get a pile on the next page build; Undo takes one back.
