Asked by the owner on 11 Oct 2026 about 14:5x UTC: "Were we ever describing pages to describe instead of sending the images?"
Sweep: four independent read-only agents (by benchmark pass, by target folder, by job brief, by tool), merged 15:25 UTC (workflow wf_436351dc-099).

## Answer as corrected by the check

- Sign readers always saw the page image; the part usually given in words was the sign alphabet. "Never in words" is not established for the page: some readers also got parts of the page in words (location anchors, page-layout notes, e.g. Dinteville), and some decodes rest on text transcriptions with no image read.
- Words-only alphabet behind current results: the three published Saint-Gouard readings (inks 63, 53_G, 54_L; 15,762 + 4,380 + 1,954 tokens, H 78.0% / 85.5% / 76.3% since VIV-ANCHOR 8 Oct); Clairambault 1161 (3,389 signs; key ours, fitted on words-only reads, splits settled by a worker on the crops); Dinteville f.130 (527) and the f.128 key rebuild (an Opus reconciliation from zoomed crops seeded by two words-only passes); Juan Manuel 1521; Vargas Mexia vowel signs and marks; August of Saxony WVO 53 and 57 (126 does not); Bergh (no reading yet); the f.61 Mayenne base transcription (its scoring corrections came from pictured reads).
- Not words-only: Gunther WVO 8246 benchmark passes had 10 pictured calibration crops; the Saint-Gouard pictured sheet sheet_tomokiyo_v1.png WAS used once, on the f.102r dev2 benchmark read (TXE2-SHEETVIV, 10 Oct: 58 edits vs 86/80 for the word list), never on a published reading.
- Rules today: TRANSCRIPTION.md requires the family image atlas before any line read for a symbol cipher with siblings (skippable with a stated reason) and owner-labelled tiles as the exemplar sheet once owner decisions exist; LESSONS.md puts a pictured tile sort before transcription on an invented alphabet. No file requires a pictured alphabet in the line-read prompt itself, and the skip clause was used whenever no picture was on disk.
- Effect size so far: Birago no.87 dev, one run per arm: 67 vs 15 of 343 wrong (word list vs picture, as measured); Vivonne f.102r dev2: 86/80 vs 58 edits, line-level not significant. The picture helps; it does not fix a hard hand by itself.

## Merged sweep answer (before the check)

Checked at 15:25 UTC on 11 Oct 2026, against origin/main f3b817861. Read-only: nothing was edited or committed.

1. No reader ever got a page described in words. Every reader pass in the repo was sent images of the page: line crops, sign tiles, windows or montages. Words were only ever used for the alphabet the reader matched signs against.
2. Several hands got that alphabet only as words, although a picture could have been cut:
   - Saint-Gouard 1572-74 (the Vivonne letters) and Saint-Gouard 1580
   - Dinteville 1591-92
   - Clairambault 1161 "Advis de flandres"
   - August of Saxony, and Günther (words plus 10 labelled line crops)
   - the vowel signs and marks of Vargas Mexía
   - the Mayenne family code book, Juan Manuel and Bergh
3. Results that rest on those word-list reads:
   - Three published Saint-Gouard readings, about 22,100 signs.
   - Clair1161: 3,389 signs, and its key was recovered from those reads.
   - Dinteville f.130 (527 signs) and the key rebuilt from f.128.
   - 15 of the 24 benchmark rows. That includes the Vivonne confirm look, and the Dinteville rows have the worst single-reader error (0.19-0.27).
4. No rule today says "give readers a picture of the alphabet before any read". TRANSCRIPTION.md asks for a family atlas first, and briefs skipped that whenever no picture was on disk.
5. How big the effect is: on Birago, one run per arm, readers made 67 errors in 343 signs from a word list against 15 from the picture (4.5x). On Vivonne the difference was only 334 vs 302 as measured (+11%), not significant line by line. So the picture helps, but it does not fix a hard hand alone.
6. What the tools can do without you:
   - Cut the picture automatically, from a printed key drawing or from tiles of the hand.
   - Add a gate that refuses any reader brief giving a hand of drawn signs its alphabet only in words.
   - Re-read the letters in the order below. The Saint-Gouard picture already exists and has never been used on a reading.
