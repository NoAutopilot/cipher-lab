la Colección Salazar y Castro catalogue on Internet Archive (identifier `salazary-castro-22-nov-2016`, not on

Charles V's Genoa and Venice embassies) in a correspondent pair, Lope de Soria and Alonso Sánchez, whose ciphers

this repo (`sources/cryptiana/web/AlonsoSanchez.htm`, 761 lines, never before cross-referenced against these
seven RAH items by shelfmark).** This is a recovery shape (apply an already-published key to an archive-held,
never-transcribed passage), not a from-scratch cryptanalysis, and none of the seven items appears in `ciphers/`,
QUEUE.md, NEAR.md, POOLS.tsv, or DECODE's own non-decrypted catalogue (`sources/decode/records-non-decrypted-
2026-09-24{,-diff}.tsv`, 1187 rows, whose RAH Salazar y Castro coverage stops at Signatura 9/28-9/31 -- these
seven items are all at A-33 and higher, i.e. Signatura 9/33+, structurally outside what DECODE has catalogued).
**Caveat, matching D1-CHECK's own honesty standard:** no page image of any of the seven folios has been seen this
session (RAH's own bibliotecadigital.rah.es is Anubis-challenged, confirmed again this session, HTTP 307); the

document, not on this session having seen a digit. The Alonso Sánchez key is dated 1522; two of the five items
below (1524, 1528) postdate it by 2-6 years -- period ciphers were sometimes replaced, so "same key still valid"
is inferred, not confirmed, until a page image is read.
