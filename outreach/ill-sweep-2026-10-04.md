# Interlibrary-loan batch, 4 Oct 2026 (ILL-SWEEP, account 3)

status: drafted -- for the owner to submit through the public library's interlibrary-loan form. Nothing in this file
is ordered or sent. No personal data and no card numbers here, by design.

Built 4 Oct 2026, 19:20-19:4x UTC (by `date -u`), from a script grep of every `ciphers/*/NOTES.md`, `AUDIT.md` and
`REQUEST.md` (162 lines matching not opened / NO_PAGES / snippet only / print-disabled / lending-only / not digitised
with a dated printed work), the open rows of ASKS.md, the queued LOCAL-QUEUE.tsv rows of kind edition-read, ia-reader
and catalogue-lookup, and the seed items in the brief. Kept only printed books and articles (manuscripts are archive
copy orders, out of scope) where a named page range or chapter answers a named open question. Before keeping an item
I checked whether it is already readable online: Open Library search API, HathiTrust bibliographic API by OCLC, and
the Google Books API (key, `country=US`). About 40 requests, at least 1.5 s apart. Where I could not confirm an OCLC
number, the field says so, and the ISBN or Google Books id is given in its place. The library can search by those.

How ILL usually works: a **book** comes as a physical loan of the whole volume. An **article or chapter** comes as a
scan, usually capped at one chapter or about 10% of a book. A request that names its pages is more likely to be filled.

Priority: **A** = the answer could change a target's status or N-class. **B** = settles a named gap or check. **C** =
context, nice to have.
