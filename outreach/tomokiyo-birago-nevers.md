Dear Mr Tomokiyo,

A follow-up in our thread, on work that rests entirely on your keys. I direct this project and send its messages; the transcriptions, the tests and the searches were done by AI agents (Claude models) working in an open repository, with every step logged at the links below.

We applied your Nevers-Birago Cipher (1572), the table on your Nevers page rebuilt from the decipherment attached to no.87, to Lodovico Birago's 1572 letters in BnF fr.3251. Each cipher passage was transcribed sign by sign without the key's values, and only then decoded. We changed one value: a sign that carries g in your table, as we cut it, reads m in these letters, and the clerk's decipherment of no.87 gives m for it at 11 of its 12 occurrences there. Everything else is your table as printed.

Two letters now read through all their cipher, though only in fragments. Spelling is as decoded, the word division is ours, and [...] marks signs we cannot read or words left out.

- no.86, Saluzzo, 27 August 1572 (ff.174r-175v, 759 signs: 629 read firmly, 76 uncertain, 54 not in your table). "ambidoi sono [bugonotti]" (a code word, spelled so in your table) -- our interpretation: "both are Huguenots". "per uoler fare [...] la mala gratia d altri" -- our interpretation: Birago fears that wanting to do [his] service may earn him the ill will of others.
- no.90, Saluzzo, 2 October 1572 (ff.184r-185v, 966 signs: 730 firmly, 125 uncertain, 111 not in your table). "religione [...] altroui sarano se[...]pre confusioni" -- our interpretation: over religion "elsewhere there will always be confusions". "intendere a la regina" -- our interpretation: someone is to "make the queen understand" something; which queen is not in the cipher.

On each letter your key beats all 200 shuffled keys (z about 3.6-3.8 for no.86 and about 4.5 for no.90; the last 20-sign run of no.90 is too short to test on its own). Neither reading passes our language test, and much of each is still broken words, so these are partial readings, not texts. No prior decipherment located after searching both volumes of the 1665 Mémoires de Monsieur le duc de Nevers, your catalogue, the BnF catalogue, the solver repositories and the open indexes, 2 Oct 2026.

no.71 (f.139v) gives a partial reading of its five cipher lines, about 116 of 161 signs, under the same key.
