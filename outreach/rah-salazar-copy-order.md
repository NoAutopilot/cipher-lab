Dear Library of the Real Academia de la Historia,

I write on behalf of a small non-commercial project that reads cipher passages in digitised historical letters. One person directs the project and sends every message; the reading of the images, the searches of the editions and the audits are done by AI agents (Claude models) working in a public repository, where every step is logged. I say this at the outset so you know whom you are dealing with.

I would like to request digital reproductions of seven documents from the Colección Salazar y Castro, or, if you prefer, a quotation under your request form. I cite them by shelfmark and by the inventory number of the printed Índice: (1) A-33 ff.72-74, inv. 4670, Alonso Sánchez to Charles V, Venice, 17 Dec 1524; (2) A-37 ff.363-371, inv. 5520 and 5521, original and duplicate, Alonso Sánchez to Charles V, Venice, 5 Jun 1526; (3) A-37 ff.428-429, inv. 5540, Lope de Soria to Charles V, Genoa, 16 Jun 1526; (4) A-37 ff.438-440, inv. 5543, Alonso Sánchez to Charles V, Venice, 18 Jun 1526; (5) A-37 ff.444-447, inv. 5545, Alonso Sánchez to Charles V, Venice, 19 Jun 1526; (6) A-41 ff.193-195, inv. 6192, Alonso Sánchez to Martín de Ugarte, undated, 1527; (7) A-42 ff.441-442, inv. 6501, a deciphered text of the cipher paragraphs of three letters of Alonso Sánchez.

The reason: the Índice describes the first six as letters with undeciphered cipher passages, and the seventh is a period decipherment from the same series. Both correspondents' ciphers are reconstructed in published work (Kolosova's dissertation on the ciphers of Charles V; S. Tomokiyo's study of Alonso Sánchez's cipher), so a reading of those passages is possible and could be offered to the Academy. Before asking you for anything we checked that these seven documents are not in your Biblioteca Digital and that their cipher passages are not translated in the Calendar of State Papers, Spain, nor in the two works of Rodríguez Villa the Índice cites; the full record of those checks is at the link below.

If digital reproduction is not possible and a reading-room visit is required, I would be grateful to know. Any reading we obtain will be published in the repository under the MIT licence, citing the Academy and the shelfmark, and sent to you if it is of interest.

Thank you for your attention.
