1. A-33, ff. 72-74 (inv. 4670): Alonso Sánchez a Carlos V, Venecia, 17 de diciembre de 1524.
2. A-37, ff. 363-371 (inv. 5520 y 5521, original y duplicado): Alonso Sánchez a Carlos V, Venecia, 5 de junio de 1526.
3. A-37, ff. 428-429 (inv. 5540): Lope de Soria a Carlos V, Génova, 16 de junio de 1526.

Dear Library of the Real Academia de la Historia,

I write on behalf of a small non-commercial project that reads cipher passages in digitised historical letters. One person directs the project and sends every message; the reading of the images, the searches of the editions and the audits are done by AI agents (Claude models) working in a public repository, where every step is logged. I say this at the outset so you know whom you are dealing with.

I would like to request digital reproductions (scans) of seven documents from the Colección Salazar y Castro. According to your "Reproducción de documentos" page, requests are made on your request form and the work is sent once the quotation has been accepted and paid; I would be grateful for that quotation, and will send the completed form if this email is not enough as a first step. I cite them by shelfmark and by the inventory number of the printed Índice: (1) A-33 ff.72-74, inv. 4670, Alonso Sánchez to Charles V, Venice, 17 Dec 1524; (2) A-37 ff.363-371, inv. 5520 and 5521, original and duplicate, Alonso Sánchez to Charles V, Venice, 5 Jun 1526; (3) A-37 ff.428-429, inv. 5540, Lope de Soria to Charles V, Genoa, 16 Jun 1526; (4) A-37 ff.438-440, inv. 5543, Alonso Sánchez to Charles V, Venice, 18 Jun 1526; (5) A-37 ff.444-447, inv. 5545, Alonso Sánchez to Charles V, Venice, 19 Jun 1526; (6) A-41 ff.193-195, inv. 6192, Alonso Sánchez to Martín de Ugarte, undated, 1527; (7) A-42 ff.441-442, inv. 6501, a deciphered text, in a hand of the period, of the cipher paragraphs of the three preceding documents (inv. 6498-6500: original, duplicate and triplicate of Alonso Sánchez's letter to Charles V, Venice, 17 Jun 1528).

The reason: the Índice describes the first six as letters in cipher, or with cipher passages, whose deciphered text does not follow them, and the seventh is a period decipherment of another letter of Alonso Sánchez, useful for checking his cipher. Both correspondents' ciphers with the Emperor are studied in published work (O. Kolosova's dissertation on the ciphers of Charles V's diplomacy, 1521-1527; S. Tomokiyo's study of Alonso Sánchez's 1522 cipher), so it may be possible to read those passages (which cipher each letter uses is still to be checked) and offer the reading to the Academy. Before asking you for anything, we searched your Biblioteca Digital for these documents without finding them and checked that the cipher passages of the six letters are not published in the Calendar of State Papers, Spain, nor in Rodríguez Villa's two works (Italia desde la batalla de Pavía hasta el Saco de Roma, 1885; Memorias para la historia del asalto y saqueo de Roma en 1527, 1875). For no. 4 the Índice notes "Publicada en parte por Rodríguez Villa, ob. cit. pp. 133 y 134", but those pages print a passage dated 13 June 1526. The full record of those checks is at the link below.

If digital reproduction is not possible and a reading-room visit is required, I would be grateful to know. Any reading we obtain will be published in the repository under the MIT licence, citing the Academy and the shelfmark, and sent to you if it is of interest.

Thank you for your attention.
