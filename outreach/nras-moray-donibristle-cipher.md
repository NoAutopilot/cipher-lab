Dear Registrar,

I direct a small open research project on unread historical ciphers and I send every message myself; the searching, transcription and comparison behind this enquiry were done by AI agents (Claude models) working in an open repository where every step is logged: https://github.com/noautopilot/cipher-lab/tree/main/ciphers/moray-wood-1568

We are working on a short cipher postscript in a letter from the Regent Moray to John Wood, 13 July 1568 (British Library, Add MS 32091 ff.213-214). We have a partial proposed reading of it, with about an eighth of the signs still uncertain, and what would settle it is any other text written in the same cipher, or a key sheet for it.

The Historical Manuscripts Commission's Sixth Report (p.638), describing the Earl of Moray's papers then at Donibristle, reports cipher papers among them that, as far as we have found, have never been reproduced. We understand these papers are now registered with you as NRAS217, and another researcher's notes place them in Box 45; we have not been able to confirm that ourselves.

I would be grateful to know: (1) whether the NRAS217 survey lists cipher letters, cipher papers or a key of the 1560s, and under which box and item numbers; (2) whether Moray's letters to John Wood of August-September 1568 are listed there; and (3) how a request to see or obtain copies of such items should be made, given that the collection is privately held.

A single page from the survey listing, if it can be shared, would be enough for us to tell whether a copy request is worth making.

Thank you for your help.
