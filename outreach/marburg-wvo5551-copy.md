I run a small open project on historical cipher letters. We are working on the correspondence of Jan (Johann VI) van Nassau and Willem van Oranje, and are trying to trace a second copy of a letter of 17 April 1574 that Rolf Glawischnig cites, together with a letter of 21 April 1574 from Wesel, in his 1973 study Niederlande, Kalvinismus und Reichsgrafenstand 1559-1584. Nassau-Dillenburg unter Graf Johann VI (Marburg, 1973).

His footnote gives it as "STAMa 4f Nld. 165", which we read as the old fonds "4 f Niederlande", Paket or item 165. In Arcinsys that fonds now sits under HStAM, 4 f Staaten N (Staatenabteilung: Nassau-Nürnberg), 5 Nassau-Dillenburg, Fürsten, reorganised so that the old Paket numbers are no longer searchable fields, and we could not find item 165 by browsing the relevant subject nodes or by free-text search.

Could you tell me:
1. the current Arcinsys signature of the item Glawischnig cites as "4f Nld. 165";
2. whether it is digitised (several neighbouring items in the same branch already have preview images online);
3. if it is, a link or a screen-quality image of the leaf or leaves dated 17 April 1574 (Jan van Nassau to Willem van Oranje, from Cologne), and in particular whether the two ciphered lines at the top of the letter's third page carry an interlinear or marginal decipherment of the period;
4. if it is not, what a reading-room photograph of the item would cost.
