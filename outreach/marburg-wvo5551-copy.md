Dear Hessisches Staatsarchiv Marburg,

One person directs this project and sends this message; the reading, the archival research and the audits are done
by AI agents (Claude models) working in an open repository, every step logged. We work on the correspondence of Jan
(Johann VI) van Nassau and Willem van Oranje, and are trying to trace a second copy of a letter of 17 April 1574
that Rolf Glawischnig cites (together with a letter of 21 April 1574 from Wesel) in his 1973 study (Niederlande,
Kalvinismus und Reichsgrafenstand 1559-1584. Nassau-Dillenburg unter Graf Johann VI, Marburg, 1973).

Glawischnig's footnote cites it as "STAMa 4f Nld. 165" -- which we read as his shorthand for the old fonds "4 f
Niederlande", Paket (or item) 165. That fonds is now catalogued in your Arcinsys system under HStAM, 4 f Staaten N
(Staatenabteilung: Nassau-Nürnberg), 5 Nassau-Dillenburg, Fürsten, reorganised so that the old Paket numbers no
longer appear as searchable fields; we were not able to locate item 165 by browsing the relevant subject nodes or
by free-text search.

We would be grateful if your staff could tell us:
1. What the current Arcinsys signature is for the item Glawischnig cites as "4f Nld. 165".
2. Whether it is digitised (several neighbouring items in the same branch already carry preview images online).
3. If digitised: a link, or a screen-quality image, of the leaf(s) dated 17 April 1574 (Jan van Nassau to Willem
   van Oranje, from Cologne) -- in particular whether the two ciphered lines at the top of the letter's third page
   carry an interlinear or marginal period decipherment.
4. If not digitised: what a reading-room photograph of that item would cost.

Full detail on the letter and what we have found so far is open at
https://github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/tree/main/ciphers/jan-van-nassau-1572-75 .
