Hello, and thank you for the project. We checked your unsolved-ciphers repository and decode-catalog.csv
(24-25 Sept 2026) for this shelfmark, ark and sender/recipient and found no entry, so this is a candidate
addition.

BnF Espagnol 144 f.22r-22v (within the Espagnol 142-144 codex, TOME III; the BnF finding aid's item 11), an
unsigned Spanish instruction to the baron (abbé) de Mercy, dated Barneton 6 June 1648, has been read in part by
our own cryptanalysis, in the wording of our audit: "BnF Espagnol 144 f.22, an unsigned Spanish instruction to
the abbé (Baron) de Mercy dated Barneton 6 June 1648, has been read in part by our own cryptanalysis (a
38-value letter cipher; 496 of 522 code tokens at grade S, 26 at M, none from a key source or known plaintext);
no prior print of its deciphered text and no prior decipherment were located in the sources logged in this
AUDIT.md, searched 25 Sept 2026 (two independent verifier passes). The reading is a cryptanalytic result, not
confirmed by a key or a clear copy."

la France et de l'Espagne aux Pays-Bas (1635-1700)* (1896), p.445 n.2, which cites two neighbouring instructions
to Mercy (15 April and 30 August 1648) without printing either:
