Dear Archives and Local Studies Centre staff,

I run a small open project on unsolved historical ciphers. I direct it and send its letters myself; AI agents (Claude models) do the reading, the catalogue and edition searches, and the checking, every step logged in the public repository linked below.

We have been looking at two letters from Edward Hyde (later Earl of Clarendon) to Sir Richard Fanshaw, written from Brussels on 4-14 June 1659 and 14-24 January 1659/60. The 1899 Historical Manuscripts Commission report on the papers of J. M. Heathcote of Conington Castle prints both letters, but only with the editors' own English reconstruction of the passages that were in cipher in the manuscript -- the report's preface says the editors partly recovered the cipher's key from a garbled attempt at it that survives among Fanshaw's own papers, and names a related, dated, published Hyde cipher table sent to Peter Barwick eight days after the first letter, which we have on file and would like to test against the originals. The National Archives' Discovery catalogue (record N13657938) names your service as the holder of the Fanshawe papers, M51-286, covering this period, with a note that the collection is split with Huntingdonshire Archives; we have checked Huntingdonshire's own catalogue directly and the two 1659 items are not there. Could you tell me whether these two letters are held at Valence House, whether their ciphered passages (the manuscript's own figures, not the printed gloss) are legible, and whether R. M. Walker and W. H. Liddell's 1999 finding aid for the Fanshawe papers, or any later work, notes that either has been deciphered? I would also be grateful for a quote for a scan of the two letters.

If the cipher groups survive and are legible, we believe we already have a strong candidate key for them -- Hyde's own cipher table of 12 June 1659, printed in the Life of Dr John Barwick and already transcribed on our side -- and I would offer back whatever we recover, openly, with full credit to your service as the holding archive.

Everything we have on this lead, including our search log and the candidate key, is at https://github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/blob/main/ciphers/_triage/heathcote-fanshawe-1659.md
