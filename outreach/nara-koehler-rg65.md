We are studying Walter Koehler, an Abwehr agent whom the FBI ran as a double agent. A German teleprinter letter of February 1944 to the Abwehr's Paris radio station ("Abwehrleitstelle Frankreich, Paris Funkstelle") quotes five enciphered messages about him, printed by David Kahn in Cryptologia 5(2) (1981), and we have not been able to read them.
