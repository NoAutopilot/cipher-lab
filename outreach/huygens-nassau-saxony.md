Dear ______,

We are a small cipher-reading project that works from the letter images WVO publishes. Six letters in the database
have cipher passages that WVO records as unread. We have read them in part with keys taken from contemporary
decipherments of sibling letters, also in WVO, and found no prior decipherment in the principal editions. We send the
readings with what the printed editions already give, so that your editors can judge whether any of it is worth a note
in the records.

**A. Lodewijk van Nassau to Orange, 1573-1574 (WVO 4610, 4611, 4616)**

Key: aligned from the contemporary decipherments of WVO 4613 and 4615.

- 4610: "WVO 4610 (Lodewijk van Nassau to Orange, 3 June 1573, duplicate) is partly read with a key aligned from the
  contemporary decipherments of WVO 4613/4615. No prior decipherment was located in the principal editions (Groen,
  Blok 1887 and 1889, Gachard, Kervyn), the KHA inventory or WVO; see AUDIT.md." Groen prints Orange's reply of 17 June
  (Archives, 1st ser., IV, Lettre CDXXVII, pp.156-160; WVO 4497). What the cipher adds: Louis's side, the proposal to
  convene the Electors and the French contacts, which the reply only answers.
- 4611: the same wording, for 2 July 1573. Groen IV, Lettre CDXXXIII, p.175ff (WVO 4498) acknowledges it. What the
  cipher adds: particulars of recruiting, including an offer by Van Minnen of twenty companies for Friesland.
- 4616: "WVO 4616 (12 April 1574, near Goch, two days before Mookerheyde) is partly read with the 4613/4615 key; no
  prior decipherment was located in the principal editions, the KHA inventory or WVO; Orange's reply of 15 April 1574
  (Groen IV CDLXXXIV) acknowledges it." (Groen IV pp.368-369; WVO 4503.) What the cipher adds: little beyond march and
  lodging.

**B. Orange and Elector August of Saxony, 1561-1564 (WVO 53, 57, 126)**

- 53: "The cipher postscript of Orange's letter to Elector August, Breda 24 Oct 1561 (Dresden Loc. 9941/3 f.266r-v;
  WVO 53), reads with a cryptanalytically recovered key (S 238, M 126 of 364 signs; no key source; matched control
  99.3%) as news that the Prince of Spain would marry his father's sister and come to govern the Netherlands, that the
  Duke of Vendôme would recover Navarre by agreement or by war, and Orange's view that, the King of France being so
  young, it would come to nothing. This is a cryptanalytic result. No prior decipherment was located in the principal
  editions (Groen, Gachard, Rachfahl, von Weber, Kluckhohn; Japikse's edition ends in September 1561) or in a phrase
  and gist search (AUDIT.md, 24 Sept 2026). The rumours themselves are in print from other senders."
- 57: "The cipher enclosure of Elector August's letter to Orange, Torgau 18 Nov 1561 (KHA A 11/XIV B/41-6; WVO 57),
  reads with the key of Orange's 1562 cipher (C 247, M 53 of 300 signs) as secret news that the Emperor had asked
  August to elect Maximilian King of the Romans in the Emperor's lifetime. No prior decipherment was located in the
  principal editions (Groen, Gachard, Rachfahl, von Weber, Kluckhohn, Goetz; Japikse's edition ends in September 1561)
  or in a full-text phrase search (AUDIT.md, 24 Sept 2026). Demandt's regest of the letter (HessJb 38, nr. 113) covers
  only the clear text. August's minute in Dresden, with a 'Zettel', has not been seen and may carry the enclosure in
  clear." The approach itself is printed from the Palatine side (Kluckhohn, *Briefe Friedrich des Frommen* I (1868),
  nr. 148, https://archive.org/details/briefefriedrichd00frie, all volumes in one item); the cipher adds that August
  told Orange, in secret, within days.
- 126: "The cipher postscript of Orange's letter to Elector August of 16 Sept 1564 (Dresden Locat 8510/5 f.139; WVO
  126) reads, with a key aligned from his 1563 letter WVO 98 and its contemporary decipherment, as news from Spain that
  the Queen had been bled and purged twice and had lost the child (C 214, M 26 of 240 signs). No prior decipherment was
  located in the principal editions (Groen, Gachard, Rachfahl, von Weber), the KHA and WVO records, or a full-text
  phrase search (AUDIT.md, 24 Sept 2026). Orange's minute in the KHA (A11-XIVi-04-26), recorded with a 'Zeitung', is
  unimaged and may carry the text in clear." The event is printed (Cabié, *Ambassade en Espagne*, 1903, pp.289-290,
  https://archive.org/details/ambassadeenespa00cabigoog/page/289);
  the cipher adds only the channel.
