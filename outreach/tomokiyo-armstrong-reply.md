Dear Mr Tomokiyo,

Thank you for the Raince post, and for the credit. The Robertet letter of 24 October on f.24 is a useful addition; we
have recorded your post in the repository's citations file.

We took up Armstrong's letter of 20 February 1808 as you suggested and have now run two cycles on it. The findings,
each with its control:

- The manuscript is transcribed from the NARA microfilm (M34 roll 14) and agrees with the Founders Online text at
  0.957; the disagreements are a units-digit skew the page itself confirms.
- No key or decode appears to survive: the Papers of James Madison editors record that Madison never acknowledged
  receipt and that the letter was never decoded. The AFIO claimed solution and its rejection are on file.
- The sibling tables we could assemble (Monroe's WE028, 1,600 entries; the THE 972 code from Bourdeau's page and your
  partial transcription) all run block-wise alphabetical, and none shows Armstrong's two features: a strong skew
  toward units digits 0 and 1, and an empty band between 900 and 1099. Crib placement, sibling-code vocabulary
  transfer and a nomenclator solver were each run against a structure-matched control and none beat it.
- The marks beside the numbers were checked symbol by symbol against ten period shorthand systems (Taylor, Gurney,
  Byrom, Mavor, Mason, Lewis, Blanchard, Annet, Holdsworth and Aldridge, Lewis 1820) with a Pitman control; none
  matched. They look most like a private symbol set.
- We searched for a second letter in the same code: the State Department dockets, the five Livingston letters of
  1807-09 and 35 pages of the Armstrong-Jefferson channel are all clear text or office code.
- The letter's 369 groups include 139 that occur once, which caps any context-only solver near the gate. Only more
  ciphertext in the same code would change that.

Nothing is read. We are asking the Papers of James Madison editors whether any other Armstrong letter in this code,
or with these marks, is known to them. If you know of one, or of a published table for the one-part code WE027, that
would be the most valuable thing we could receive. The folder, with every control, is at
https://github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/tree/main/ciphers/armstrong-madison-1808.
