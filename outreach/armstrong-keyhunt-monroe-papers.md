Dear Editors of the Papers of James Monroe,

I direct a small open project on unsolved historical ciphers and send its letters myself; AI agents (Claude models) do the reading, the searches of the printed editions and the checking, and every step is logged in the public repository linked below. We are working on a letter John Armstrong, U.S. minister in Paris, sent to James Madison on 20 February 1808, in a numeral cipher the State Department could not read; Madison thought it was a cipher "concerted with another correspondent".

The printed Madison Papers record the same accident three years earlier. On 2 April 1805 Armstrong told Madison that in copying his letter of 18 March 1805 to Monroe, then at Madrid, "the cypher established between him and me was employed, instead of that common to you and myself", and Madison wrote to Monroe on 23 May 1805 that those passages "remain locked up" (Papers of James Madison, Secretary of State Series 9). The copy of the 18 March letter that survives in the State Department's despatch volume (NARA M34, roll 13, frame 35) is, as we read it, in the Department's own code with an interlinear decipherment, so the specimen in the Armstrong-Monroe cipher itself appears not to be there. We also looked, at low resolution, through the Library of Congress Monroe Papers (Series 1, reels 3 and 4, late 1804 to 1807) and found no page in numerals from Armstrong; every Armstrong letter to Monroe listed in the 1904 Library of Congress calendar that we located there is in clear.

I would be grateful to know whether the project's document files record where the recipient's copies of Armstrong's letters to Monroe of 12 and 18 March and 1 and 5 April 1805 are held, whether any Armstrong letter to Monroe of 1804-1807 is noted as partly in cipher, and whether a cipher key or table exchanged between the two men is known in any repository. A location is all I am asking for; I will write to the holding institution myself.

A key, or another letter in the same cipher, is the one thing that could make the 1808 letter readable. Everything we have done so far, including what we ruled out, is at https://github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/tree/main/ciphers/armstrong-madison-1808
