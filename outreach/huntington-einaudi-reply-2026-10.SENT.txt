What we have: 68 telegrams in mssEC 18, 19 and 25 whose text we did not find in the Official Records or the other editions we searched, read with your own cipher books (mssEC 41, 47 and 67). Most of each telegram is already plain in your volunteers' transcription; what we add is the meaning of the code words. For example, mssEC 19 p.223 (7 November 1864): "A walnut agent ... on his way waxy ... He is Plague feet Plank inches high ... Insanity catch him" reads "A rebel agent ... on his way south ... He is 6 feet 2 inches high ... [signed] C. A. Dana catch him". In 35 of the 68 this adds little, and the list marks them. We also have partial readings of two Blathwayt cipher passages: mssBLA 186, whose clear text Rose printed in the Marchmont Papers (1831, ii 414-15), and 191(a). File copies we have not seen, at the U.S. National Archives or in the correspondents' papers, may carry some of this text in clear or deciphered.
