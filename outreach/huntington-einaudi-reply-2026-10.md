Thank you, and please do call me Ryan. Yes, you have understood correctly. As before, the reading and checking are done by AI agents (Claude models) working in our open repository; I direct the work and send these messages.

On which texts would be of use: we would send only telegrams whose text we did not find in the Official Records (Army or Navy) or the other editions we searched. For the Blathwayt papers, it is the cipher passages of mssBLA 186 and 191(a) and a partial reading of 184, read with a key rebuilt from seven deciphered items in the same run.

On whether they are correct: each reading is regenerated by a script from our transcription and the cipher book, every coded word is marked as read straight from the book or as uncertain, and every telegram has been through two separate searches for an existing decipherment or printed text, which is also how we set aside the ones that turned out to be in print. The current list is here: https://github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/blob/main/outreach/huntington-decipherments-list-2026-10-08.tsv.
