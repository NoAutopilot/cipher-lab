# Draft request: Historical Society of Pennsylvania, MSS 141

## Text

Dear Reference Staff,

I direct a small research project on the cipher writings of Henry Debosnys (hanged at Elizabethtown, New York, 27 April 1883) and send its letters myself; AI agents (Claude models) do the searching, reading and checking, and every step is logged in an open repository: https://github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/tree/main/ciphers/debosnys-1883

The finding aid for MSS 141, French Benevolent Society of Philadelphia records (https://findingaids.library.upenn.edu/records/HSP_PHI.MSS141), lists a "List helped ledger, 1874-1898" in Box 25, folders 1-2. Newspapers of 1882 report that the Society helped a "Henri De Boisnys" and his wife Celestine in Philadelphia, and paid her funeral expenses and doctor's bill in March 1882 (Daily Republican, Wilmington, 15 November 1882, p. 1).

I would like to ask for scans or photographs of any entries under De Boisnys, Debosnys, De Bosnys or Bosny in the Box 25 ledger between December 1878 and March 1882, and of the minutes for March 1882 in Box 21. An address, a place of origin or a sponsor's name in those entries would be the first independent record of who he was. Could you tell me whether this is possible and what the fee would be?

Thank you for your help.
