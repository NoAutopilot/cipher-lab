WHY.
- TXE3-4: two-reader agreement cannot see the agreed-wrong half, and err_2reader is retired as a predictor. So a check has to look at the tile.
- TXE3-7: a kNN on sibling tiles that overrode a line read fixed 0 and broke 11, and 0/22 in the second test.
- X2b and TXE3-10: training on own ink moved the right way (3/0 and 5/0).
- X4: the readers' own top-3 probabilities were miscalibrated (ECE 0.159; the truth was in the top-3 at 3 of 12 errors). This job calibrates the machine instead.
If one pass plus a free check reaches the two-pass figure, the second reader and the adjudicator can be dropped (TRANSCRIPTION.md target 8). The ceiling must be stated in the PREREG: a classifier trained on pass A's labels learns A's systematic misreads, so it can only catch A's inconsistent reads.

TOOL CHANGE (offline tests for each).
- tools/glyph_atlas.py classify gains --self-labels PASS.tsv --box-map MAP. It trains only on the given pass's own reads at the item's 1:1 boxes, leave-one-line-out: the line being classified never trains.
- It also gains --calibrate FIT.tsv: an isotonic map of P(top-1 in the truth set) against vote share and distance margin, fitted from a dev table. Calibrated p1..p3 are written into the topk TSV.
- New tools/tx_selfcheck.py applies the flip rule, unless tool_shelf names an existing tool to extend.
Tests: LOLO never trains on the classified line; the isotonic map is monotone; the flip rule respects tau and eps.
