## The framework every standing lane uses (ITERATE.md in the target folder)
- One table, appended, never rewritten: date | hypothesis | instrument | matched control (rule 3, design-matched, can
  fail differently from the target) | result (both numbers) | verdict (pass / non-test / fail) | what it taught.
- Before each attempt: read ITERATE.md + HYPOTHESES.md; never re-run an instrument retired under rule 3 (third attempt)
  without new material or a genuinely different instrument; prefer the attempt with the largest information gain per $.
- After each attempt: one line in ITERATE.md "next best attempt and why"; that line is the next wave's job 1.
- Learning: every 4 attempts, a short retrospective section in ITERATE.md (which instrument families moved anything,
  which never did, what the data say the cipher is NOT) -- this narrows the next wave.
- Standing, not one-shot: the lane orchestrator re-arms itself with send_later every 3 h, spends at most $40 per account
  per UTC day on its target, writes one ROOM line per wave, and never stops on its own except (a) a reading passes its
  verifier, or (b) every known instrument is retired AND no new material -- then it writes "idle-standing, waiting on
  <named material>" and re-checks once a day for that material. The owner can stop it in ROOM.md / chat.
- Rule 10, rule 3 and rule 4 hold unchanged; a pass goes to a separate verifier session before it is reported.
