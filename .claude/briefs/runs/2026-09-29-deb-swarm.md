# Debosnys swarm, round 1 (owner's experiment, 29 Sept 2026 02:2x UTC)

Why: the Debosnys track has run as one runner working one idea at a time. The owner asked for an experiment in
the style of a large multi-agent proof search: several isolated groups on opposed hypotheses, a context group, one frozen scorer
every claim must pass, and a merge round that moves what was learned (not keys) between groups. The texts are
short (c1 and c2 settled at 81.6 and 82.8 pct three-reader agreement; control 0.102 at N 385); the swarm does not
add information, it lowers the risk of stopping on one idea. A firm negative is a result too.

## Shared rules (every swarm session)

- Account 3; Fable while Fable answers, else Opus 5.5, never below (BUDGETS.md "Model choice under a warning").
- PUBLIC COPIES ONLY. The museum's restricted material is never available to you (ciphers/debosnys-1883/RESTRICTED.md).
  `python3 tools/restricted_guard.py --outgoing` before every plain git push; tools/room.py push runs it for you.
  An image or PDF you save under ciphers/debosnys-1883/ (even from a published book or paper) needs a line in
  tools/restricted_allowlist.txt (sha1, path, the published source) in the same commit, or the guard fails CI;
  prefer sources/<topic>/ at the repository root for material that is not Debosnys's own.
- Folder: ciphers/debosnys-1883/swarm/. The harness owns swarm/README.md, swarm/score.py and swarm/controls/.
  Group X writes ONLY in swarm/G-X/. Never edit score.py or the controls; a bug there goes to ROOM
  "for the orchestrator: DEB-SWARM bug: ..." and you carry on with Phase 1 work.
- ISOLATION: until the merge round, do not open another group's folder (swarm/G-*/ other than yours) and do not
  read other swarm groups' ROOM lines. Read freely: CLAUDE.md, ciphers/debosnys-1883/NOTES.md, HYPOTHESES.md,
  inventory_settled.tsv, the settled pass files for c1 and c2, the h*.json results, sektu-2017.md, and any
  swarm/DIGEST-*.md.
- swarm/controls/sealed/ is read only by score.py. Never open it; never print its contents.
- Phase 1 (stepping stone): run your method on your matching synthetic control first (made at Debosnys's size
  and sign counts, answer sealed). Your method must reach score.py's control bar before you score real texts.
  If score.py is not frozen yet (no FROZEN line in swarm/README.md), build and test your tooling and do Phase 1
  by hand-planted texts of your own; score real texts only after the FROZEN line.
- Phase 2: the real texts, c1 and c2. The only accepted test is score.py's HELD-OUT mode (fit on one text, score
  on the other, both directions). A claim is a key that passes score.py's bar; it then gets two audits by separate
  sessions before anything is counted. Nothing short of that is called a reading.
- LOG: swarm/G-X/LOG.md, one row per attempt: time, method, parameters, control result, real-text held-out
  percentile, why it failed. Dead ends are the main input to the merge; write them plainly.
- Rule 10 wording (never solved, cracked, novel, first, new). Never name the owner. Never call AskUserQuestion.
- ROOM lines via tools/room.py as "DEB-SWARM-X (worker, for the orchestrator)". Claim line at start; a candidate
  line the moment a key passes the bar (then stop and wait for the audits: do not tune further); a done line at
  the end: "DEB-SWARM-X done: control <pass/fail, recovery pct>; best held-out <pct>/<pct>; <n> attempts; lead:
  <one line>". Commit, pull --rebase, push before ending.

## Sessions

  1. swarm/score.py: input a key TSV (sign, value) and a text id (c1, c2, or a control id). Reports: coverage;

     real score. HELD-OUT mode: `--fit c1 --test c2` and the reverse: the key is fixed on one text and scored on

     any string). Output JSON, never plaintext of a control.
     A THIRD word model, DEB-VOCAB (owner's point, 29 Sept 02:3x UTC: his clear writing likely shares the

## Amendment 29 Sept 03:2x UTC (from DEB-SWARM-A's bug line of 03:04, for DEB-SWARM-0)

A value-shuffled-key null does not account for the fitting step: a key fitted by an annealer on text with no
language already sits in that null's tail (G-A: 20 NULL held-out pairs, median percentile about 91, max 99.8).
score.py adds a SECOND null before FROZEN: refit the same method on the fit text with its token order shuffled
(at least 50 refits), score each held-out the same way; the bar becomes above 99.9 on the shuffled-key null AND
above the 99th percentile of the refit null, in both directions. Groups pass their fitting command to score.py
(`--refit-cmd`) or supply the 50 refits themselves. Report both nulls in every result.
