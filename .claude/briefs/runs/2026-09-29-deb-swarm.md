# Debosnys swarm, round 1 (owner's experiment, 29 Sept 2026 02:2x UTC)

Why: the Debosnys track has run as one runner working one idea at a time. The owner asked for an experiment in
the style of a large multi-agent proof search: several isolated groups on opposed hypotheses, a context group, one frozen scorer
every claim must pass, and a merge round that moves what was learned (not keys) between groups. The texts are
short (c1 and c2 settled at 81.6 and 82.8 pct three-reader agreement; control 0.102 at N 385); the swarm does not
add information, it lowers the risk of stopping on one idea. A firm negative is a result too.

## Shared rules (every swarm session)

- Account 3; Fable while Fable answers, else Opus 5.5, never below (BUDGETS.md "Model choice under a warning").
- PUBLIC COPIES ONLY. The museum's restricted material is never available to you (ciphers/debosnys-1883/RESTRICTED.md).
  `python3 tools/restricted_guard.py --outgoing` before every plain git push; tools/room.py push runs it for you.
- Folder: ciphers/debosnys-1883/swarm/. The harness owns swarm/README.md, swarm/score.py and swarm/controls/.
  Group X writes ONLY in swarm/G-X/. Never edit score.py or the controls; a bug there goes to ROOM
  "for the orchestrator: DEB-SWARM bug: ..." and you carry on with Phase 1 work.
- ISOLATION: until the merge round, do not open another group's folder (swarm/G-*/ other than yours) and do not
  read other swarm groups' ROOM lines. Read freely: CLAUDE.md, ciphers/debosnys-1883/NOTES.md, HYPOTHESES.md,
  inventory_settled.tsv, the settled pass files for c1 and c2, the h*.json results, sektu-2017.md, and any
  swarm/DIGEST-*.md.
- swarm/controls/sealed/ is read only by score.py. Never open it; never print its contents.
- Phase 1 (stepping stone): run your method on your matching synthetic control first (made at Debosnys's size
  and sign counts, answer sealed). Your method must reach score.py's control bar before you score real texts.
  If score.py is not frozen yet (no FROZEN line in swarm/README.md), build and test your tooling and do Phase 1
  by hand-planted texts of your own; score real texts only after the FROZEN line.
- Phase 2: the real texts, c1 and c2. The only accepted test is score.py's HELD-OUT mode (fit on one text, score
  on the other, both directions). A claim is a key that passes score.py's bar; it then gets two audits by separate
  sessions before anything is counted. Nothing short of that is called a reading.
- LOG: swarm/G-X/LOG.md, one row per attempt: time, method, parameters, control result, real-text held-out
  percentile, why it failed. Dead ends are the main input to the merge; write them plainly.
- Rule 10 wording (never solved, cracked, novel, first, new). Never name the owner. Never call AskUserQuestion.
- ROOM lines via tools/room.py as "DEB-SWARM-X (worker, for the orchestrator)". Claim line at start; a candidate
  line the moment a key passes the bar (then stop and wait for the audits: do not tune further); a done line at
  the end: "DEB-SWARM-X done: control <pass/fail, recovery pct>; best held-out <pct>/<pct>; <n> attempts; lead:
  <one line>". Commit, pull --rebase, push before ending.

## Sessions

- DEB-SWARM-0, harness (Fable, cap 40, box 120). Build and freeze, in this order, before the groups need it:
  1. swarm/score.py: input a key TSV (sign, value) and a text id (c1, c2, or a control id). Reports: coverage;
     French and English letter-quadgram log-likelihood per character from a stated public-domain corpus (period
     prose, 1850-1900, cited with its source); dictionary hits for words of 4+ letters; each against 1000
     shuffled-key controls that permute values across signs keeping the value distribution; percentile of the
     real score. HELD-OUT mode: `--fit c1 --test c2` and the reverse: the key is fixed on one text and scored on
     the other (signs unseen in the fit text stay unread). Syllable and word values allowed (a value may be
     any string). Output JSON, never plaintext of a control.
     A THIRD word model, DEB-VOCAB (owner's point, 29 Sept 02:3x UTC: his clear writing likely shares the
     cipher's subjects): words of 3+ letters from Debosnys's PUBLIC clear writing (clear_poems.tsv, the
     signatures and clear notes in NOTES.md, published clear texts cited in sektu-2017.md), plus
     swarm/G-E/CRIBS.tsv when it exists (names, places, dates, topic words). Report DEB-VOCAB hits against the
     same 1000 shuffled keys, separately from French and English. The file may grow; the controls use the same
     list, so the percentile stays fair.
  2. swarm/controls/: four synthetic texts shaped like c1 and c2 (same length, same sign-count curve from
     inventory_settled.tsv, same line lengths): FR-HOMO (French verse, homophonic letters), EN-HOMO (English
     verse, homophonic letters), FR-SYLL (French, signs for syllables and a few words), NULL (no language:
     generated to match the sign frequencies and line and rhyme structure). Plaintexts only under
     swarm/controls/sealed/; score.py `--control <id>` reports recovery pct against the sealed text.
  3. The bar, written in README.md before any group scores: a key passes when held-out percentile is above
     99.9 in BOTH directions in one language, and the same group's method reached 70 pct recovery on its
     matching control. For group D: NULL told apart from the three language controls, blind, with the same
     test it applies to c1/c2.
  4. Self-test: a planted key on a planted text must pass; a random key must not; commit, then add the line
     "FROZEN <git sha of score.py> <UTC time>" to README.md and post ROOM "DEB-SWARM-0 done: frozen <sha>".
  Sanity-check against the earlier runner's controls (h9_controls.json, h13_noise.json, h17_planted.json) and
  say in README.md where they agree or differ.
- DEB-SWARM-A (Fable, cap 60, box 180): French plaintext, homophonic letter substitution. Control FR-HOMO.
- DEB-SWARM-B (Fable, cap 60, box 180): English plaintext, homophonic letter substitution. Control EN-HOMO.
- DEB-SWARM-C (Fable, cap 60, box 180): syllabic or mixed system (signs for syllables, words or names, French or
  English; the rhyme result h8_rhyme_settled.json is a constraint to use). Control FR-SYLL.
- DEB-SWARM-D (Fable, cap 60, box 180): the disproof group. Build the strongest case that c1 and c2 carry no
  readable language (invented script, decoration, or meaningless writing), with tests that would come out the
  other way if a language were present. Control: tell NULL from the three language controls, blind. Earlier
  negatives (shorthand, plain alphabet, word code) are inputs, not results to repeat.
- DEB-SWARM-E (Fable, cap 60, box 180): context and pictures (owner's points, 29 Sept 02:3x UTC). Scores no keys.
  1. swarm/G-E/PAGEMAP.md: every region of every PUBLIC page image (images/manifest.json, the published scans
     cited in NOTES.md): cipher blocks, clear text, drawings and portraits, signatures and monograms (L.M.F., the
     bird-and-key monogram), pencil or ink, what overlaps what, and which cipher line sits next to which picture.
     One row per region with its image and pixel box.
  2. swarm/G-E/LIFE.md: his life from public sources (the 1882 killing of his wife Elizabeth Wells, the trial and
     the 1883 execution at Elizabethtown, his languages, the identity claims researched in sektu-2017.md, period
     newspapers); dates and places, each with its source. What would a man in that cell write about?
  3. swarm/G-E/PICTURES.md: for each drawing and each pictogram class in H31 (PICT-*, SUN, STAR, HEART, RAM): what
     it shows, candidate meanings (French and English words for it, rebus syllables, a person or event from
     LIFE.md), and one TESTABLE prediction each (H31: pictograms open lines far more often than chance, p 0.0002;
     the runner's open idea is word-initial syllables written as pictures; test it). Run the tests you can.
  4. swarm/G-E/CRIBS.tsv (word, language, source, why): names, places, dates, topic words for DEB-VOCAB.
  Group E's files are read by the merge; groups A-D see them only through the digest.
- DEB-SWARM-MERGE-1 (queued by the orchestrator after the four group done lines; separate session): reads all
  four LOG.md files and the ROOM done lines; writes swarm/DIGEST-1.md: per group, what was tried, the best
  numbers, the failure states, and at most three transferable insights (a structural fact about the texts, a
  constraint that kills a class of keys, a tool). No keys are copied between groups. Ends with round-2 prompts:
  one paragraph per group, each allowed to change its hypothesis if the digest says it is dead.
