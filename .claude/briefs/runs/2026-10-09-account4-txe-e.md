# TXE-E: a confusion matrix learnt on dev re-weights the key-constrained lattice (LANE TX-ENGINEER, idea M5; account 4, Opus 5.5; cap 5, box 75 min; no vision call)

Read `.claude/briefs/runs/2026-10-09-account4-txe-COMMON.md` first, then `benchmark-tx/PREREG-txeng-2.md` (units, blindness,
Amendment: gate p < 0.01), then research/TX-IDEAS-2026-10-09.md row M5. Why this job exists: half of pass A's errors on Birago
no.87 are the same wrong sign in both readers (taxonomy class 1: d T18 <- s T98 and back, p T90 / t T53, n T76 / e, h T64 / l);
`tools/key_decode_lattice.py from-passes` spreads a fixed 0.15 of each read sign's weight over its top-3 neighbours in
`harvest/confusion_1572.tsv` (a disagreement count, not an error rate), and TX-DECODE found the lattice at lam 1 raises error
and lam 4 gains ~2 tokens. A matrix of P(read | true) estimated on the dev lines, where the readers' systematic swaps are
known, may put the truth into the lattice where today it is absent (only 27 of 97 errors have the truth in the lattice).

## Build, as options on `tools/key_decode_lattice.py` (never a private script; test tools/tests/test_key_decode_lattice.py extended)
1. `from-passes ... --confusion-matrix M.tsv [--spread S]`: M.tsv has columns true, read, p (P(read | true) per reader pass or
   pooled); each read sign r at a position adds, for every true sign t with p(r|t) > 0, candidate t with weight
   S x weight(reader) x p(r|t) / sum_t p(r|t) (a Bayes flip with a flat prior over the signs that could have produced r), on
   top of the existing rule; S default 0.3; `--matrix-only` replaces the confusion_1572 spread with it.
2. `learn-confusion PASS.tsv ... --truth TRUTH.tsv --lines L... --out M.tsv`: estimates P(read | true) over the named lines
   from the per-position alignment (`tools/tx_bench.py`'s align; import, do not copy), with add-0.5 smoothing over the sheet's
   51 cells, and prints the matrix's top off-diagonal mass. Learning uses truth, so it runs ONLY on dev_tune lines and the
   matrix file names the lines it was learnt from in its header.
