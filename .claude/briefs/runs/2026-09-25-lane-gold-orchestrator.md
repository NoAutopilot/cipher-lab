How this lane works (the Navier-Stokes pattern, cipher form):
- A hypothesis family is one group. Each family has an append-only `ciphers/<t>/HYPOTHESES.md` section: method, parameters, seed, corpus, the CONTROL number and the TARGET number side by side, partial decodes, cribs tried, dead ends. Workers in a family append; they do not rewrite.
- Sibling first. No worker runs a family on the target until the same worker's matched control (same lengths, alphabet, language, design) has been run and read; the control number and the gate are in the job brief. A family whose control does not reach its gate is not run on the target; the tool or the method is fixed first.
- Consolidate, then re-prompt. Every cycle (about four hours, or when a family's swarm has reported) one Opus consolidator (cap $8) rewrites the top summary of HYPOTHESES.md, decides continue / pivot / park per family, and writes the next round's job brief with what the last round learned pasted in. Post "for the parent: GOLD cycle N: <family> control a/b, target c/d, next <what>" in ROOM after each cycle.
- Machine gate only. Any candidate plaintext is reported with `tools/judge_plaintext.py <spec> --file <reading>` output pasted; a PASS gets a fresh Sonnet session that has seen only the spec and the key material and re-derives it with the script (rule 7); only then "for LANE V6: reading ready" in ROOM. This lane never writes solved, new, first or unpublished (rule 10); NOTES.md status words are open / partial / found-solved / blocked only.
- Park a family, not the target: when its control reads at or above its gate and the target's best restart is at control-noise level after the restarts the brief named, log it in the spec's `cheap_test_done` with both numbers and move to the next family.
