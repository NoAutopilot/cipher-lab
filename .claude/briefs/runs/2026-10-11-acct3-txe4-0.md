UNIT 1 -- the join gate (8a). About 35 minutes, USD 1.2.
New tools/join_check.py, a library plus a CLI.
- Statistic: the truth-free shift check of box_token_align.py's offset_check. For every check tile placed on one token, take the first offset in 0, -1, +1, +-2 at which the check label equals an acceptable read sign, or 'none'. Count per line and overall. When the check labels also steered the map, use the leave-one-out positions: pass --steered, or detect it when the --check-labels file is the --topk or --tile-labels source.
- Bound, registered here:
  - B1, per line: every line with at least 5 placed check tiles has more matches at 0 than at -1, and more than at +1. Lines under 5 are listed 'unchecked', not failed.
  - B2, overall: the matches at -1 and +1 together are at most max(0.05, p95) of the placed check tiles. p95 is the 95th percentile of the same share over 20 seeded permutations of the check labels within each page.
  - B3, overall: the share at 0 exceeds the same null's 95th percentile.
  - Verdict: UNCHECKABLE when B3 fails (the labels cannot see a shift); otherwise FAIL when B1 or B2 fails; otherwise PASS.
- Writes MAP.tsv.joincheck.json beside the map, holding the verdict, the counts per line and overall, the null p95s, the map's sha256, the inputs' sha256 and the UTC time. Exit codes: 0 PASS, 2 FAIL, 3 UNCHECKABLE.
- require(map_path) is for consumers. It refuses (exit 2) a map with no sidecar, with a sidecar that is not PASS, or whose sha256 differs from its sidecar. The escape is --allow-unchecked-join 'REASON', which exists only to reproduce old committed results: it prints the reason and stamps 'UNCHECKED JOIN: REASON' into the consumer's output header.
- tools/box_token_align.py gains --check-bound. It requires --check-labels, runs join_check on the map it just wrote, writes the sidecar, and exits 0, 2 or 3. On FAIL or UNCHECKABLE the map is still written so it can be inspected, but it gets no PASS sidecar.
- tools/tests/km_birago_map.py: its PASS also needs the neighbour-label count at or under 0.05 of the scored tiles. Its docstring says a KM pass alone never shows that a map is right (old map 75/381 fails; label-aware 3/373 passes).
Offline tests in tools/tests/test_join_check.py. The docstring of join_check.py names these same cases (CLAUDE.md 8a).
