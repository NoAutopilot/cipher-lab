How to fix a cut

Drag an edge of the box, or drag inside it to move the whole box. Drag a round corner handle to move that corner alone (a slanted or uneven box for a wide looped sign). On a phone, the arrows move one edge or the chosen corner 2 pixels a tap. "Erase stray ink": paint over a neighbour's ink inside the box and it is left out of the tile (shown as paper); "Undo stroke" takes back the last stroke. A sign with no tile of its own: move the box onto it and press "Add as a missed sign" (this tile's cut stays as it was). A box that holds two signs: fit it to one of them and press "Split: keep this part, add the rest"; the rest of the box becomes a sign of its own. Added signs get a pile on the next page build; Undo takes one back.
