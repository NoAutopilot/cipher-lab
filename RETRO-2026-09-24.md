### 4. Transcription: write down the clean-inventory gate and what a pass does when it fails (safe)

Motivated by: row 86 (Raince, 5,725 signs / 50 types, fails "fewer than 40 types" — a gate that exists only as
the owner's ad hoc line in ASKS.md row 22 and a prose mention in STATUS.md) and row 78's own unacted-on lesson
("cluster glyphs from the images before trusting either pass's codes"). Two independent passes that each
invent their own shape codes cannot converge on a page with 22 rare (1-33 occurrence) sign types; nothing in
the template tells the second pass to check the first pass's glyph atlas before coding its own.

```diff
--- a/.claude/briefs/transcription.md
+++ b/.claude/briefs/transcription.md
 Two independent passes by Sonnet subagents from the images, never from an existing transcription; reconcile
 row by row against the image; a third pass only where the two disagree on more than a tenth of rows. Output
 ciphertext.txt in the repo's group format and a reconciliation log. + common tail.
+Clean-inventory gate (set by the owner for Raince, 24 Sept 2026; apply to any symbol-alphabet target): the
+reconciled sign inventory must hold fewer than 40 types with the two passes agreeing within 5% on total token
+count, before a solver session is assigned. On a page with many rare (fewer than 5 occurrence) sign types,
+each pass must first segment glyphs from the images and cluster them by shape into a shared candidate atlas
+(not each pass's own ad hoc code list) before transcribing; reconciliation compares against that atlas, not
+across two independently invented code books. If the gate still fails after this, say so in NOTES.md and stop
+for the orchestrator rather than starting a third full pass on the same method as the first two.
```
