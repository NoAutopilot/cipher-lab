# Citations

One row per public citation of this project's work by someone outside the repository: a public page or a public
post that names or links this work. A private email reply is logged in CONTRIBUTIONS.md, not here, until it
becomes public (rule 10 discipline applies to credits too: a reply is "reported by email, public page not yet
showing it" until a public page carries it). The owner is never named here (rule 9): the repository is credited,
not a person, even where the public page itself names whoever signed the outreach.
