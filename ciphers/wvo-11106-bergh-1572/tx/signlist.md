# Provisional sign list, WVO 11106 p.2 (shapes only, no meanings). Labels are ASCII.
The cipher is written in a 16th-century italic hand. Each sign is one glyph. Use exactly these labels:

Lowercase letter-shapes, label = the letter: a b c d e f g h i k l m n o p q r s t u x y z
  - s   = short round s
  - S   = long s (tall, with descender, like an integral sign)
  - sz  = the ss-ligature (looks like German sharp-s), ONE sign
  - dd  = round-backed d whose ascender loops back to the LEFT (like Greek delta), distinct from ordinary upright d
  - o2  = o with a tail/flourish at top right (like Greek sigma); plain closed o = o
  - G   = g-like sign whose tail is a big hook curving to the left/below (like a long-tailed 3-shape under the line); plain g = g
  - yx  = y (or any sign) whose descender is crossed by a short horizontal stroke (crossed tail); label it yx whatever the top looks like, and add ? if unsure
  - zx  = z/3-like sign with a crossing bar (a crossed z)
Capitals: A B C D E   (E is the epsilon-shaped capital; B, A, D, C as written)
Digits: 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9   (careful: 9 vs g vs q; 3 vs z; 7 vs crossed forms)
X = the large x-cross sign.
If a glyph fits none of these, label it NEW1, NEW2 ... and describe it once in a comment row.
