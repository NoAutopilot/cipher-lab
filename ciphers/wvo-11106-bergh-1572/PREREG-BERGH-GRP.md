# PREREG-BERGH-GRP (LANE FAMILY-A2d, account 2, 9 Oct 2026, written 00:4x UTC by `date -u`, pushed before any strip is read)

Second instrument after BERGH-STRIP (16/19, FAIL at 17; PREREG-BERGH-STRIP.md): the same 19 gate windows, the same atlas boxes and the
same number -> sid key (atlas/strips_grp/key.tsv is byte-identical to atlas/strips/key.tsv, `atlas/strips_grp.py --check-key`), but
(1) readers answer **sign groups** -- "box numbers that together make one sign -> label" -- so a sign split into several boxes is one
answer and a box holding two signs is one answer with two labels; (2) a **de-stacked layout**: no leader lines; each box has a bar in
its own colour spanning exactly its own x-range with its number directly under the bar, bars packed into rows so boxes sharing an
x-range sit in different rows, a number offset sideways when two box centres are within 20 px; a clean copy of the window above the
outlined copy. This is the second attempt at box-level labels with a different instrument; rule 3: if it fails, no third run of it.

## Reader answer format (fixed)

One row per group: `strip<TAB>boxes<TAB>labels<TAB>note`. `boxes` = numbers joined by `+` (e.g. `3+4`); `labels` = the sign(s) the
group holds, left to right, space-separated, from tx/signlist.md (e.g. `y`, or `d 4` for one box holding two signs), or `FRAG` (ink that
is not a whole sign and not part of any sign of this line: speck, stray stroke) or `OTHERLINE` (ink belonging to the line above or
below, whole or partial). Every number of a strip appears in exactly one group. A trailing `?` marks doubt.

## Method (fixed before reading)

- Strips: `python3 atlas/strips_grp.py --check-key` (19 PNGs, 1280 px wide, regenerated byte-for-byte; not committed).
- Two blind Sonnet passes A and B, 2 calls each (strips 1-10 and 11-19; B in reverse order 19-11, 10-1). Readers get tx/signlist.md
  and the strip paths only; never tx/, atlas/boxmap.tsv, atlas/box_sign.tsv, atlas/strips/pass*.tsv or this file.
- Score: `python3 atlas/score_groups.py atlas/strips_grp/passA.tsv atlas/strips_grp/passB.tsv` (written before the reads).
