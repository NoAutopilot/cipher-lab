# Imaging request — Bodleian MS. e Musaeo 203

No digitised image was found this pass (Digital Bodleian search for "e Mus. 203" returns "No items found";
the finding-aid pages at archives.bodleian.ox.ac.uk 503'd repeatedly, so this may be worth a plain browser
check by hand before ordering). If a copy order is wanted:

- **Shelfmark:** Bodleian Library, MS. e Musaeo 203 (John Wallis's 1653 gift, "A Collection of Letters and
  other Papers... intercepted... written in Cipher. Deciphered by John Wallis").
- **What to request:** page images of the four items EMLO marks as not deciphered — Henri Brasset to Antoine
  de Bordeaux-Neufville, 4 Apr 1653 (French); an unknown sender, Scotland, 16 Jan 1651; George Villiers, 2nd
  Duke of Buckingham, undated; Peter Townesend, Flanders, 5 May 1658 (plus its accompanying blank "A Key to
  the Cipher foregoing" page). EMLO's manifestation records give page references within the volume (pp.
  203/212/215/219 ff., per QUEUE.md EM4) but not exact folio/page numbers for each of the four — worth asking
  Bodleian Imaging Services for a full contents list first, or requesting the whole volume if the four are a
  small fraction of it.
- **Contact route:** Bodleian Imaging Services, standard reader/imaging order form (bodleian.ox.ac.uk).
- No personal data (name, address, payment) logged here per CLAUDE.md rule 9 — the person places and pays for
  the order themselves.
