# Copy request: Kenneth Spencer Research Library, University of Kansas, MS P556

Take the exact contact route from spencer.lib.ku.edu ("Using the Library" > "Request Reproductions") rather
than from here, since addresses and forms change.

## The order

1. **MS P556**, "John Dalrymple, 2nd Earl of Stair (1673-1747) Correspondence", Folder 1: "Diplomatic report in
   form of a letter to Charles Viscount Townshend, Ambassador to the Netherlands," 27 Feb 1710, 3p and docket.
   Ask for a high-resolution (TIFF) scan of all 3 pages plus the docket (4 images). Say why: page 3 is
   catalogued "largely in code (undeciphered)" and the request is to attempt a decipherment.
2. Ask whether a lower-cost PDF reference copy ($0.25/image) is adequate for a first pass, with TIFF only for
   the ciphered page 3 if budget matters; the whole item at TIFF rate is about $20 (4 images x $5).

## Cost and turnaround (from spencer.lib.ku.edu/using-the-library/request-reproductions, checked 20 Sept 2026)

- PDF reference copy: $0.25/image
- Low-resolution JPEG: $2.00/image
- High-resolution TIFF: $5.00/image
- About 3 business days; delivered by email or a cloud link that expires after 30 days — download and commit
  the images promptly once received.
- Self-scanning in the Reading Room is free but not publication quality (not useful for glyph-level work).

## Before ordering, worth doing for free

Check J. M. Graham, *Annals and Correspondence of the Viscount and the First and Second Earls of Stair* (1875),
on Internet Archive/HathiTrust/NLS digital.nls.uk, for this 27 Feb 1710 letter or its phrases ("Fitzdome",
"Danzig", "Fleming", "Polish jealousy") — if the letter is printed there in clear (even partially), that
changes what needs to be requested, or may make the request unnecessary.
