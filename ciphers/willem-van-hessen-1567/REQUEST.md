**Target:** the original (not the draft) of Willem van Hessen to Willem van Oranje, 28 January 1567, Kassel.
WVO briefnr 1127 record (https://resources.huygens.knaw.nl/wvo/app/brief?nr=1127) lists this shelfmark as the
original's location, with the note "Een gedeelte van het origineel is in onopgelost cijferschrift" (part of the
original is in unsolved cipher). **No PDF or digital image of the original is listed in the WVO database** --
only its own draft (Hessisches Staatsarchiv Marburg, Bestand 3II, imaged free, already in `images/01127.pdf`)
is online.

**What is needed:** a digital scan or photograph of A 11/XIV B/15-43 (the full item, both the plain and
enciphered passages) from the Koninklijk Huisarchief, Den Haag. This is the only route to the actual cipher
text -- the draft alone cannot be aligned against an unseen original.

**Why:** the draft is explicitly noted by WVO's own curators as giving "de complete tekst" (the complete text),
which makes this a strong recovery-by-crib candidate (LESSONS.md §2, "look for the sibling") -- but only once
the enciphered original is in hand to align against.
