# Copy request — Lambeth Palace Library MS 649, ff. 490-495

Status: waiting on you (no free digitised image found, check-solved 24 Sept 2026).

## What to request

Page images (or a scan from the microfilm) of Lambeth Palace Library, MS 649, ff. 490-495 ("Letters in cypher
to BACON (Anthony), Secretary to the Earl of Essex", part of the Bacon Manuscript, MSS 647-662).

## Why

Catalogued as ciphertext with no noted decipherment, 6 folios, plural "letters." No free image is online:
Lambeth's own LUNA image database returned no hit for a "MS 649" query; the library's CalmView catalogue record
gives no digitisation link; the only reproduction routes found are a Lambeth in-house microfilm (MS Film 793)
and the subscription-only Adam Matthew Digital "Early Modern England" database (not accessible without an
institutional login).

## Routes, cheapest first

1. **Lambeth Palace Library reading room / copying service.** Registration and booking:
   https://www.lambethpalacelibrary.info/using-the-library/registering-and-booking/. Request a scan of MS 649,
   ff. 490-495 from microfilm MS Film 793, or ask whether the item has since been digitised for the public
   catalogue.
2. **British Library Microform Research Collections** also holds a copy of the Bacon Papers micropublication
   (per the fonds's Copies note, https://www.bl.uk/services/document/microrescoll/rescoll.html#128) — may be
   viewable in the BL reading rooms without a Lambeth-specific visit.
3. **Adam Matthew Digital**, if the person or an affiliated institution already has a subscription to
   *Early Modern England: Society, Culture & Everyday Life, 1500-1700* — the item "Papers of Anthony Bacon MS
   649 Volume 3 [2]" (dated 1593 in AMD's public metadata) may or may not be the volume containing ff.490-495;
   would need confirming with AMD's own finding tools.
