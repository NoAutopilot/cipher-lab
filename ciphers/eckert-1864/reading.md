Sixteen of the twenty were matched to an Official Records print by the subagent sweep of 26 volume-parts of
series I on the Internet Archive (or_check in NOTES.md section 4); the four it did not match (E4, E5, E6, E12)
were audited on 20 Sept 2026 (AUDIT.md): E6 is in OR I/32 pt 3 p.498 (the sweep missed it), E12 was printed
in 1864 and in every edition of Lincoln's works, E4 and E5 were not found in the sources searched (N3) and
may not be called unpublished. Every entry reads cleanly against the book; the seventeen OR-printed ones agree with the printed text word for word apart from
the clerk's slips and the times, which the OR rounds or omits (E2 7.40 vs 7.20; E14 10 vs 10.15; E16 12.30 vs
noon; E19 2 PM vs 1.40). The addressees at the Baltimore, Harpers Ferry and Cairo posts are the words the book
does not give (Prss/Praise, Submit, Mackerel/Mutton, Macbeth/Maynard): key.md section 7.

## Notes on particular readings

- The entries begin "Growl"/"Grapes" (= Washington, the place of origin), then the month in clear and the day in
  numeral words, then the time word; the shorter entries drop the place and the month. The operator's header
  gives the same date and often the same hour in clear, which fixes the time words independently.
- The signature words: Brutus/Infant = Secretary of War (Stanton, E1-E2); Ireland/Italy/Jacob/Jordan/Jew/Ivory/
  Inland = General-in-Chief (the book's meaning; in 1864 the signer is Halleck as Chief of Staff, and mssEC 43
  writes "Maj Gen H. W. Halleck" on the same lines); Ingress = President (Lincoln, E11, E12); Bender = Quarter-
  master General (Meigs, E5, E7); Jupiter/John = Grant.
- E4: "Baden Smyrna/Sidney" is read "Roanoke Inland" from the book; the meaning is written "Inland" on p.21 l.17
  but the sense (and Butler's reply) is Roanoke Island. "Camels" and "youths" are plain words that happen to be
  printed code words (Camel = Harpers Ferry, Youth = signature); they are marked plain in ciphertext.txt.
- E9: "for Mackerel tulip Energetic ..." - "tulip" is a book word (Open) but stands where the Grant-headquarters
  cipher of the Beckwith entries uses it as a punctuation word; the operator on the Harpers Ferry line seems to
  have slipped into that other cipher's habit. Left as the book reads it.
- E17: "pickted" (defeated: the OR prints "defeated"; the book's Putnam/Picket = Defeat, and the clerk wrote
  "pickted" for "picketed"), "Nansy" (south) and the doubled tail with a second signature: the whole tail after
  "Ireland" is the operator's service message to Clowry.
- E18: "Mammy duke" = Marmaduke, split by the clerk, "duke" marked plain (Duke = Meridian in the book).
- E11, E12 (Lincoln): the OR's "Yours of to-day" is the ledger's "Yours of Hedge"; Hedge is not in mssEC 41
  (Wedge/Wharf = Today), so it is graded C.

## Reconciliation of the transcription

Two subagents transcribed the ten pages independently (passes A and B, 19 Sept 2026; pass A of the first five
pages had to be rerun after an API refusal), without access to the volunteer text; the orchestrator compared
them word by word (difflib), took the volunteer transcription as third witness, and re-read the page image
(enlarged crops) at every disagreement not settled two to one. Decisions:

- E1 line 1: A "Prss[?]", B "Press", volunteers "Press"; the page reads "Prss" (the clerk's slip). Kept "Prss".
- E2: A "Country" / "men attacked", B "Counties" / "are attached" (volunteers "Counties", "were attached"); the
  image reads "Counties ... are attached". Kept B.
