## Rights

Each item record carries the statement: "For information on use of Digital Library materials, please see Library
Rights and Permissions: https://www.huntington.org/library-rights-permissions". That page (read 19 Sept 2026 via
the Wayback Machine; the live page sits behind a browser check) says: "The Huntington Library does not require that
researchers request permission to quote from or publish material that is in the public domain or for which The
Huntington does not own the copyright, nor does it charge for such activities. The Huntington Library does ask that
researchers identify and cite specific item information when publishing images or quoting from materials held by
the Library." The originals are US government records of 1864 and are in the public domain. Cite as: Thomas T.
Eckert Papers, mssEC 19 / mssEC 41, The Huntington Library, San Marino, California. The page transcriptions in
`../ciphertext.txt` and `../key.md` were made here from these images; the volunteer transcriptions of Decoding the
Civil War (2016-2017), which the Huntington publishes on the ledger item pages, were used only as a third witness
in the reconciliation of the ledger entries and are credited there. The cipher-book pages carry no volunteer
transcription.
