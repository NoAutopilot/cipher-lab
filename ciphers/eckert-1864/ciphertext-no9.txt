### O9-A | Page 3 | 8895 | 30 Jan 1864, Halleck to G. H. Thomas (operator Capt J. P. Willard)
Capt J. P. Willard  Washn Jan. 30. 1864.
Pagan Jany Thirtieth Catharine for
Vernon Thomas Probably Quotients detachment
is made to counter act
Blubbers Zodiacs as I presume
no immediate Wales on Lonesome
is expected period Village K
Garrard late chief of Relay
Bureau has been ordered to
report to you for duty
period What is it about
the evacuate = ion of Java
sig Applause very warm here

### O9-B | Page 3 | 8895 | 1 Feb 1864, Halleck to Lockwood at Baltimore (operator Baldwin)
Baldwin  Washn Feby 1st 1864
For Vermont Lock = wood Please
send imm'y to Harpers Ferry
such youth as you can
spare for a few days
for the protection of the
Somers against raids period Reply
how many can you send
Ab = or = tion Florence raining

### O9-C | Page 4 | 8896 | 1 Feb 1864, Halleck to Kelley at Cumberland and Sullivan at Harpers Ferry (operator Lawrence / Kelly)
Lawrence Lt W. B. Kelly  Washn Feby 1st 1864

wood has been directed to send

reach Harpers Ferry Yankees can be
