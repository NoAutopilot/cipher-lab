# Moray-Wood cipher (1568)

- **Source:** BL Add MS 32091, f.213-214. Letter partly in cipher from James Stewart, Earl of Moray, Regent of Scotland, to John Wood, Scottish ambassador in England, 13 July 1568.
- **Status:** partial (corrected LANE CX 25 Sept 2026, from Open -- see check-solved verdict above).
- **Transcription:** `ciphertext.txt` (Tomokiyo's, from `sources/cryptiana/web/elizabeth_moray.txt`). Tokens separated by `;`. Note: Aymeloglu's repository reports this transcription conflates two glyphs under one id and misreads one f-barred glyph inconsistently (s in one place, f in another); their own re-transcription (`moray-1568/transcription.txt` in aaymeloglu/unsolved-ciphers) is the one to work from for any further attempt, not this file, per their README and this check-solved pass.
- **What's known:** Short ciphertext. Symbol set mixes numbers (4, 10-19), letters (a, c, e, f, g, o, p, r, s, x, z), and subscripted variants (x2, x3, z1-z5, o2, Eb, 4b). Cryptiana says "the cipher seems simple".
- **Background page:** `sources/cryptiana/web/elizabeth.htm`.
- **Ideas:** Likely a nomenclator with a simple substitution alphabet plus a few homophones. Cleartext parts of the letter give context. Compare with other Scottish/English ciphers of 1568 in elizabeth.htm.
- **Solver status (19 Sept 2026):** Substantially read by Aymeloglu (unsolved-ciphers/moray-1568) from the DECODE page image R8345, 18 Sept 2026: 119 of 134 glyphs S, key-shuffle z = 17. Reads the Queen at Carlisle and Lord Fleming sent home; seven signs at the end unresolved. Tomokiyo's transcription (this folder) conflates two glyphs. Do not re-solve; work from Aymeloglu's transcription if at all.
