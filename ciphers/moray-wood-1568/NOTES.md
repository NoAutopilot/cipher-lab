# Moray-Wood cipher (1568)

- **Source:** BL Add MS 32091, f.213. Letter partly in cipher from James Stewart, Earl of Moray, Regent of Scotland, to John Wood, Scottish ambassador in England, 13 July 1568.
- **Status:** Open.
- **Transcription:** `ciphertext.txt` (Tomokiyo's, from `sources/cryptiana/web/elizabeth_moray.txt`). Tokens separated by `;`.
- **What's known:** Short ciphertext. Symbol set mixes numbers (4, 10-19), letters (a, c, e, f, g, o, p, r, s, x, z), and subscripted variants (x2, x3, z1-z5, o2, Eb, 4b). Cryptiana says "the cipher seems simple".
- **Background page:** `sources/cryptiana/web/elizabeth.htm`.
- **Ideas:** Likely a nomenclator with a simple substitution alphabet plus a few homophones. Cleartext parts of the letter give context. Compare with other Scottish/English ciphers of 1568 in elizabeth.htm.
