# Access request — RAH Colección Salazar y Castro, five items, 1524-1528

Target: `ciphers/rah-salazar-soria-sanchez-1524-28/`. See NOTES.md for the full search log.

## What is needed

Page images (or a reading-room visit) for five letters catalogued by the Real Academia de la Historia's own
printed *Índice de la Colección Salazar y Castro* as "en cifra, sin descifrar":

| Shelfmark | Inv. no. | Correspondent | Date | Place |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-33, ff.72-74 | 4670 | Alonso Sánchez → Charles V | 17 Dec 1524 | Venice |
| A-35, ff.310-312 (+ 1 duplicate) | 5180 | Lope de Soria → Charles V | 21 Sept 1525 | Genoa |
| A-37, ff.428-429 | 5540 | Lope de Soria → Charles V | 16 Jun 1526 | Genoa |
| A-42, ff.243-244 | 6417 | Lope de Soria → Charles V | 14 Apr 1528 | Mirandola |
| A-42, f.443 | 6502 | Alonso Sánchez → Charles V | 17 Jun 1528 | Venice |

Plus, if available, A-42 ff.441-442 ("Texto descifrado de los párrafos en cifra de los tres documentos
anteriores" — a period decipherment of three of the above, in the same legajo).

## Why this can't be done without a person

RAH's public online digital library (`bibliotecadigital.rah.es`) has no searchable record for any of these
five items under either correspondent's name, under "cifrada"/"cifrado"/"descifrar" (the exact terms that do
surface the library's other ciphered items, confirmed working this session), or under the shelfmarks tried.
This is not the site's usual Anubis bot-challenge (every search this session returned a normal result page);
it looks like these items simply have not been individually digitized into the online portal yet, unlike
the Morillo papers, which were. No `imagen_id.do` record id exists for this worker to fetch.

## Suggested action

An email to the RAH library (biblioteca@rah.es, per the site's own contact page) or the Sala de
Investigadores, citing the printed Índice's own inventory numbers above (not DECODE ids, since DECODE does
not catalogue these either) and asking whether digital images exist or a reading-room visit is needed.
Alternatively, someone with JSTOR/library access could try to open Eduardo Ibarra y Rodríguez y G. Arsenio de
Izaga's *Catálogo de los documentos del archivo de Lope de Soria, embajador del emperador Carlos V* (Boletín
de la Real Academia de la Historia, tomo 98, 1931) first — a web snippet says it describes some of Soria's
Charles-V correspondence as carrying cipher passages with interlinear translations already in print, which
could settle several of these items without any archive visit at all (see NOTES.md, "(c) Tomokiyo" section).

person's own, only the archive's shelfmarks.
