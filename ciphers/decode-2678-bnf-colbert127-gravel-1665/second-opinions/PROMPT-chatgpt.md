THE ITEM
A letter signed "R. de Gravel" to Jean-Baptiste Colbert, dated "a Ratisbone ce 29 Janvier 1665", Bibliothèque
nationale de France, Mélanges de Colbert 127, f.349r-v (Gallica https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b10035540v,
canvases 356-357; DECODE record 2678). It lists payments in Reichsthaler to French clients in the Empire, mostly in
clear ("pour 29 auquel on doit donner 15000 ... qui en doit avoir 500. Pour Mr Frichmann auquel il en est deu 600 ...
qui doivent aussy recevoir 1000 Reichsdalles pour la demye année de leur pension"), with three short passages in a
two-digit figure cipher with over- and underlines. Under Satoshi Tomokiyo's published key of the 1672 Colbert-Gravel
cipher (cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/louisxiv0.htm, Mélanges de Colbert 159 f.102) two passages read, in part,
"so n f re [?] e" (probably "son frère") and "le s c ha no i ne [?]" ("les chanoines"); the 15,000-Reichsthaler payee
is a single code group, 29, not read.
