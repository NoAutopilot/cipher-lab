## Answer and confidence

The strongest directly examined additional study, Georg Schmidt (1989), discusses the October 1573 political setting but does not supply the missing words on the relevant pages. ## Page-citable evidence

1. **The target and an important correction to the prompt's attribution.** G. Groen van Prinsterer, ed., *Archives ou correspondance inédite de la maison d'Orange-Nassau*, première série, tome IV (Leiden, **1837**), letter **CDXLIV**, pp. **217–226**, especially **223–225**, [page-labelled edition](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/groe009arch04_01/groe009arch04_01_0063.php). The text leaves an omission after “Bey dem Herzog von Sachsen und” on p. 223 and an omission before “helt sich wol und thut in warheit viel” on p. 225. Neither place is supplied with a name in this edition. The heading names **Jean, Louis and Henri** as senders, not only Jan and Lodewijk. The page also refers to “unsere Graveneinigung” on p. 224. These facts establish a printed surrounding text with gaps, not a page-cited decoding of those gaps.

2. **Catalogue control.** Huygens ING, *Briefwisseling van Willem van Oranje*, record **5797**, 22 October 1573, [item record](https://resources.huygens.knaw.nl/wvo/app/brief?nr=5797), “Brongegevens” and “Opmerkingen” (unpaginated catalogue entry; **not** a page citation). The record lists Lodewijk, Jan **and Hendrik** as senders, KHA Den Haag **A 3, 895/I**, a *minuut*, and Groen IV, pp. 217–226, letter CDXLIV as its edition. It notes cipher passages and points to reply no. 5804. It does not list a decipherment. The catalogue's silence cannot exclude a separate gloss or another archival copy. [Manuscript image linked by the catalogue](https://resources.huygens.knaw.nl/media/wvo/images/05000-05999/05797.pdf) is a manuscript lead; I have not identified an independent gloss from it in this run.

3. **The specifically requested Schmidt study, now accessible as page images.** Georg Schmidt, *Der Wetterauer Grafenverein: Organisation und Politik einer Reichskorporation zwischen Reformation und Westfälischem Frieden*, Veröffentlichungen der Historischen Kommission für Hessen **52** (Marburg: Elwert, **1989**), **pp. 273–287**, especially **285–286**; [university digitization and bibliographic record](https://archiv.ub.uni-marburg.de/eb/2021/0160), [p. 285 image](https://archiv.ub.uni-marburg.de/eb/2021/0160/jpg/default/00294.jpg), [p. 286 image](https://archiv.ub.uni-marburg.de/eb/2021/0160/jpg/default/00295.jpg). The contents page **VII** identifies pp. 273–287 as the section on Nassau involvement in the Netherlands, 1566–1575. I examined those pages. On p. 285 Schmidt describes Louis's diplomacy, French funding, and the Palatine court; on p. 286 he says Louis addressed the Hessian chamberlain Simon Bing for a proposal to Landgrave Wilhelm, that the Palatine elector showed interest, and that Wilhelm would have nothing to do with the French alliance or imperial-election plan. Schmidt then gives **23,000 gulden from the Palatinate** for the subsequent campaign (p. 286). His notes point to **Groen IV, pp. 97–107** (n. 84) and **Groen IV, appendix pp. 115–116** (n. 86), rather than CDXLIV, pp. 217–226. Neither quoted blank nor a decoding of it appears on pp. 273–287. This is contextual evidence only. It cautions against turning the p. 223–225 name readings into a claim that both rulers backed the same plan. A full-book negative claim would require checking other pages and the index; **unverified**.

4. **Related correspondence, not a substitute text.** The letter printed by Groen as IV, CDXLIV is already the primary page citation in item 1. Schmidt's pp. 285–286 cite other Groen pages for related negotiations and name archival material from 1573 in notes, including **HHStA Wiesbaden 170.III, Jg. 1573** for Louis to Johann, 8 July (p. 285 n. 82). Those are separate documents. I found no Schmidt reference assigning a Wiesbaden folio to a duplicate or gloss of **22 October 1573**. Treat a search of HHStA Abt. 170/171, KHA's related files, and any recipient's copy as an archival lead, **unverified** for the target.
