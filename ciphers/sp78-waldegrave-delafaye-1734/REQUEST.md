# Copy request — TNA SP 78/205/95

**Status:** waiting on you (a person needs to place/confirm this; not an archive-request draft an agent can
send).

**What to request:** a page copy (photograph or scan) of **The National Archives, Kew, SP 78/205**, folio
146: "Waldegrave to Delafaye. He managed to see a paper carelessly left on a table by Fleury which was a copy
of a letter in cypher from Newcastle to Comte de Marsay revealing success of French espionage and knowledge
of the cypher," 1734 July 21, Paris.

**Why, and what to check first:** the check-solved sweep of 24 September 2026 (NOTES.md) flags this item as
likely a *report about* a cipher letter (the French having copied an English Secretary of State's cipher to
Newcastle), not ciphertext inside Waldegrave's own hand — the fuller catalogue description reads as a
plain-English account, and nothing found this pass confirms cipher symbols actually appear on f.146. **Before
ordering a full copy, a cheaper first step is worth trying: ask the Discovery record page or a reading-room
enquiry whether f.146 itself carries any untranscribed ciphertext**, the same check flagged for the similar
N65 (Andrew Stone) item. If it does not, this target should come off the board rather than be copied.

**Order via:** The National Archives' own paid copying service (TNA Discovery record page, "order a copy"),
or an enquiry first per the note above.
