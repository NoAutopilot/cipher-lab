# Sign labels for the Morvilliers 1549 cipher (fr. 3138 fo. 66r), D1-SEURE 6 Oct 2026
Use exactly these labels, separated by single spaces. If a sign fits none, write `?` followed by a short
description with no spaces (e.g. `?loop-with-tail`). If a sign is unreadable, write `?`.
Symbols:
  #      hash: two short vertical strokes crossed by two horizontal strokes
  III    three (or four) short vertical strokes joined/crossed by one horizontal stroke (like a barred "m" or "ш")
  =      two parallel horizontal strokes (often with small hooks at the ends), no vertical strokes
  ff     two TALL vertical strokes joined by a crossbar (like "Ħ" or an "ff" ligature)
  +      simple cross
  ++     double-barred cross (vertical with two horizontal bars, like "‡" or "ǂ")
  phi    circle or oval with a vertical stroke through it (Φ)
  dx     a "d"-shaped sign whose ascender is crossed by a stroke (đ, ϑ-like)
  d      plain "d" / delta shape with a curved ascender, no cross stroke
  oo     lying 8 / infinity sign (∞)
  Co     an open C-shaped curve with a small o inside or attached (like a "6" lying on its side, "⊂o")
  6      a "6" or "σ" with a small mark above it
  o      small plain circle
  f      long s / f (tall stroke with hook, ſ)
  A      open triangle or lambda / inverted V (Λ, λ)
  v      V or downward triangle (ᐯ, ∇)
  M      large looped M / W (ꟿ, three tall arches)
  X      cross like an X (✗, ✕), also the X with a bar
  H      H
  rt     two small ligatured strokes like "ri"/"rt"/"rı"
  7  4  8  9  2  3   digits
  q  y  z  x  n  m  h  g  p  e  c  l  b  t  u   lower-case letter shapes (use the closest)
Whole words in ordinary French handwriting inside the cipher (e.g. "dict", "par", "Neantmoins", "faict"): write
them in braces, e.g. `{dict}`, as one token.
