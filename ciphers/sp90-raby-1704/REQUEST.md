# Copy request — British Library Add MS 61137, ff.41 and 55

**Status:** waiting on you. Open, stage 2 verified unsolved — see NOTES.md's Check-solved section, 25 Sept 2026
(LANE CX2).

**What to request:** page images of British Library, Blenheim Papers, **Add MS 61137** ("BLENHEIM PAPERS. Vol.
XXXVII. Correspondence with Thomas Wentworth, Baron Raby... chiefly as Ambassador at Berlin; 1703-1705"),
specifically:
- **f.41** — "Sir Charles Hedges, Secretary of State: Letters, etc., to: 1702-1708: Partly Fr. and cipher,"
  immediately followed by "Thomas Wentworth... Letters to Sir C. Hedges: 1704" — Raby's own retained copies of
  his 1704 letters to Hedges, the same correspondent and year as the target (TNA SP 90/2/335, 337, 348).
- **f.55** — "Baron Johann Nicolaus von Reichardt, Bavarian Secretary of State: Letter of: 1704: Fr.: Copy" —
  a likely duplicate of SP 90/2/409/409v (Reichart to Berlepsch, French, copy, partly in cipher).

This is the smallest useful set: a duplicate-holding lead in Marlborough's own archive (Blenheim Papers), not
yet opened, that may carry a decipher the TNA original's own catalogue note does not mention.

**Why:** TNA's own catalogue confirms all five SP 90/2 target items (335, 337, 348, 409, 409v) are
`digitised: false`; Cartwright's *Wentworth Papers* (1883), the standard printed edition of Raby's Berlin
correspondence, does not cover 1704 (starts 1705) — checked by this worker's own full-text grep. This BL copy
is cheaper to check than a TNA copy order if it is legible and carries a decipher.

**Order via (checked 25 Sept 2026):** British Library Imaging Services online order form
(`forms.bl.uk/imaging`) or email `itemrequests@bl.uk`, quoting Add MS 61137, ff.41 and 55. Note found this
date: the Library states it "is unable to provide scanned copies from original manuscripts" unless a microfilm
surrogate exists — ask about a surrogate before assuming a reading-room visit (Reader Pass) is the only route.
