## General caveat on confidence

This appendix's cipher lines are dense, small, and written in a cursive secretary hand with numerals
that are frequently hard to distinguish from each other at the resolution available (no independent
zoom/crop tool was used, only the page images as supplied). The digit pairs most prone to confusion in
this hand, encountered repeatedly while transcribing:
- 4 vs 7
- 2 vs z (the code-letter "z" and the digit "2" have similar loops in places)
- 5 vs 8 vs S
- 6 vs G
- 0/O in multi-digit numbers
- single code-letters with heavy flourish (e.g. "y", "z", "g") vs. plain-word letters

Every digit group and every single lowercase-letter code symbol was transcribed as read; only tokens
where the shape was genuinely ambiguous between two plausible readings were flagged with "?" in the
token file. Given the density of this material, treat this whole pass as a first independent read to
be reconciled against the other pass, not as a final key-grade transcription.
