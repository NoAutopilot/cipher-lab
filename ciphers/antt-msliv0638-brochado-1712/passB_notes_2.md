## Dateline at the end of Carta 123 (m0296)

The Deciffrada-only text of Carta 123 ends with the closing formula and dateline, read as:

> "...A grande impreza que agora medita hé fazer-se Reino Republico depois da morte da Rainha.
> D.s Gd.e a V. Ex.ª. 7 de 9bro de 1713."

i.e. the dateline itself is **"7 de 9bro de 1713"** (7 November 1713), marking the end of the appendix
span covered by these leaves, as stated in the task brief. The abbreviation before the date ("D.s Gd.e
a V. Ex.ª", "Deos Guarde a Vossa Excelência") is a standard period letter-closing formula and is my
best reading of a compressed, flourished abbreviation — flagged as a moderate-confidence read overall,
though the date itself ("7 de 9bro de 1713") is clear and legible.

## General note on digit-group readings

Given the resolution of the images and the density of some entries (Carta 92 and the m0290 Passage 2a
run to over 100 coded tokens each), exact digit-by-digit accuracy for every token cannot be guaranteed
from a single non-magnified read. Tokens I was reasonably confident of are given without "?"; anywhere
I was genuinely unsure I appended "?" to the token. This pass should be reconciled token-by-token
against the other independent pass, especially for Carta 92, Passage 2a (m0290), and Passage 3a
(m0291), which are the longest and most visually dense runs.
