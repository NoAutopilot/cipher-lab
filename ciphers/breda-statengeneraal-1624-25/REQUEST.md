# Request: breda-statengeneraal-1624-25 (row HU11, copy of NA 1.01.02 bundle 4945)

No personal data below; log the person's own archive request separately, by date and archive only, per
CLAUDE.md rule 9.

**What's needed:** a photograph or digital scan of Nationaal Archief 1.01.02 (Staten-Generaal), bundle 4945
(catalogued "1624 juli - december"), specifically piece "I" as cited by the printed edition (Roelevink/Japikse/
Smit, *Resolutiën der Staten-Generaal, nieuwe reeks*, Deel 7, resolution 573, note d) -- the magistrate of
Breda's letter to the States-General of 18 September 1624, described as "vrijwel geheel in cijferschrift,
inclusief de dag in de datum" (almost entirely in cipher, including the day in the date). The bundle also holds
two related items cited by the same resolution and worth requesting together: Dras's letter of 19 Sept (note a)
and Prince Maurits' letter of 18 Sept about the Sint Andries bridge (note b, partly printed already in Van der
Kemp's *Maurits van Nassau* IV p.391).

**Why a request is needed:** confirmed this pass (24 Sept 2026) via the archive's own per-item JSON that invnr
4945 is `"availability":"PHYSICAL","scans":[]` -- not digitised. Same accessor and positive control as the
Heinsius-series requests (see `ciphers/heinsius-dopff-1702/REQUEST.md`), applied here to the different NA
collection 1.01.02.

**Where to send it:** Nationaal Archief, The Hague, via their standard reproduction-request route.

**Status:** waiting on you, but lower urgency after 25 Sept 2026. NOTES.md's print check (25 Sept 2026) closed
the Van der Kemp IV p.391 lead negative (it prints only Maurits's bridge letter, nothing of Breda's), but found
a separate 1868 print -- G.G. van der Hoeven, *Geschiedenis der vesting Breda*, Bijlage XIX (p. xxxii) -- whose
sender, recipient, date and content all match this letter, giving what is on every identifying detail checked
the letter's plaintext. NOTES.md marks the row **found-solved**, pending a verifier's read of Van der Hoeven's
own source note for the bijlage. The archive copy is still worth having (to confirm the cipher-to-plaintext
mapping and cover Dras's and Maurits's letters in the same bundle), but this request no longer gates a "no
print found" outcome and should not be treated as urgent.
