## Boyd calendar: Tempest and Barret letters

**Answered.** Boyd calendars both letters with cipher passages left as editorial placeholders. A decipherment of either letter's cipher passages was **not found** in these entries.

Source read directly in the Codex in-app browser on the owner's computer: William K. Boyd (ed.), *Calendar of the State Papers relating to Scotland and Mary, Queen of Scots*, volume VIII, A.D. 1585–1586 (Edinburgh, 1914), HathiTrust `miun.abe1726.0008.001`. The title-page image at [scan 8](https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=miun.abe1726.0008.001&seq=8) gives Boyd, volume VIII, and 1914. **Calendar entry 227**, "[ ] TO MR. TEMPEST", December 1585, starts on printed p.176 and ends on p.177. The match to the queue's letter is supported by the addressee, the English priest's Paris address, and the Thomas Philippes endorsement. The calendar does not print the modern item number SP53/16/78 beside this entry; that modern shelfmark comes from the queue and repository notes.

> Although I have received no letters from you since those to which I replied by the same way that you directed me by them, and which I hope you have received long since, so it is that having found this present commodity I would not fail to write you these lines to always give you advertisement as the affairs we have in hand require.

[Printed p.177 / scan 212](https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=miun.abe1726.0008.001&seq=212), continuation and closing excerpts:

> I have also communicated the above to the man named in your previous letters, who has the matter in sufficiently good recommendation.

> but above all take care seriously that [in cipher to the end].

These are excerpts; the intervening sentence between the continuation and closing excerpt is omitted. The manuscript extent is also omitted here rather than copying the OCR's faulty rendering of the printed fraction.

**Finding:** the calendar prints an English rendering of the clear portions, but replaces the cipher sections with "[Here follows cipher]" and "[in cipher to the end]". It does not supply their deciphered content. The literal expression "not deciphered" was not found in the entry; the cipher placeholders are the evidence.
