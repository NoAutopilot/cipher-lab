## Description

1579-1598). Catalogue entry (verbatim, CalmView record `MSS-647-662/647/218`, confirmed 24 Sept 2026):
Title "CASENOWE (A. Dufauk de)."; Date "[1586]"; Description "Letter to HENRI IV, King of France."; Language
