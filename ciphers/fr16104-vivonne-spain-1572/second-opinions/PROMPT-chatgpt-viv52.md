THE ITEM
Jean de Vivonne, seigneur de Saint-Gouard, French ambassador in Spain, to Catherine de Médicis, Madrid, 5 September 1572.
Bibliothèque nationale de France, ms. français 16104, ff.164r-168v (heading "du S^r de S^t gouard a la Reyne"; plain opening
"Madame."; docket "5 Septembre 1572"; address leaf f.169v). Gallica https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9009609w/f178.item .
Almost entirely letter cipher, with a plain close mentioning the Infante. Not to be confused with his letters of the same day
to Charles IX (ff.157r-159v, Gachard's entry L, deciphered on ff.162r-163r) and to the duc d'Anjou (ff.170r-171v). No
decipherment of the letter to the Queen was found on the neighbouring leaves; Gachard's 1875 analysis of the volume (La
Bibliothèque nationale à Paris, II) has no entry for it.
Key: Satoshi Tomokiyo's published 1572-74 Vivonne key (https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/henryiii.htm). Only the first two
cipher pages (f.164r-v) have been transcribed; under the key we read fragments only: "avoir traicte avecq ses", "intelligence
de ce", "relation de ce", "travailloit a continuer", "apporter ... alteration", "du dangier", "charge de France", "religion".
