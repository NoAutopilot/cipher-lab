THE ITEM
Jean de Vivonne, seigneur de Saint-Gouard, French ambassador in Spain, to Charles IX, Madrid, 10 October 1573.
Bibliothèque nationale de France, ms. français 16105, ff.187r-194r (heading "du S^r de S^t Gourd au Roy"; docket
"10 Octobre 1573 Madrid"). Gallica https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9009663p/f192.item (cipher from f195).
Three plain pages (the audiences of 22 September and St Michael's day, the passport Charles IX asked of Philip II for
his brother the King of Poland, the secretary Çayas), then about 300 lines of letter cipher (ff.190r-194r), then a
plain close. Gachard, La Bibliothèque nationale à Paris II (1875-77), entry LXIII, pp.435-436, summarises only the
plain part and marks the letter "En partie chiffrée, sans le déchiffrement"; no decipherment was found in the volume.
G. de Brémond d'Ars, Le père de Madame de Rambouillet, Jean de Vivonne (1884), cites the letter for the passport
negotiation. Key: Satoshi Tomokiyo's published 1572-74 Vivonne key (https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/henryiii.htm).
Under it we read only fragments of the cipher part: "de Poulougne", "toute extraordinaire et non usitée", "le prince
d'Orange", "commandeur" (five times), "Flandres", "trois marchands", "nombre de chevaulx ... artillerie", "les troubles
ou par doulceur d'un pardon général et bien ample", "vigilant et curieux", "toute diligence", "bientost en Flandres".
