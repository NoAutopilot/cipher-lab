THE ITEM
Jean de Vivonne, seigneur de Saint-Gouard, French ambassador in Spain, to Charles IX, Madrid, 7 September 1572.
Bibliothèque nationale de France, ms. français 16104, f.173r-v (address leaf f.174v endorsed "7 septembre 1572").
Gallica https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9009609w/f187.item . Three plain lines ("Sire, j'ay receu la depesche
de vostre majesté par le courrier ...") then about 45 lines of letter cipher, then a plain closing. No separate
decipherment of it was found in the volume; a few words are written above the cipher (e.g. "au faict", "en ainsi",
"partisan contre"). Gachard's 1875 analysis of the volume (La Bibliothèque nationale à Paris, II) has no entry for it.
Key: Satoshi Tomokiyo's published 1572-74 Vivonne key (https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/henryiii.htm). Under it we
read only fragments: "toute diligence de faire", "munition", "liberation", "tousiours ont doubte que c'est", "contre".
