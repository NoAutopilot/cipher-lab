THE ITEM
Jean de Vivonne, seigneur de Saint-Gouard, French ambassador in Spain, to the duc d'Anjou (the future Henri III),
Madrid, 5 September 1572. Bibliothèque nationale de France, ms. français 16104, ff.170r-171v (heading "du S^r de S^t
gouard au Duc d'Anjou"; address leaf f.172r "Monseigneur", docketed 5 September 1572).
Gallica https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9009609w/f184.item . Seven plain lines ("Monseigneur, Comme je n'ay
intention que a bien et fidellement servir ... ce que j'ay traicté avecques le Roy catholique en l'audiance") then about
100 lines of letter cipher, then a plain closing. Not to be confused with his letter to Charles IX of the same day
(ff.157r-159v, Gachard's entry L), which has a clerk's decipherment on ff.162r-163r. No separate decipherment of the
letter to Anjou was found in the volume; a few words are written above the cipher ("myssent", "en quelque", "l'Infanct",
"longuement"). Gachard's 1875 analysis of the volume (La Bibliothèque nationale à Paris, II) has no entry for it.
Key: Satoshi Tomokiyo's published 1572-74 Vivonne key (https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/henryiii.htm). Under it we
read only fragments: "inutile de negotier", "intelligence", "ministres", "frontiere", "bon chrestien", "bonne intention",
"de contradiction il ne pourroit doubter", "grand bien qui en adviendroit", "capitaine qu'il entend", "vostre altesse".
