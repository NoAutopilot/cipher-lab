Vivonne 1573: split two signs

36 signs from the Vivonne cipher in two trays. Our readers gave every sign in tray A one name and every sign in tray B another, but each tray may hold two different shapes the clerk kept apart. Within each tray, make a new pile for each shape you see and move the signs into them; leave a sign where it is if you cannot tell. Do not mix the trays. About ten minutes.

What to do: a key

Tap a sign = put it aside in the tray. Hold a sign = open its card (large, on its line). Most fixes start with the card’s Fix the cut button, which puts handles on the box; Save cut, Add as a missed sign and Split are right under it. Never read what a sign says: only where its box is.

Step 2 (the boxes the machine was unsure of, one at a time): judge the sign from its line; a box too wrong to tell the sign gets Bad cut, nothing more. It was right checks it as it is.

One sign, the box fits it (a little paper round it is fine)Leave it. Nothing to do.
Two signs in one boxHold it. In the card press Fix the cut (the box gets handles). Pull its side in (drag it, or tap the Right edge arrows) until it holds only the first sign, then press Split: keep this part, add the rest. The rest becomes its own box.
One sign cut into two boxesHold the first piece. In the card press Fix the cut, stretch the box over the whole sign, press Save cut. Then hold the other piece and press Trash (not a sign): it is part of the first box now.

A sign with no boxHold the box next to it. In the card press Fix the cut, drag the box onto the sign without one and press Add as a missed sign. The first box stays as it was.

How to fix a cut

Drag an edge of the box, or drag inside it to move the whole box. Drag a round corner handle to move that corner alone (a slanted or uneven box for a wide looped sign). On a phone, the arrows move one edge or the chosen corner 2 pixels a tap. "Erase stray ink": paint over a neighbour's ink inside the box and it is left out of the tile (shown as paper); "Undo stroke" takes back the last stroke. A sign with no tile of its own: move the box onto it and press "Add as a missed sign" (this tile's cut stays as it was). A box that holds two signs: fit it to one of them and press "Split: keep this part, add the rest"; the rest of the box becomes a sign of its own. To see ink beyond the picture, drag anywhere off the box: the page moves (with "Erase stray ink" on, slide with two fingers). Added signs get a pile on the next page build; Undo takes one back.
