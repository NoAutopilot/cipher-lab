How to fix a cut

Drag an edge of the box, or drag inside it to move the whole box. Drag a round corner handle to move that corner alone (a slanted or uneven box for a wide looped sign). On a phone, the arrows move one edge or the chosen corner 2 pixels a tap. "Erase stray ink": paint over a neighbour's ink inside the box and it is left out of the tile (shown as paper); "Undo stroke" takes back the last stroke. One tile is one box (no "split here"): if the box holds two signs, fit it to the first sign and mark the second with "Bad cut" as now.
