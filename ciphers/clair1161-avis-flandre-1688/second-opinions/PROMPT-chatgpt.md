THE ITEM
Six cipher leaves headed "Advis de flandres" (c186R) and "Autres advis" (c187L), one leaf with the clear date "Juil
23" (no year), in Bibliothèque nationale de France, Clairambault 1161 (Saint-Esprit minutes series, volume slot
"Année 1688"), mounted before a clear letter signed "Noailles e. d'Acqs", Paris, [20?] December 1570.
Gallica https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b90010063 (canvases f186-f189). The BnF finding aid
(archivesetmanuscrits.bnf.fr ark:/12148/cc137837/cd0e35310) and Ph. Lauer, Catalogue des manuscrits de la collection
Clairambault, t. II (1924), p. 332, describe them only as "Avis de Flandre, chiffrés ; lettre orig. de François II de
Noailles, évêque de Dax, au marquis de Villars, 20 déc. 1570". About 3,389 signs of a pen-sign homophonic-type
cipher; a contemporary marginal note beside one block is a period decipherment of it ("... escript et mander a la
royne ... quant au fet de la religion en ce royaulme ... de Espaigne ... ce peuple et gens ... en grand repoz").
Under a key we recovered by cryptanalysis we read only fragments ("les aultres prisonniers", "entreprinse",
"reportees a la cour", "ce qui sera execute", "religion en ce royaulme", "en plus grand repos", "leur conseil").
