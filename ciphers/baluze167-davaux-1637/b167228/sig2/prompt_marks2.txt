numeral, detached from it), a DIAERESIS (two dots above), a BAR (a horizontal stroke above), or NO mark. Note: in this hand the digit 1 is
often written like a dotted i, and the dot can be a short slanting flick sitting directly on top of the 1; that flick belongs to the digit
