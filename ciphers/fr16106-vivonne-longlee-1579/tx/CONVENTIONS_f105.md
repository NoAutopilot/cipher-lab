# Reading conventions for the clerk's copy f.105r-106v (VIV-105, 13 Oct 2026)

No d'Ars or Mousset conventions sheet existed in this folder when VIV-105 started (grep for spelling/convention/orthograph
found none; Mousset's text was not kept on disk, VIV-WALK). This sheet states the conventions given to every reader, drawn
from VIV-T's header to tx/plain_c110_f105r.txt (markers only, not its text) and the usual practice of late-16th-century
French secretary hands as printed by Mousset 1912 and d'Ars 1884 (transcribe as written, abbreviations kept).

- Transcribe diplomatically: keep the clerk's spelling (vostre, faict, nostre, estre, sieur, ledict, icelle, mesmes, aussy).
  Do not modernize, do not add accents beyond what is written, no apostrophes unless written.
- u/v and i/j: write what the letter shape is (u for medial v is normal: "auoir", "nouuelles"; initial v for u: "vn").
- Long s and round s are both "s". Final -z/-s and the -es/-ez loop: write as it looks; "z" only when clearly z.
- "&" for the ampersand / tironian et; "et" only when written out.
- Abbreviations: keep as written with superscript letters after a dot: "V.re Ma.te" (Vostre Majesté), "S.r" (Sieur),
  "p.r" (pour), "q." / "q.e" (que), "Mons.r", "led." (ledict), "cõ"/"quãt" with a tilde for a nasal stroke.
- Numerals as written. Proper names: capitalize only if the clerk did; spell exactly as seen.
- Uncertainty: {?} = illegible word (one per word), [word?] = doubtful reading. Never guess a fluent sentence over ink you
  cannot see; a gap marker is better than a plausible invention.
- Strike-throughs: <del>word</del>. Interlinear insertions: ^word^.
