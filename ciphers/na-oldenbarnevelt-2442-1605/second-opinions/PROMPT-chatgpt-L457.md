THE ITEM
- Don Juan Gara de Senisteros (probably "García"; identity not established) to Juan de la Peña, Alcalá, 23 December
  1605. A contemporary copy in the papers of Johan van Oldenbarnevelt: Nationaal Archief, The Hague, 3.01.14, inv. 2442
  (https://www.nationaalarchief.nl/onderzoeken/archief/3.01.14/invnr/2442). Spanish prose, mostly in a cipher that writes
  vowels as digits (a=4, e=8, i=3, o=7, u=2) inside otherwise plain words; we recovered that key ourselves.
- This request is about folios 59v-62r of the letter (our earlier request SO-OLDEN-2442-BC1 covers folios 55-56). Read, in
  period spelling and only partly graded as secure: the writer visited "Agustin Mexia, el cuñado del Regente", who on
  hearing that the Cardinal had given a canonry "se dio al demonio, diciendo que auia de ser la canongia para el doctor
  Cetina o para el doctor Sosa"; "el doctor Garai" and "Horacio Doria" asked whether he brought a letter of V.S. for the
  Cardinal; the writer "hice una firma de V.S. en un pliego de papel lo mejor que pude" and had a servant write the
  substance of the letter, "diciendole que V.S. me auia dexado algunas firmas en blanco"; a second letter "en nombre de
  V.S." concerned a bull "para poder tener con la rectoria la canongia i cathedra"; and a student nephew (Chaves) took
  "quarenta reales" from the money bag. Clear text on a neighbouring leaf mentions "la Abadia de Lerma donde el Duque funda
  ahora una iglesia collegial".
- Our audit and search log: https://github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/blob/main/ciphers/na-oldenbarnevelt-2442-1605/AUDIT.md
  (section "AUDIT 3").
