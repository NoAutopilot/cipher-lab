THE ITEM
- Don Juan Gara de Senisteros (probably "García"; identity not established) to Juan de la Peña, Alcalá, 23 December
  1605. A contemporary copy in the papers of Johan van Oldenbarnevelt: Nationaal Archief, The Hague, 3.01.14, inv. 2442
  (https://www.nationaalarchief.nl/onderzoeken/archief/3.01.14/invnr/2442). Spanish prose, mostly in a cipher that writes
  vowels as digits (a=4, e=8, i=3, o=7, u=2) inside otherwise plain words; we recovered that key ourselves.
- This request is about the cipher passage on folio 64r (scan 10, right page; our earlier requests SO-OLDEN-2442-BC1 and
  SO-OLDEN-2442-L457 cover folios 55-56 and 59v-62r). Read, in period spelling and only partly graded as secure: the writer
  will not decide a matter without V.S.'s answer; a lady and "el inquisidor Salcedo" had as grandmother "una hija de un
  clerigo i de una muger casada con un herrero", the clérigo having fathered her while the herrero was away and raised and
  dowered her in his house; "para casarla debieron de hacer al inquisidor collegial en Oviedo i despues inquisidor"; now
  "don Garcia de Medrano, collegial de Salamanca, natural de Soria, del habito de Santiago, del Consejo Real" has married
  "un hermano mayor suio, maiorazgo, con una hermana desta doncella", and "poco ha caso un oidor de chancilleria con otra";
  "por ser muger casada aquella del herrero ... no me atreuo a nada sin el parecer de V.S."
