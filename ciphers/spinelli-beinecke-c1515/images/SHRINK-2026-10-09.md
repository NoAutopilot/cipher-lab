## Why nothing was deleted

1. **Citations.** `images_manifest_full.tsv` lists all 213 image files with size, sha256, dimensions, source,
   regen line and `cited_by` (git grep -F of each basename across the whole repository, excluding
   images/manifest*.json; plus a `family:` entry for files naming a crop family prefix such as `p1v_`).
   All 25 full pages are cited by name: the three letter pages (beinecke3811294_*) by NOTES.md, LOOSE-ENDS.tsv,
   benchmark-tx/txeng2/txe2-sheet2/census_ownleaf.tsv and (p.1) the confirm flags and spinflags; the seven sibling
   pages and the fifteen bundle17296147 canvases by benchmark-tx/txeng2/txe2-sheet2/census_siblings.tsv. The two
   native regions (src_2_*) are cited by benchmark-tx/txeng/confirm/. Every line-crop family and the debug
   overlays are cited by family in NOTES.md, CAMPAIGN.md or corpus/*.tsv. Zero files are uncited.
2. **Format.** Every full page is already JPEG, so the "convert cited PNG/TIFF pages to JPEG" step has nothing
   to act on; re-encoding a JPEG is lossy and was left alone as the brief says.
3. **Testability.** Only the 20 crops cut from the two on-disk native regions can be byte-tested offline. The
   other 158 crops were cut from full canvases that were deliberately not kept (manifest.json
   `_note_sources_28sep2026`); testing their regen needs a Beinecke fetch, which this job did not have.

## Regen test (before any deletion would have been allowed)

`./regen_images.sh verify` (offline): re-cuts every crop whose source is on disk (box minus the region offset,
RGB, JPEG q85, the tools/iiif_lines.py default) and compares sha256: **20 byte-identical, 0 differ**
(p1c_L01-L08 s1/s2, p2c_L01-L02 s1/s2). `./regen_images.sh crop p1c_L03_s1.jpg` rewrote the committed file
identically (cmp clean). The full-canvas crops, p1v (sheared), p2x (0.19 grey levels, not byte-identical per its
own manifest note) and the debug overlays have regen lines in the manifest but are untested.
