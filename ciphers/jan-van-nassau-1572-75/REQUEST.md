## Lookup/copy ask: Staatsarchiv Marburg (HStAM), the "4f Nld. 165" second copy of the 17 April 1574 letter

**Target:** WVO 5551 (briefnr 5551), Jan (Johann VI) van Nassau to Willem van Oranje, 17 April 1574, from Köln
-- the two ciphered lines at the top of p.3 (`ciphers/jan-van-nassau-1572-75/AUDIT.md`, "V-TX2 second audit").
Rolf Glawischnig's 1973 study *Niederlande, Kalvinismus und Reichsgrafenstand 1559-1584. Nassau-Dillenburg unter
Graf Johann VI* (Marburg, 1973) cites, per a Google Books snippet read by a previous verifier pass, a second
copy of this letter cluster at **Staatsarchiv Marburg, "4f Nld. 165"** (his own footnote shorthand for "Bestand
4 f Niederlande", item/Paket 165).

**What was found this pass (TX-KEYS, 25 Sept 2026):** the fonds is now catalogued online in **Arcinsys Hessen**
(arcinsys.hessen.de), the Hessian state archives' finding-aid and digitisation system. It is reachable without
login (browser tool needed -- the typeahead-driven search forms do not work with plain curl POSTs, and a
site-wide free-text search only finds terms that literally co-occur in the same record). The relevant material
lives under **HStAM > 4 f Staaten N (Staatenabteilung: Nassau-Nürnberg) > 5 Nassau-Dillenburg, Fürsten**, split
across per-subject nodes (5.12 "Krieg; Militär", 5.14 "Politik", and others, 5.1-5.20) -- each item's own
"(Vor-) Provenienzen" field confirms these were reorganised out of an **older fonds literally named "4 f
Niederlande"** (e.g. one item's provenance note reads "Landgraf Wilhelm IV., aus 4 f Niederlande, Paket 1"),
each with its own old "Paket" number that does not survive as a literal substring in the new catalogue entry.
**Item "165" specifically was not located** in the pages sampled this pass (5.12 "Krieg; Militär" page 1 showed
items numbered 228, 780, 778...; 5.14 "Politik" showed 819, 141, 254, 364, 1574, 1579, 1651...; neither an
identifier-search nor a free-text search for "4 f Niederlande" AND "165" returned a hit). Several items in this
same branch do carry preview thumbnails in the online catalogue (confirmed by screenshot), so at least part of
this fonds is digitised -- but this pass could not confirm digitisation, or even locate the current catalogue
entry, for the specific old "165" item Glawischnig cites.

**What is needed:** the archive's own staff can resolve an old fonds/Paket citation like "4f Nld. 165" against
their current Arcinsys signature far faster than more blind browsing here (state archives normally keep a
concordance between a pre-digitisation numbering and the current one). Ask:
1. What HStAM's current Arcinsys signature is for the item cited by Glawischnig (1973) as "4f Nld. 165" (old
   fonds "4 f Niederlande", Paket/item 165).
2. Whether that item is digitised in Arcinsys (many neighbouring items in the same branch already are).
3. If digitised: a link to or a screen-quality image of the leaf(s) datable to 17 April 1574 (Jan van Nassau to
   Willem van Oranje, from Köln), specifically whether the two ciphered lines carry an interlinear or marginal
   period decipherment.
4. If not digitised: the cost/process for a reading-room photograph of that item.
