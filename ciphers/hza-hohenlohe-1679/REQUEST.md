# Copy request — HZA Neuenstein Sf 35 Bü 161 (with its key leaf), then Bü 226 and Bü 165

**Status:** waiting on you (ASKS row 116). Refreshed 3 Oct 2026 (GAPS103): all units below have LABW records and
no images online; no digitised sibling exists (NOTES.md, GAPS101 and GAPS103 sections). Nothing further can be done
in the cloud until copies exist.

**Archive:** Hohenlohe-Zentralarchiv Neuenstein (house archive maintained by the Landesarchiv Baden-Württemberg),
finding aid Sf 35 "Wilhermsdorf I / 1650-1719" (http://www.landesarchiv-bw.de/plink/?f=3-543). Order through LABW's
own online order basket ("Einheit in den Bestellkorb übernehmen") on each unit record; reproduction orders need an
LABW user account (Nutzungskonto). Reproduction terms may need the house's consent: ask when ordering.

**What to order, in this order:**

1. **Sf 35 Bü 161** — http://www.landesarchiv-bw.de/plink/?f=3-88062 — "Berichte (teilweise chiffriert) des Lic.
   Melchior an Graf Wolfgang Julius aus Wien, Prag und Straßburg", 1679-1680, 1 Fasz., Folio. The record's
   "Enthält" note reads "Beilage: Chiffrierschlüssel". Ask for: **digital colour scans of the whole bundle,
   explicitly including the enclosed cipher key (Chiffrierschlüssel) leaf or leaves**; if a whole-bundle order is
   too large, ask first for the key leaf plus every leaf carrying cipher (numbers or symbols), and a leaf count.
2. **Sf 35 Bü 226** — http://www.landesarchiv-bw.de/plink/?f=3-88134 — "Chiffre-Schlüssel zur Korrespondenz in
   Sachen Ochs", 1 Schr. (one sheet), undated (Ochs correspondence 1656-1670). Ask for: a digital scan of the sheet,
   both sides. Cheap, and a second key from the same chancery.
3. **Sf 35 Bü 165** — http://www.landesarchiv-bw.de/plink/?f=3-88066 — "Berichte (teilweise chiffriert) der ... nach
   Wien entsandten Diener ... Pape und ... Köhler", 1689, 1 Fasz., Folio. Ask for: scans of the leaves with cipher,
   or the whole bundle with a leaf count. Whether the Bü 161 key serves it is untested.
4. Optional, lower priority: **Oe 1 Bü 4330** — http://www.landesarchiv-bw.de/plink/?f=3-197690 — 1653-1655, the
   "Schriftstück mit Passagen in Geheimschrift sowie dazu passende Aufschlüsselung" inside that bundle only (ask the
   archive to locate it; the bundle is large).

**Suggested wording for the order note (German):** "Erbeten werden Digitalisate (Farbscans) von Sf 35 Bü 161
