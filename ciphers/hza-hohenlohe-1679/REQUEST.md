**What to request:** page copies (photographs or scans) of two bundles in the **Hohenlohe-Zentralarchiv
Neuenstein**, Sf 35:

  from Vienna, Prague and Strasbourg.
- **Bü 165**, 1689: reports (partly ciphered) of the servants Kanzleirat Johann Christoph Pape and
  Kammersekretär Georg Ludwig Köhler, sent to Vienna on family business.
