# Sign inventory, Juan Manuel cipher 1522 (shared by every pass)
The cipher mixes (a) lowercase Latin code groups of 2-3 letters (sof, gap, kef, qid, mus, bas, dim, ka, le, li ...)
and (b) non-Latin SYMBOLS standing for letters. Write Latin groups exactly as read (lowercase), and each SYMBOL as
one uppercase label below, every token separated by a single space. A Latin group often runs straight into a symbol
with no space ("bog3" = bog 3; "dim3" = dim Z if the 3 hangs below the line): split them.
Labels:
  V  v-shaped sign with a hooked/flourished left stroke (like a v with a curl; often starts a line)
  4  a numeral-4 shape
  T  crossed double-t, looks like "tt" with one bar through both, or "#"
  Z  z / 3 whose tail hangs well BELOW the line (like the letter yogh "ʒ")
  3  a 3-shape sitting ON the line, no long tail
  F  tall long-s with a loop / figure-8 at its foot (looks like "ß" or "f8"), usually stands alone
  Q  q or g whose descender is crossed by a horizontal stroke (like "ꝗ" or a dagger under the bowl)
  A  open alpha / fish sign ("α", like "∝" or "œ")
  D  d whose ascender loops back to the left ("ꝺ", like "∂")
  R  small raised sign like "ro" or "co" with a tiny "o" tucked below it (often follows a group)
  E  large epsilon "ε" / reversed 3 with a big upper bowl
  9  numeral 9
  X  a stand-alone x
  W  two linked o's / "co" joined with a loop ("ꝏ")
  B  a capital-B-like sign
  ?1 ?2 ...  any other symbol: give it the next free ?n label and describe it ONCE in the notes column.
A word or phrase written in plain (clear) Spanish among the cipher: write it inside [square brackets] as read,
e.g. [que me escriuiese que yo]. A group you cannot read at all: write ~ . Doubtful reading: append ? to the token.
Do not guess Spanish meanings; do not use any key or decipherment.
