## What is wanted, and why

Moore (TLS 1977, text on ericsams.org) says Sams's "solutions to unsolved cipher in Thurloe ... have been filed in the
Bodleian Library with Clarendon MS 94 (1973)". The volume itself is a set of seventeenth-century cipher keys. Ask for:

1. **Digital images of all 28 leaves of MS. Clarendon 94** (first choice), and of any loose or inserted material
   filed with it, in particular "notes by Eric Sams, 1973, on ciphers in Thurloe's State Papers".
2. If a full copy is refused or priced too high: images of only (a) any key headed for or attributable to William
   Stamford / "W. S." (Calais, 1654-55), and (b) the Sams notes.

Caveat for the reader: the catalogue calls these ciphers "on the royalist side"; Stamford wrote to Thurloe, so his key
may well not be in the volume. The Sams notes are the more likely payoff (they may say which Birch letters he read).
