## What to order

Four items, The National Archives, Kew, all `digitised: false` on Discovery, no route to view them
online:

1. **SP 78/111/93** — Folio 212, "Letter entirely in cipher." Covering date 1642 Sept 20/30. Discovery id
   C7327886.
2. **SP 78/111/135** — Folio 300, "Letter in cipher." Covering date 1644. Discovery id C7327928.
3. **SP 78/113/57** — Folio 106, "Despatch largely in cipher from [Wm. Swyfte]." Covering date 1657 Feb 24.
   Discovery id C7328029. Checked against Thurloe's *State Papers* vol. 6 (1657, archive.org
   `collectionofstat06thur`) on 23 September 2026 — not found in print there (see NOTES.md "Thurloe check").
4. **SP 78/113/123** — Folio 189, "Letter mostly in cipher — deciphered." Covering date 1657 May 18/28.
   Discovery id (see NOTES.md/sp78_113_123_detail.json). This item's own catalogue text says it already
   carries a decipherment, but since it is not digitised, whether that decipherment is interlinear on the
   original (as with Lorraine 377 and Dupuy 155, which turned out not to be cryptanalysis targets once
   imaged) or a separate translation cannot be told without the image — the copy is needed to decide
   whether this item is a target at all, not only to read it.

## Why these four and not others in the same pieces

Neither piece has any other item flagged cipher/decipher/undeciphered in a full Discovery term-sweep (see
NOTES.md, "Check-solved sweep"). No sibling decipherment exists for /93, /135 or /57 anywhere in either
piece. Not on DECODE, not in Bourdeau's or Aymeloglu's working lists, not on Tomokiyo's cached pages.

## Format requested

Digital copies (photograph/scan) of each folio, sufficient for transcription — not a certified/legal copy.
The National Archives' own digitisation-on-demand service is the usual route for this class of request;
exact pricing and turnaround were not looked up here (access playbook: no price guessed).
