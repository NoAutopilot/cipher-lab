## no.34 (fol.63r, lower block)
- Heading (M): "Rondinelli al S. D. [?] Fiorenza di parigi" (passes split on the middle words: "al S[curl] D. d", "Firenze/Fiorue", "di pregi/parigi"; "Rondinelli" read by both).
- French rule (H for the words, M for "s'escrivent" spelling): "les voyelles s'escrivent / dessus les consonantes / comme".
- Example (M): a struck pair "5 8" (struck), then four stacked units, small figure over a sign: 3 over "5-like", 8 over "u", 3 over "y", 3 over curl-"l"; below it "di parigi" (both passes read "di rarign/rarigi"; M).
- Example sign by sign, using only this block's own letter list (M, key-consistency of the annotation, not a reading of f.62): d=5, p=u, r=y, g=l, so the units are [3 over d] [8 over p] [3 over r] [3 over g]; the over-figures 8 and 3 match "a 8" and "i 3" in the list (e=[epsilon-like 3].n is a different glyph), which gives d+i, p+a, r+i, g+i = "di parigi". The example is therefore consistent with the list; the stacked figures read from the crop are M.
- Plain letters in the list that are written as non-Latin forms: t as a cross, y and z as curls (M).
