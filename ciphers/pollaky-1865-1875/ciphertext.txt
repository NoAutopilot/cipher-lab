## image: images/ad1-1865-secret-writing.png
## Layout: plaintext sentence with a run of 10 invented signs standing in for one clause/phrase.
## Sign table (each sign given an ID; SIGN-01 recurs, all others occur once in this ad):
##   SIGN-A  : a vertical bar followed by two dots stacked (like "|:"); appears once, at the very
##             start of line 1, immediately before the plaintext word "Your" -- position and shape
##             both suggest it could be a signature-initial code (compare "W." opening ads 3/4), but
##             this is an observation, not established.
##   SIGN-01 : two dots side by side, above a single horizontal dash. Occurs twice (positions 1 and 6
##             of the 10-sign run) -- the one repeated sign in the run.
##   SIGN-02 : a vertical bar with three dots stacked to its left and two dots (one above, one below)
##             to its right.
##   SIGN-03 : three horizontal dashes stacked (like "≡"), with two dots stacked to its right (like ":").
##   SIGN-04 : one horizontal dash, with three dots cascading diagonally down and to the right of it.
##   SIGN-05 : a vertical bar with one dot to its right.
##   SIGN-07 : an open and close parenthesis enclosing three dots stacked vertically.
##   SIGN-08 : three dots in a horizontal row, above a single horizontal dash.
##   SIGN-09 : two horizontal dashes stacked, with one dot above the top dash and one dot below the
##             bottom dash.
##   SIGN-10 : three dots stacked vertically, then three vertical bars side by side, then one dot.
##
