# MAJOR CORRECTION to the 23 Sept check-solved characterisation of this item (M6 sweep,
# ROOM.md 20:33 and NOTES.md "What the leaves show"): that sweep viewed only the low-resolution
# thumbnail of the recto (canvas23_folio20_carpi_cipher.jpg, 900x672) and described the cipher
# as "arithmetic-numeral groups" of "roughly 70-90" tokens confined to "the final third" of a
# single leaf. Full-resolution imaging this session (images/img/, native ~8300x6214 px per
# opening) shows instead:
#   1. The cipher is NOT arithmetic numerals. It is an invented symbol/glyph alphabet (letter-like
#      shapes resembling Greek minuscules, a few actual arabic digits, a triple-box mark, a "z"
#      shape etc.), consistent with a 1520s French diplomatic "chiffre a figures" of the kind
#      Tomokiyo catalogues for this circle -- see the key-family leads in NOTES.md.
#   2. The cipher passage does not end on the recto. It continues, unbroken, onto the leaf's own
#      VERSO (which the brief asked to check) for a further ~22 lines, then onto the recto of the
#      following leaf (foliated "21" in the manuscript's own period ink numbering, visible top
#      right of that leaf) for ~13 more lines, before the letter closes in plain French: two
#      paragraphs, then "Madame, je prie a Dieu qu'il vous croisse et vous doint tresbonne et
#      longue vie. De Rome ce xxvij [27] jour d'Octobre l'an mil Vc xxv [1525]", signed "de Carpi".
#   3. That closing date, 27 October 1525, does not match the finding aid's date for item "20"
#      (22 oct. 1525, BnF Archives et manuscrits notice cc885911, "Presentation du contenu").
#      Given the letter reads as one continuous document (no new salutation or address restarts
#      between the recto tail, the verso, and folio 21's recto -- the verso opens and closes
#      mid-cipher with no heading), the more likely explanation is that 22 is a misreading of the
#      roman numeral for 27 (xxij / xxvij, an easy confusion in a period hand) rather than a
#      second, uncatalogued letter; this is flagged, not resolved, and is NOT a claim of a new
#      item (rule 10) -- it needs a fresh check-solved/finding-aid comparison, not a solver's say-so.
#   4. Total extent is therefore roughly 44 cipher lines across 3 page-sides (recto tail ~9,
#      verso ~22, folio-21-recto ~13), on the order of 500 tokens at this hand's ~11-12
#      tokens/line -- not "70-90" and not "one leaf".
#
