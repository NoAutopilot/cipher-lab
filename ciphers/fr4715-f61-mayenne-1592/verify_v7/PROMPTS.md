## setD (with the 2-hook category)
Categories: A = 4-head: a figure-4 stroke (an angled open 4) rising above or sitting on the hash, often with one vertical running down below the line.
D = 2-hook ("2#"): a 2-shaped hooked stroke (a curve with a flat or hooked foot) leading into the hash from its left/top, the whole reading like "2#" or
"24". B = looped: two small loops (closed rings) sitting on the hash, with only short verticals. N = other: a plain hash with nothing attached, a
different sign, or you cannot tell. "Do not guess what letters these signs mean; shape only."

## setN (without)
A and B as above; N = other: anything else, including a plain hash with nothing attached, a different sign, or you cannot tell.
