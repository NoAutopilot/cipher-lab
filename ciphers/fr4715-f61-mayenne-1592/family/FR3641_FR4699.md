## BnF fr.3641, item 59, f.126r-v (the "Noyon" lead)

- Finding aid: Français 3641, ark:/12148/cc500920, item 59 (FRBNFEAD000050092_d0e900 region), **Fol. 126**: "Lettre,
  avec chiffre et déchiffrement, relatant la prise de Noyon par le duc de Mayenne et traitant de l'état des affaires de
  la Ligue. « Da Reims, alli 4 di maggio 1593 ». En italien." The record flags a digitised version:
  https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b52508089f.
- Gallica: 348 canvases; folio labels read from the manifest: **f.126r = canvas 269** (3994x5791), f.126v = canvas 270
  (4009x5827). Five offset runs in the volume (k = 8, 10, 12, 14, 18), so use the labels, not a formula.
- **Cipher: not f.61's.** The leaf (recto 30 cipher rows, verso about 30 rows ending "da Reims alli 4 di maggio 1593")
  is enciphered in Arabic **two-digit figure groups** with a few extra signs (Δ, ∇, p, nr, a hooked 2, a dotted i, and
  dot or colon separators). The native crop's first cipher row reads "Δ 17 92 4 91 p 49 82 92 55 63 67 . 17 67 2 22 55 :
  70 2 45 74 46 46 81 75 46 90 75 16 56"; the first four rows alone use about thirty distinct figure groups (17 92 49 82 55
  63 67 45 74 46 81 75 90 16 56 66 78 88 60 80 62 22 68 47 43 24 57 91 73 ...). That is a figure substitution
  (homophonic or syllabic, Italian), the design family of the Spanish-side ciphers Tomokiyo lists for this volume
  (`sources/cryptiana/web/phelippes.htm`: fr.3641 is a file of intercepted Spanish letters, 1588-90 and 1592-93). The
  Mayenne cipher writes none of this: no numerals in figure pairs, about 11 sign shapes in all.
- **Decipherment: yes**, interlined above every cipher row in Italian ("Scrissi a V.S. Illustrissima nel principio di
  marzo la quale lettera le sara come spero venuta a le mani di poi li ho mandata un altra mia..."). Useful for someone
  working that Italian figure cipher; no use for f.61's key.
- Step 3 does not apply: no pass, no alignment, no per-leaf key.
- Aside, not checked further: items 68 and 71 of the same volume (f.143, f.148: Mayenne and the League council's
  instruction to Brisson, and Mayenne's letter to the Pope, 1589) are in Cg.13 per Tomokiyo, also a Spanish figure
  cipher, so not leads either.

## BnF fr.4699, items 18-19, f.37 and f.41 (the Fortia lead)

- Finding aid: Français 4699 (Anc. 9521), ark:/12148/cc57749t: **Fol. 37**, item 18: "lettre, avec chiffre et
  déchiffrement, de P. de Fortia « à Mr de Roissieu, commissaire general des vivres, conseiller et secretaire d'Estat
  pres de Monseigneur [le duc de Mayenne]... A Lyon, le 7 de fevrier 1593. Dupplicata »"; **Fol. 41**, item 19: the same
  to "Mr de La Chapelle, conseiller et secretaire d'Estat pres de Mr le duc du Mayne... A Lion, le 7 de fevrier 1593".
  The record's content note: "Les feuillets 39, 40, 87 et 94 ont été coupés" (ff.39-40 sit between the two letters).
- **Not digitised**: the record carries no "Version numérisée" link (fr.3641's does), and Gallica SRU
  `dc.source adj "Français 4699"` returns 0 records where the identical query finds fr.3641 (1 record, btv1b52508089f).
- Same cipher? **Undetermined.** French, the League side, written to two of Mayenne's secretaries of state in the same
  month as f.108v (28 Feb 1593) -- a more plausible user of the Mayenne family ciphers than fr.3641, but whether its
  cipher is the polyphonic design cannot be told without the image. Decipherment: the catalogue says yes (as it did,
  correctly, for fr.3641 item 59).
