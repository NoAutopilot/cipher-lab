Debosnys Sign Sorter

Every sign from the four published cryptogram images, cut out and piled by the label our readers gave it. Your job is to settle the alphabet: which piles are one sign, which tiles were put in the wrong pile, and which marks are not letters at all.

Tile in the wrong pileClick tiles to select them (orange outline), then "Move to" another pile, or "New pile" to start one, e.g. X with two bars. Moved tiles show a dot.
Two piles are one signPick the other pile under "Same sign as". Small pen wobbles do not make a new sign; a mark repeated on several tiles (a bar, a dot) usually does.
Not a letterDots, stains, flourishes and joins: press "Not a letter". If a pile looks right, press "All one sign".
