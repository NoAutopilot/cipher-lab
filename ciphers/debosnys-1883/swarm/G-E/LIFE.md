## Languages

Period print: "can write and speak six languages -- English, French, Italian, Spanish, Greek and Portuguese" (Police
Gazette 12 May 1883; Herald reprints). Wikipedia lists French, Greek, Latin, Portuguese and Spanish writings. The one
published sample of his "Portuguese" (Farnsworth 2010, transcribed by Sektu, 23 Jun 2017) is not Portuguese that Sektu
could read: "Comoderondas inacia bêco olondo inoto para / Imbiabo kotaronc molonk niarotan pérana", and a passage headed
"inno calledaz" ("Ontro de palade mosa kaen faleï tonüe dhala pico indor kouniss plaira colrose ..."). Sektu notes
word-initial k-, dh-, no repeated word, one monosyllable, and leaves three readings open: phonetic Portuguese, an argot or
obscure language, or an invented one. His Latin sample is a Vulgate quotation; his Greek is Moore's Greek preface ode,
altered (verso of cryptogram 4); a clear French poem of his is on the c3 page and another was shown to be copied
(Cipherbrain, 29 Oct 2021, "Henry Debosnys war ein Abschreiber").

## Names

Henry Deletnack De Bosnys (NY Tribune 1882), Henry D. Debosnys, "Edward Debosnys" (Savannah 1883), "Hênêcos Debosnostys"
(signature, c4b), "H.D.D.L.M.F." (c2a L09). The Police Gazette: "Debosnys is merely an assumed name". Bauer's index adds
"Debosnys Captain, Maurice", "E. A. Deletnack" and "Midole D.". Proposed identities (all later, none established): Jacob
Pomries of the Cimbria manifest (Sektu, Part IV, 29 Jun 2017, tentative); "Colonel Henry" = Henri Prodhomme as a parallel
(Sektu, 3 Jul 2017); "Deletnack" from "vicomte de Letnac" (Cantel reversed; Sektu, 5 Jul 2017); Pierre Keff (Pelling, 3
May 2021, ruled out by Pelling himself on 7 May 2021: released 1889, died Paris 1911); Edouard Keff (Pelling, 15 Aug 2021,
inconclusive).

## The three trunks (brief item 5a)

Wikipedia, citing Bauer (2017, pp.195-217): three trunks were sent to him in jail; two with weapons and ammunition were
seized, the third, with papers, cryptograms and poems, reached him; sender unknown [S]. **No period report of trunks,
boxes or baggage sent to the jail was found** in the Chronicling America pages read (40 hits, 16 full texts) or in IA full
text. Not a negative: NYS Historic Newspapers (Elizabethtown Post, Essex County Republican), the likeliest local source,
answered 403 (Cloudflare), and Bauer's trunk passage did not surface in any snippet. The only trunks in period print are
unrelated: the woman's trunk left in his yacht (Burlington, 11 Aug 1882) and Lemaire's trunk (Burlington, 24 Nov 1882).

## What would a man in that cell write about? (for CRIBS.tsv; context, not evidence)

- His innocence and the "mistake" (gallows speech); the blood on the knife; the Scotchman; whiskey.
- His wife Betsy/Elizabeth; his earlier wives (Judith; Mlle. Desmarais); a woman who visited him (the papers' keeper).
- Escape: the invisible-ink letter asked for help to escape; weapons in two trunks.
- His claimed past: Lisbon/Belem, Paris, St Brieuc, the Arctic, Crimea, Mexico, Gettysburg, Bourbaki, the Commune, Custer.
- Death and eternity: the c3 clear poem ("je passe", "l'avenir", "l'éternité"); "donkey to ride through purgatory".
- Friendship and brotherhood: the handshake with "L.M.F." (Farnsworth: the Masonic grip "Boaz"), the three links, the
  trowel, the dove -- see PICTURES.md.
- His birthday: the clear "516" and the 6-1-5 cube on the No.9 page equal his claimed birth date, 16 May (5/16).

## Discrepancies logged

Execution 27 Apr (period) vs 26 Apr (brief); jury ten minutes (period) vs eight (Wikipedia); correctionhistory.org puts
the trial in "December 1882" (period: March 1883); body $10 vs $15; Arctic leader Leclaire vs McClure; four daughters
vs three children; victim Betsy A. / Elizabeth / Eliza.

## Additions from the writings pass (WRITINGS.tsv, 29 Sept 2026)

- A third wife-name in his papers: "my dear Celestine", a poem dated 5 Mar 1882 and signed "By her husband" (copied from
  Chivers, "To Isa in Heaven"; Brown 2021). Farnsworth captions a drawing "in a dream", "presumably of wife Celestine".
- Tattoos, described once: two crossed French tricolour flags, an anchor with a coiled cable, a sabre scar on the right
  knuckles (*A Garvey Family*, 1989, Google Books snippet). The anchor is also an in-line cipher picture.
- His autobiography was printed in two instalments by the Elizabethtown Post (1883, per correctionhistory.org; not read,
  nyshistoricnewspapers.org is Cloudflare-blocked from the cloud).
- A turnkey's autograph book (a 17-year-old boy) with Debosnys entries is held by the Essex County Historical Society
  (Adirondack Life 1996, snippet): a public-catalogue item, contents not described.
- He gave autographs to several people on the morning of the execution (Der Deutsche Correspondent, 28 Apr 1883).
