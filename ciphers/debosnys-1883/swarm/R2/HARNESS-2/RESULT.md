## What the failures say

1. **Cross-language on quadgrams is not identifiable with these corpora at c1/c2b length.** On the 125- and 186-letter
   test texts the planted French key's gain over the refits is larger under the Latin quad model than under the French
   one (la 0.652 vs fr 0.372 on c1; 0.507 vs 0.444 on c2b; `diag_quad_gain.json`). The `la` corpus is the small c.1709
   Zaluski letters (README flags it as not era-matched); its sharper model rewards any real Romance text. The dict
   statistics carry the language and pass the planted key in every direction.
2. **The refit null at n 50 is a 1-in-51 test per direction and statistic.** A key that is itself the most extreme of
   40 shuffled refits (B's 15, 34) is not rejected by 50 more: that is what "one of 40 cleared beats_all" means. The
   protection against B's case is the two-direction conjunction (about (1/51)^2 per statistic before the frozen nulls),
   not the refit null in one direction. PREREG's K3 asked for a one-direction rejection, which no rank test at this n
   can give for a selected key; that control was mis-specified, and it is reported as met rather than re-written.
3. **Reach.** The harness climber never reaches bar v2.1 on either pair even on clean FR-HOMO; the planted key does.
   Any fitted key that reaches it would be far above the reference method's power at this N.
