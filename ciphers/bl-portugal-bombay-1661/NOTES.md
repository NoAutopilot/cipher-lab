# Portuguese royal despatches, Afonso VI's ministers to the ambassador in England/France — BL Add MS 38038

QUEUE row: N45, "Candidates not on DECODE" (N41-N66 block, scoring pass of 24 September 2026). British
Library, Western Manuscripts, Add MS 38038. Catalogue description: "ORIGINAL DISPATCHES, many of them in
duplicate, from Alfonso VI, King of Portugal, and his ministers [Luis de Sousa e Vasconcellos], Conde de
Castello Melhor, and Antonio de Sousa de Macedo, to Fran[cisco de Mello, Marquez de Sande]," 1662-1666, "many
of them in duplicate, with seals," containing a named "Cyphers: Portuguese despatches In cypher: 1662-1666"
section (per QUEUE.md's next-step note; the full uncut catalogue description was not re-fetched this pass,
BL's own catalogue search having been offline since the 2023 cyber attack per LESSONS.md item 4). The embassy
is Francisco de Mello, Marquês de Sande's, extraordinary ambassador in England and France 1658-1665, covering
the period of the Bombay-cession marriage treaty (23 June 1661) and Charles II's marriage to Catherine of
Braganza.

## Editions-first check (24 September 2026)

The standard edition for this embassy is Edgar Prestage, *The Diplomatic Relations of Portugal with France,
England, and Holland from 1640 to 1668* (Watford, 1925), reviewed at
`persee.fr/doc/hispa_0007-4640_1927_num_29_2_2274_t1_0221_0000_2` (confirms the book's existence and scope;
review text itself not fetched, out of budget). Also named in the brief: *Corpo diplomatico portuguez*
(Academia das Ciências de Lisboa) and Santarém's *Quadro elementar*. `archive.org` holds only one volume under
the `corpodiplomatico*` identifier prefix (`corpodiplomatico09acad`, v.9) via `advancedsearch.php`; a WebSearch

coverage (by treaty-partner sub-series, not strict chronology) was confirmed to include the 1662-1666
Sande/Castello Melhor/Sousa de Macedo correspondence with England specifically — the *Corpo diplomatico
portuguez* series is organised by counterparty state across many tomes, and which tome covers Anglo-Portuguese
relations 1660s was not identified this pass. No full-text search was run against either indexed volume (would
need a second archive.org request under this session's shared 60-request cap; deferred, see below). WebSearch
for Prestage's book text directly, and for "Sande" "Castello Melhor" "cifra"/"cypher" together, returned only
general Portugal-Bombay historical background (the 1661 marriage treaty, Castelo Melhor's tenure as Secretary
