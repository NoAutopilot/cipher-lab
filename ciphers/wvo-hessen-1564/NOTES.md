## Correction to the brief's own description

The brief describes 1109 as "Willem van Hessen to Willem van Oranje". **WVO's own record reverses this**:
1109 is **Aan Willem van Hessen** ("Aan" = To) -- i.e. **Willem van Oranje to Wilhelm IV, Landgrave of
Hesse-Kassel**, sent from Brussels, 18 Sept 1564, archived at the *recipient's* archive (HSAM, Bestand 3II,
Nassau-Niederlande, Korr. 1564-1565, f. 22r-24v) -- which is exactly why the original with its cipher
enclosure survives at Marburg rather than at the sender's own Nassau archive. 1107 (22-8-1564 per WVO,
Wilhelm of Hesse to the Prince, from Kassel) is the letter 1109's own Opmerkingen field cross-references
("Vgl. 1107" = cf. 1107) -- an earlier, separate, uncoded letter in the same exchange (see below), not the
letter carrying the cipher.
