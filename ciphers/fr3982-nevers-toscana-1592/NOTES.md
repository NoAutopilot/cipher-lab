## Leaf look (image: openapi.bnf.fr, canvas 57 of btv1b9060543f; canvas 58 is f.26, blank, with the foot of f.25 at its left edge)
- Gallica ark btv1b9060543f (fr.3982), manifest 561 canvases, all labelled "NP". Canvas 57 = f.25 recto (pencil 25 not read on canvas 57; canvas 58 carries pencil "26" on the blank leaf that follows). Images: images/c57.jpg, c58.jpg, cipher_block_2x.jpg (local crop of c57), images/m.json.
- Clear text, Italian, a minute in a secretary's hand, with a pencil date at the top ("2? d'aoust 1592") and a later hand. Opening (as read, M): "del Duca suo figliolo ... come il Re ... la proposta si [fa?] ... Duca di Mayena ... la inevitabile divisione del Regno"; then "Il Duca di Parma fa leva ... fra 2 mesi ... non potrà resistere a tal forza ma l'impedirà di no[n] far gran progresso" followed by the cipher block; then "... il tempo, per il meno sei mesi", a plea to the Grand Duke to attend to the affairs of France, dated "Del Campo sotto Provin il 26 ago 1592" (month abbreviated, M), signed "Humilliss.mo Ser.re et Ser.re Obedient.mo".
- Cipher extent: one block of 7 lines inside the Italian text, about 35-40 digits per line, about 250 digits in all (rough, from the 1600 px view). It reads as one unsegmented string of digits 0-9 with no separators and no marks above the figures that I could see; some digits carry a flourish or overbar-like stroke (I, 1 with serif), M.
- Sign type: figures only, run together (no group breaks), the shape that Desenclos and Lasry 2024 describe for fr.3620 f.70 ("mostly contiguous digits").
- No interlinear or marginal decipherment on f.25 and none visible on f.26. Faint verso show-through only.
