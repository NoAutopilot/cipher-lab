## Scoring (crib14/run.py)
Wide corpus = tools/data/en16_repo + en18 + sco16 (all era-nearer English on disk; en16_repo alone is 31.5k words,
too thin for word bigrams). Same unit/run construction as likely9/run.py (unkeyed tokens break runs).
- 19: S(c) = letter-weighted mean 4-gram log10 P (NgramModel, wide) over every readable run of >= 4 letters that
  contains a 19 token, with 19 := c.  Statistics: m = max S over {r,n} - max S over the other 24 letters;
  d = S(r) - S(n).
- 216: S(c) = mean word-bigram log10 P (WordBigram as likely9, wide) over adjacent readable pairs that include a 216
  token, with 216 := c.  Statistic: g = S(top) - S(second) over the in-band list; also the top word and whether any
  off-band candidate beats it.

## Controls (each can vary on the statistic: they change which positions/figure the loop scores)

- C2 position shuffle: the figure's own occurrence count placed at that many random unkeyed positions of the same range
  (0-99 for 19, 100-430 for 216), replacing those tokens, seeds 1-50; same statistic.
