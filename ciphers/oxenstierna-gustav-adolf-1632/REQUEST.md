# Request: oxenstierna-gustav-adolf-1632 (row W1/R10, key hunt)

No personal data below; log the person's own archive request separately, by date and archive only, per
CLAUDE.md rule 9.

**What's needed:** copies (photographs or digital scans) of two candidate holdings at Riksarkivet i
Stockholm/Täby, neither digitised (confirmed via the Sok-API, 24 Sept 2026):

1. `SE/RA/202/1` — Chifferklaver, box ("låda") I, item 1: "Kanslichiffer 1620-1650-talen", a bundle of about 20
   cipher-key booklets, including Anders Svensson's own ciphers (bound 1630), and material for Camerarius,
   Falckenberg, Spens and the Oxenstierna-Torstensson correspondence. This is a different box from the one
   already read by github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver (`riksarkivet1628/`, `baner1640/`, both drawn from
   Chifferklaver låda II) — låda I has not been surveyed by either solver repository.
2. `SE/RA/720095/05/01/E 2348` and `E 2350` — in the Horn-Bielkesamlingen (Riksmarsken Gustaf (Carlsson) Horns
   papper): "Gustaf II Adolfs brev till Gustaf Horn" (1631-1632) and "Kungliga skrivelser (dubletter) till
   Gustaf Horn" (1628-1644, "dubletter" = duplicates) — the likely home of the "duplett"/"triplett" cipher
   letters of 23 July 1632 that the 19th-century editor's footnote names but says he could not find a key for.

**Why a request is needed:** neither item is digitised; the Riksarkivet's own search UI (`sok.riksarkivet.se`)
is captcha-walled to automated access, and its underlying Sok-API (`data.riksarkivet.se`) gives catalogue
records only, no images. A reading-room visit or a reproduction order is the only route to either item.
