# Request: oxenstierna-gustav-adolf-1632 (row W1/R10, key hunt + original-letter hunt)

No personal data below; log the person's own archive request separately, by date and archive only, per
CLAUDE.md rule 9.

**What's needed:** copies (photographs or digital scans) of three candidate holdings at Riksarkivet i
Stockholm/Täby, none digitised (confirmed via the Sok-API, 24 Sept 2026):

1. `SE/RA/202/1` — Chifferklaver, box ("låda") I, item 1: "Kanslichiffer 1620-1650-talen", a bundle of about 20
   cipher-key booklets, including Anders Svensson's own ciphers (bound 1630), and material for Camerarius,
   Falckenberg, Spens and the Oxenstierna-Torstensson correspondence. This is a different box from the one
   already read by github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver (`riksarkivet1628/`, `baner1640/`, both drawn from
   Chifferklaver låda II) — låda I has not been surveyed by either solver repository.
2. `SE/RA/720095/05/01/E 2348` and `E 2350` — in the Horn-Bielkesamlingen (Riksmarsken Gustaf (Carlsson) Horns
   papper): "Gustaf II Adolfs brev till Gustaf Horn" (1631-1632) and "Kungliga skrivelser (dubletter) till
   Gustaf Horn" (1628-1644, "dubletter" = duplicates) — the likely home of the "duplett"/"triplett" cipher
   letters of 23 July 1632 that the 19th-century editor's footnote names but says he could not find a key for.

3. `SE/RA/720701/II/01/B/I/E 614` — "Gustaf II Adolf", 1630-1632, in the **Oxenstiernska samlingen**
   (Oxenstiernska samlingen > Oxenstierna af Södermöre > Axel Oxenstierna > Inkomna brev och ansökningar >
   Furstars, ämbetsmäns och enskilda personer brev och ansökningar) — Axel Oxenstierna's own bound volume of
   incoming letters from the King. Its catalogue note states verbatim "Breven finns tryckta i Axel Oxenstiernas
   skrifter, Avd. II, Band 1." (the letters are printed in Axel Oxenstiernas skrifter, Section II, Volume 1),
   confirming this is Styffe's own source volume for the printed letter no. 602 this target is built from. Not
   digitised (`onlyDigitisedMaterials: false`; confirmed at the RDF level — its only instantiation is typed
   Analog, no image manifest). No item-level listing exists, so the 23 July 1632 leaf's position in the volume
   is unconfirmed until it is opened. **This is the strongest candidate for a contemporary interlinear or
   marginal decipherment**, since it is Oxenstierna's own received copy, not a later transcript — found by the
   original-letter hunt, 24 Sept 2026 (LANE R worker R5); see NOTES.md's "Original letter hunt" section for the
   full search log, including a digitised but provenance-mismatched Riksregistraturet volume (`SE/RA/1112.1/B/176`,
   the domestic Council's Stockholm register, not the King's field correspondence) ruled out as the wrong channel.

gives catalogue records only, no images. A reading-room visit or a reproduction order is the only route to any
of them.
