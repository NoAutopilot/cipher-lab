(Discovery id C3677736, catalogueLevel 6/piece level, `isParent: false` -- no item-level breakdown exists in

under the heading "Cipher for Spanish" -- "PRO SP 106/6 no. 34 is a decoding table of a similar cipher with a
vocabulary in Spanish. It uses 1-211 without diacritics, 1-210 with an overbar, 1-211 with a double bar, 1-211
with triple dots, 1-210 with m, 1-220 with a crossed circle, 1-240 with a grave, 1-239 with a double prime,
1-234 with a %-like sign. Single letters are scattered at several groups in these figures rather than forming
their own section."

## Verdict

**open.** Eleven genuine, still-undeciphered ciphertext items exist in SP 84/165 (ff.187, 228, 231, 243, 258,
263, 287, 306, 313, 315, 317) naming or plausibly connected to Spanish diplomatic correspondence involving the
Marqués de la Fuente; none of them, nor a decipherment of any of them, appears in any of the six sources
checked. No prior work by either solver repository exists on this correspondence. A candidate key (SP 106/6,
with three DECODE-listed pages of Spanish vocabulary) exists in the same office's filing but its identity with
Tomokiyo's "no. 34" and its applicability to these specific intercepts are both unconfirmed (premise check (a),
(b)). None of the eleven items is digitised; a copy order is the only route to an image.
