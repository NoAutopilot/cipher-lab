# The Duke's 1447 slips (italien 1584 f.5, f.8, f.15): ONE label convention for every reader (SFZ-DUKE, 8 Oct 2026)

Why: SFZ-NEXT (8 Oct 2026, ROOM 00:35 UTC) found its Pusterla readers clashing on conventions (T=/b-, d/g), so a reader
disagreement measured whether two readers chose different *names*, not different *shapes*. For the Duke every reader
(SFZ-D's first read and every later pass) uses the labels below and only these. Labels are ../amidani/labels.md's plus its
"Additions (SFZ-D)" N-labels; nothing is renamed. Rules fixed before the second read:

1. **Split, never join.** One token per letter-shape. A run that looks like a word ("par", "per", "co", "mo", "bı", "g#")
   is written shape by shape (`cp ca cr`, `c co`, `g #`); `·ll·` is `l l`. Joining is a later, scripted hypothesis on the
   committed tokens (g1_duke_tests.py), never a reading decision, so two readers can only differ on shapes.
2. **The clash pairs, decided once:** `g` = the 9-shaped sign (open or closed upper bowl, descender) wherever it stands,
   including "9o" (= `g co`); `8` = figure-8 with a CLOSED lower loop; `d` = eth-like d with the ascender curled left;
   `b` = plain b/6; `P` = b with a bar through the ascender (ƀ); `N1` = h with a bar through the ascender (ħ), with or
   without a second bar or dot (variants are not split); `T`/`N13` = π (both labels exist; write `N13` for the Duke).
   `t` = cross / plus-like sign with a long bar ("+", "⊢" with a hook); `D` = division sign ÷ (bar with dots).
3. **Marks**: `N7` is the stand-alone "=" after a sign (written as its own token, after the sign: `N1 N7`); a
   superscript dot or circle is `^` appended to the label. Unreadable: `?`; torn between two labels: `a/b?`.
4. **Off-sheet shapes**: `X1`, `X2`, ... with a one-line description (pass-local; harmonised by description afterwards).
