# Copy request — William L. Clements Library, Clinton Papers, vol. 64:14 and 64:15

**Status:** waiting on you. Open — see NOTES.md's Check-solved section, 25 Sept 2026 (LANE CX2): Doniol vol.
IV and Meng's *Despatches and Instructions of C. A. Gérard* both full-text searched with controls; neither

**What to request:**
- **Clinton Papers, vol. 64:14** — Admiral d'Estaing to Gérard (Martinique, 30 April 1779), the ciphered
  letter itself (this target's `ciphertext.txt`), intercepted by the British and forwarded home by Sir Henry
  Clinton in July 1779.
- **Clinton Papers, vol. 64:15** — d'Estaing's clear letter to Jean Holker of the same date (two copies, one
  in André's hand) — a plaintext letter from the same sender on the same day, useful as a language/register
  control and possible crib source even though it is a different addressee and (per Tomokiyo's page) not
  itself the plaintext of 64:14.

**Why:** per `sources/cryptiana/web/unsolved.htm`, Tomokiyo lists this pair as unsolved and already identifies
the exact shelfmark; the cipher's own key would sit in AAE, Correspondance politique, États-Unis suppléments
t.1 (not online) or a British decrypt at TNA if Willes read it — neither of which is what is being requested
here. This is a straightforward image request for a named, already-identified item, not a search.
