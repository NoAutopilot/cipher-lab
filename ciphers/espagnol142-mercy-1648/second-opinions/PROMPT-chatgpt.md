SECOND OPINION REQUEST, label SO-MERCY-F22

We are an open cipher-research repository (github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab). We have read, by our own cryptanalysis,
part of a Spanish ciphered instruction of 1648, and we want you to try to prove that its text was already known before
us (in print, in a calendar, or in a clear copy), and to find mistakes in our reading. Be adversarial. Give exact
citations (author, title, year, volume, page, or archive and folio, with a URL where one exists). Say plainly when you
cannot find something; do not invent a citation.

THE ITEM
- Bibliothèque nationale de France, Espagnol 144 (tome III of Espagnol 142-144, ex Supplément français 1257), f.22r-22v.
  Finding aid: https://archivesetmanuscrits.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cc347546 (item 11: "Autre instruction chiffrée pour
  l'abbé de Mercy. Barneton, 6 juin 1648"). Images: Gallica ark:/12148/btv1b10035717h, views 58-59.
- Unsigned; opens in clear "Baron de Mercy mi Sumiller de Cortina ..." and closes "Barneton a seis Junio de 1648".
  The same volume (f.20-21v) holds plain instructions to the same "Abbad de Mercij, mi Sumiller de Cortina", catalogued
  as given by Archduke Leopold Wilhelm, Brussels 8 Feb and 13 April 1648 (talks with the duc de Longueville, the
  duchesse de Chevreuse and the comte de Saint-Ibal at Kempen). Mercy was Leopold Wilhelm's chaplain (Lonchay 1896,
  p.445). We infer, but cannot read, that the sender is Leopold Wilhelm.
- Cipher: 521 numeric code tokens over 38 values, a letter-level homophonic cipher, interleaved with clear Spanish.

OUR READING, as the decode renders it, brackets ours (cipher runs in capitals; grade S = cryptanalytic, some tokens M = uncertain; no key sheet, no known plaintext)
"... ALANDOSE [= hallandose] ESTAS ARMAS EN CAMPAGNA y asi Holgare me digais lo que en esta razon saueis entendido. DE LA DUQUESA DE
CHEUREUSE Y DEL [word code] y porque qualquiera ora de tardanca ... PASAREIS A CLEUES A UEROS CON EL ELECTOR DE BRANDENBURG
... Y EMBIAN CARTAS DE CREENCIA QUE UAN CON ESTA Y LES PROPONDREIS DES I SE [= de si se] PERMITIRA SE LEUANTEN EN AQUEL PAIS TRES MIL
HOMBRES DE INFANTERIA EN DOS O TRE SEGIMIENTI [= tres regimientos, not yet reading cleanly] Y CON QUE CONDICIONES ... En todo os encargo la brevedad ... Barneton a
seis Junio de 1648". About two fifths of the cipher does not yet read.

WHAT TO LOOK FOR
1. Any print or calendar of this 6 June 1648 instruction, or of a mission by the abbé/Baron de Mercy to the Elector of
   Brandenburg at Cleves (Kleve) in June-July 1648, or of a Spanish request to raise about 3,000 infantry in Cleves or the
   Brandenburg Rhineland lands in 1648. Candidate places: Lonchay, Cuvelier and Lefèvre, Correspondance de la Cour
   d'Espagne sur les affaires des Pays-Bas au XVIIe siècle, vol. IV (1933); Urkunden und Actenstücke zur Geschichte des
   Kurfürsten Friedrich Wilhelm von Brandenburg (any volume); Acta Pacis Westphalicae; CODOIN vols 82-84; Victor Cousin,
   Madame de Chevreuse; Lonchay, La rivalité de la France et de l'Espagne aux Pays-Bas (1896); studies of Leopold
   Wilhelm's government of the Netherlands; Dutch or German studies of Brandenburg-Spanish relations 1648.
2. Any clear copy of the instruction: Archives générales du Royaume, Brussels, Secrétairerie d'État et de Guerre
   (Lonchay's "S.E.E." registers t. LXIV-LXV, 1648), Simancas Estado (Flandes, 1648), Vienna HHStA.
3. Any published study of BnF Espagnol 142-144 or of the abbé de Mercy (brother of the general Franz von Mercy), with
   his first name and career.
4. Any key of Leopold Wilhelm's household or the Brussels secretariat of 1647-1650 (for example DECODE records 958-965,
   "chiffres 1647-98") published anywhere, and whether it matches a 38-value letter cipher.
5. Anything in our reading that is historically impossible or implausible for June 1648 (for example, was the Elector
   at Cleves then?).
