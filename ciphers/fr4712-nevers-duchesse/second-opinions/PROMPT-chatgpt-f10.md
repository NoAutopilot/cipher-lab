THE ITEM
- Bibliothèque nationale de France, Français 4712, f.10 (Gallica https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9058289m, view 18):
  a letter of Louis de Gonzague, duc de Nevers, to the duchesse de Nevers (Henriette de Clèves). The BnF catalogue of 1895
  says it "a une partie chiffrée", is dated October 1592 and signed with a monogram. The cipher passage is 37 numbers
  (printed by S. Tomokiyo, cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/nevers.htm, as "undeciphered"):
  82 82 52 14 10 85 92 21 60 13 16 52 74 56 92 8 59 [sign] 31 72 10 78 12 69 20 21 22 95 85 25 12 19 93 [sign] 10 24 17 81 26
- Folio 13 of the same volume, "Fragment de dépêche en clair et en chiffre", carries code numbers with interlinear glosses
  of the time. Three of them occur in the f.10 passage: 82 glossed "D. n.", 21 "Berry", 12 "b. Pal.". On a rule fixed in
  advance, and on one reader's judgement that the two leaves are in the same hand, we take those six f.10 numbers as those
  words. The other 31 numbers are unread. We claim nothing about what the passage says.
- Our audit and search log: https://github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/blob/main/ciphers/fr4712-nevers-duchesse/AUDIT.md
  (section "AUDIT 1").
