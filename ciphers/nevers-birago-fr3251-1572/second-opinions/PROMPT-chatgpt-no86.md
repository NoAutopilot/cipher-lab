THE ITEM
- Lodovico Birago (French governor of the Marquisate of Saluzzo) to Louis de Gonzague, duc de Nevers, Saluzzo,
  27 August 1572. Bibliothèque nationale de France, ms. français 3251, ff.170-177 (no.86); the cipher is on
  f.174r (foot), f.174v (a full page), f.175r (head) and f.175v. Gallica
  https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9060248g/f178.item (f.174v). Italian prose. The clear text names
  a "Mons. di Monte..." expected to come, the "Maestro di Campo Autefort" (Hautefort), "il Baron de Sadres", Voluera and
  Carmagnola's garrison ("buona parte uganotti").
- Key: the "Nevers-Birago cipher (1572)", reconstructed by Satoshi Tomokiyo from the decipherment attached to
  no.87 of the same volume (https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/nevers.htm, section BnF fr.3251).
- Under the printed key, a value-blind transcription of all 759 cipher signs gives a fragmentary reading. The key
  beats 200 of 200 shuffled keys, z about 3.6-3.8. Fragments include "huomini e [carmagnola] ne restano", "ambidoi
  sono [ugonotti]", "domestico loro", "non posino", "baron de Sadr..", "bene a caro le cose", "la mala gratia
  d'altri", "pratica stretta" and "del governo con il baron". Nothing more is claimed; the text fails a language
  judge and 54 signs are unkeyed.
- Our audit and search log: https://github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/blob/main/ciphers/nevers-birago-fr3251-1572/AUDIT.md
  (section "no.86"). Volume 1 of the 1665 Mémoires de Monsieur le duc de Nevers was searched inside on Gallica
  with no hit; volume 2 was not found.
