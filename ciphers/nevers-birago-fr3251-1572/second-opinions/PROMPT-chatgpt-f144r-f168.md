  (a) no.73, 27 March 1572, f.144r (cipher run mid-page) and f.144v (a second short run, not yet transcribed);
      Gallica https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9060248g/f146.item and .../f147.item. The clear text of
