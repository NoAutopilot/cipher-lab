# Request: nevers-birago-fr3251-1572 -- the tipped-in sheet at f.179 (BnF fr.3251)

No personal data below; log the person's own archive request separately, by date and archive only, per
CLAUDE.md rule 9.

**What's needed:** an archive photograph (colour, highest resolution, both recto and verso) of the small
loose sheet of paper tipped in near f.179v of BnF fr.3251, visible on Gallica canvas 183 lying at an angle
over what would be f.179v.

**Why this leaf:** Tomokiyo's note on the Nevers-Birago Cipher (1572) says the key "can be reconstructed from
the decipherment attached to no.87" (`sources/cryptiana/web/nevers.htm`, section id=BnFfr3251). No.87 (Lodovico
Birago to the Duke of Nevers, Saluzzo, 8 September 1572) is confirmed by eye-checked ink foliation to run from
the last two lines of f.178r through all of f.178v and the first three lines of f.179r (LANE NEV worker NEV-C1,
27 Sept 2026); it carries no interlinear plaintext anywhere on the page. The tipped-in sheet at canvas 183,
immediately after this cipher passage, is the only candidate location for the clerk decipherment the key was
built from -- a faint archival annotation in its bottom-left corner was read by a subagent as possibly
containing "87" and a date-like group resembling "1572" (confidence L-M, not confirmed). Without this sheet,
none of the seven undeciphered 1572 letters in this folder (ff.138, 144, 152, 160, 168, 174, 184) can be
calibrated against a genuine period plaintext; the fallback, already running in parallel, is a control-backed
decode of the no.87 cipher passage itself against 20 shuffled keys (LANE NEV, NEV-C2 cycle).

**Why a person is needed:** the sheet's face photographed on Gallica shows only out-of-focus, low-contrast ink
bleed-through from its far side -- confirmed independently at three zoom/contrast levels by one worker and by a
second subagent given only an enhanced crop, both reaching the same conclusion (`images/f179v_insert_crop_enhanced.png`,
NOTES.md "The attached decipherment"). Neither the front-facing legible side of this loose sheet, nor its
correct orientation, is available through Gallica's existing digitisation at any crop or exposure this project
can apply from the image alone; a physical/archival examination or a fresh photograph of the actual sheet (not
a re-crop of the same scan) is the only route to its other side.

**Cote and location:** BnF, Département des Manuscrits, Français 3251 (ancienne cote Anc. 8760). Gallica ark
`btv1b9060248g`, canvas 183 (`https://gallica.bnf.fr/iiif/ark:/12148/btv1b9060248g/f183/full/full/0/native.jpg`).
Finding aid: `archivesetmanuscrits.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cc49712p` -- fetched and read 27 Sept 2026 (this job): cote
confirmed as "Français 3251"; the item list gives item 87 as "Lettre, avec chiffre, de LODOVICO BIRAGO... au duc
de Nevers... Da Saluzzo, li 8 di settembre 1572" starting at "Fol. 178", with the next item (88) starting at
"Fol. 181" -- consistent with no.87 running through the loose sheet's position at canvas 183, but the finding
aid's item list does not separately name a tipped-in insert or an "attached decipherment," so its identity is
not confirmed by the catalogue text alone. The finding aid also names a substitute microfilm cote for the whole
volume, "MF 31541" -- likely the same source used for the existing Gallica digitisation and its bleed-through
image of this sheet, so a fresh photograph of the original loose sheet itself (not a reproduction ordered
against that microfilm) is what this request needs.

**Format requested:** colour digital photograph (research use), both faces of the loose sheet, at the highest
resolution the department offers for this format; no print or certified copy needed.

**Where to send it:** BnF, Département des Manuscrits. Per the Access playbook and the routes already
