## Summary (English, 3 lines)

1. Read (fragments): talk of a negotiation with a count and of "articles" the writer sent; "to fortify themselves"; a captain Scipione; "after the execution" the house of Montmorency "remains under some suspicion" and many are "stunned and doubtful"; a stronghold "in favour of the Huguenots".
2. Read (fragments): a Francesco Ga...o "has presented the letters" but nothing has come back in writing, and he has asked for money and a subsidy; someone "has his mind on the government of Turin"; over religion "there will always be confusions and difficulties", to the prejudice of the service; someone should "make the Queen understand" that everything depends on the house of Montmorency; Carmagnola again at the end.
3. Inferred, not read: that "the execution" refers to the events of late August 1572 in France, that "the Queen" is the Queen Mother, and that the letter concerns Huguenot sympathies around Carmagnola and Montmorency's standing; none of these identifications is in the cipher text itself.
