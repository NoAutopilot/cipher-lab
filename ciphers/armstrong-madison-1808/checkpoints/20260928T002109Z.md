## Completed results

### 1. Armstrong to Monroe, 30 May 1806

Frames 852–853 are the dated witness and docket. Frame 852 is headed “Paris 30th May 1806,” signed J. Armstrong, and addressed to Monroe. It is ordinary prose concerning information Mr. Sullivan was to procure and Sullivan being too unwell to transact business. Frame 853 is docketed “May 30 1806 Genl Armstrong.” No numerical or graphic coded passage was observed on these two surfaces.

This is a bounded negative result for this exact witness, not a claim about other Armstrong correspondence.

### 2. Armstrong to Monroe, 27 February 1806

Frames 749–751 are the witness and docket. Frame 749 is headed “Paris 27 feb. 1806” and begins “Dear Sir”; it introduces an enclosed letter by Mr. Symmes of New Orleans and discusses the Spanish/French controversy. Frame 750 continues and ends with John Armstrong's signature. Frame 751 is docketed “27 Feby 1806 Genl Armstrong.” No numerical or graphic coded passage was observed on the three surfaces.

Again, this is a bounded negative for the inspected witness, not evidence that Armstrong lacked a cipher.

### 3. Independent decoded control for WE028

Frames 740–744 are a separate letter: George W. Erving to James Monroe, Paris, 5 February 1806, marked Private and signed on frame 744. Frames 742–743 contain extensive numerical code with contemporary interlinear plaintext.

The interlinear readings visibly agree with the complete WE028 table already in the repository. Directly checked examples are:

| Codes | Table pieces | Interlinear reading |
|---|---|---|
| 134 1379 | mis + ter | Mister |
| 1399 1229 637 | this + govern + ment | this government |
| 1576 1385 970 | of + the + florida | of the Floridas, with a small manuscript mark following 970 |
| 648 | million | millions in the witness when followed by a small mark |
| 794 | dollar | dollars in the witness when followed by a small mark |
| 569 182 | to + him | to him |

The small marks therefore function as suffix/plural marks in these particular WE028 contexts. That behavior must not be transferred to Armstrong's cipher without independent evidence.

This is a positive primary-source control for WE028, not a discovery of Armstrong's key. It also reinforces the published distinction between the Adams/official code with THE=1385 and Armstrong's reported code with THE=972.

def exact_bytes(path, expected_sha):
    data = Path(path).read_bytes()
    if hashlib.sha256(data).hexdigest() == expected_sha:
        return data
    if data.endswith(b"\n") and hashlib.sha256(data[:-1]).hexdigest() == expected_sha:
        return data[:-1]
    raise ValueError(f"hash mismatch: {path}")
