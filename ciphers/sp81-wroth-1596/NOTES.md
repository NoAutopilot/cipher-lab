Preceding half of the paragraph (the start of the Wroth sentence, before "go see those places") was not retrieved; the volume's other Wroth mentions are items 9, 63, 206 and 326 (p. 259 "Wrothe delivered Her Majesty's letters to the States" is the 326 context), none flagged cipher.
