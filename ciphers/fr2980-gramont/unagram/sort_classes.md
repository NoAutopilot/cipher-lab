# UNA-GRAM sorter's class descriptions (one Sonnet call, verbatim)

C1: curl/hook "d"-like sign: a thick stroke curving over into a closed loop at the bottom right (open top-left stem, round bowl).
C2: "f"-like sign: a long diagonal/curved stem with a small hook or flag at the top right and a crossing bar or tail near the bottom left.
C3: "Z"/"2"-like sign: a short top stroke, a diagonal, and a long baseline sweep to the right (thin, sloping z).
C4: heavy blocky "Z/E" sign: a short upper bar and a stubby diagonal, with a fat, squat lower body and a tick/flag at the upper left.
C5: tall composite sign: a vertical or curled left stroke with a top bar and a tail (J/T-like with a curl at the bottom left).
C6: "H"/"n" shape: two uprights joined by a crossbar, with a flat base.
C7: small "9"/loop-on-stem sign: a round loop at the bottom with a tail rising right.
C8: single thin diagonal stroke ("\" or "/"), with no loop or bar.
Sorter's note: 2 tiles ambiguous among C3/C4, chosen by the stem and flag at upper left (C4) versus a plain sweep (C3).
