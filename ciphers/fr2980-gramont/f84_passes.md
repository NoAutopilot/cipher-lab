3. TOPICS
The letter reports field news from northern Italy: cavalry and foot companies led by the count Claude Rangon went out from Piacenza toward the Trebbia, killed many of the enemy and fought some skirmish; a count (Gaiazzo?) has left the Empire's service for the pope's. The postscript adds news of a marquis and a count Jean, a muster under an ensign, a report of ten thousand(?) lansquenets killed, and a count made marquis, apparently in the king's service, all with low reading confidence.

3. SUMMARY

The letter reports Italian campaign news. Count Claude Rangone's light horse and foot sallied from Piacenza toward the Trebbia and Po. A skirmish followed against the imperial side, in which a Burgundian captain was taken. A count has left the Emperor's service for the Pope's. The writer is in good health and recommends himself, and the letter closes with a reference to the king, François I. The postscript, which is badly legible, names a marquis and a count Jehan, mentions Piedmont and Rome, and appears to pass on a forwarded enclosure or news. Both summaries are low confidence.
