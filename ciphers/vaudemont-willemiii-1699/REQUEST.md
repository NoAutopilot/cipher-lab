**What's needed:** identification and a copy (photograph or digital scan) of one letter: Vaudemont
(Charles Henri de Lorraine, prince de Vaudemont, or his wife Anna Elisabeth de Lorraine, princesse de
Vaudemont) to Willem III or Hans Willem Bentinck, *25 March 1699, described in the printed edition's own
alphabetical index (N. Japikse, ed., *Correspondentie van Willem III en van Hans Willem Bentinck, eersten

**Why a request is needed:** the originals of the Willem III/Bentinck correspondence are held in the Portland

playbook already states these papers are not copy-free. No online catalogue search for the item-level call

deciphered ("ook een ontcijferde brief is aanwezig") -- finding which one, from the Huygens retroboeken viewer
alone (no archive visit needed for that step), would identify a solved sibling before any copy order is placed.
That triage is cheap and copy-free; worth doing before committing to a request for the 25 March original.
