## What the leaves actually show

Finding aid (`archivesetmanuscrits.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cc50509p`, fetched 23 Sept 2026): a cluster of related
items around Fol.63-64 --

- Fol. 63 - 66 "Proceiz fait a Bimart, sur la depesche surprise du president Du Faure, et sa confession."
- Fol. 63 - 67 "Lettre de creance du presidant Du Faure au Sr Galepin, qui se tenoit a Beaucaire... De
  Frontignan, ce 19 juillet 1622."
- Fol. 63 - 68 "**Lettre en chiffre du presidant Du Faure a Galepin.**" (the QUEUE M11 target item)
- Fol. 63 - 69 "Memoire du presidant Du Faure, trouve a Giraut."
- Fol. 64 - 70 a further letter of creance, 20 July 1622.

Both leaves viewed via Gallica IIIF (`images/f63_item66_proces_bimart.jpg` = canvas f76/folio 63,
`images/f64_item68-69_plaintext.jpg` = canvas f77/folio 64; calibration in `images/manifest.json`).

**Item 66 (folio 63 recto) explains the whole cluster and is itself plain French throughout:** "Ginaud secretaire
du president du Faure fut pris aupres Aigremortes par ceux de Nismes, charge d'une depesche ou une partie de ses
memoires et instructions estoyent en chiffre, par ou on aprit les intelligences de plusieurs personnes qui
vouloyent trahir le Duc de Rohan et le party qu'il soustenoit, sur tout en... la ville de Montpellier...
Ginaud est mort a la bonne heure pour luy... **Les pieces justificatives de ce que dessus sont incerees cy
dessoubz**" (the supporting documents are inserted immediately below). This is a Crown/Rohan-party
counter-intelligence dossier: Du Faure's secretary was intercepted in 1622 carrying a partly-enciphered
despatch, and what follows in the register (items 67-69) is the captured material as entered into this record.

**Item 68 (folio 64 recto), the letter the QUEUE row names, is headed "Lettre en Chiffre du president du Faure a
Galepin" and the body text immediately below the heading is fully plain, fully readable French** -- "Voicy
l'occasion pour servir vos amis... Monsieur de Bertrieres veut servir et Monsieur Bimart aussy..." -- continuing
for the rest of the page with no cipher symbols anywhere. Item 69 ("Memoire du president du Faure trouve a
Giraut"), on the same leaf, is likewise entirely plain French.

**Conclusion: as with M10 (fr.4137/Sabran), the catalogue's "en chiffre" records how the original was sent, not
the state of what survives here.** This register (also explicitly a captured-papers dossier, item 66's own
text) preserves the **already-deciphered or decoded content** of Du Faure's letter -- almost certainly the
crown's own 1622 decoding of the intercepted despatch, made as part of building the case against Rohan's
correspondents, not the ciphertext itself. No cipher symbol, numeral run, or undeciphered passage appears
anywhere on either leaf viewed.
