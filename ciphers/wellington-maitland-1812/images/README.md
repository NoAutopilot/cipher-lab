# Spink catalogue images, lot 1184, saved 19 Sept 2026

These are Spink's catalogue photographs of lot 1184 ("Great Britain / Duke of Wellington / Peninsular War etc / General
Frederick Maitland (1763-1848)") in sale 26066, *Historical Documents, Autographs and Ephemera*, timed from 28 Aug 2026 with
the live session on 23 Sept 2026 at 14:00 London time (live.spink.com). They were downloaded on 19 Sept 2026 for private
study before the sale, because the lot goes to a private buyer and the images may be taken down afterwards. Copyright in the
photographs is Spink's. **The user decides later whether these files stay in a public repository**; until then treat them
as study copies, do not redistribute them, and cite the lot page rather than this folder.
