## Reconciliation of the transcription

Two subagents transcribed the ten entries from the page images independently (pass A, pass B, 19 Sept 2026),
without access to the volunteer text; the orchestrator compared both with the Decoding the Civil War
transcription on the item pages and re-read the page image at every disagreement. Disagreements and decisions:

- T1 line 2, third word: A "Jim", B and volunteers "you"; the image shows a short cramped word; OR prints "you".
  Kept as "you[?]".
- T1 line 4: A "Ohii[?]", B and volunteers "Ohio". Kept "Ohio".
- T1 signature line: all three see the ink line "signed G B McClellan Maj Genl" with the G struck and "Andes"
  written after it, and pencil words above ("Im", "for", "the.", "union"): the clerk's plain signature was
  replaced by the coded tail "Andes Im for the union". Transcribed as ink line plus <ins> pencil words.
- T2 line 8: A and B "first[?]" (a lead-in stroke), volunteers "first". Kept "first".
- T3 line 12: B "Call[?]" (written Cale). Kept "Call". Line 15: both passes "despatches", volunteers
  "dispatches". Kept "despatches".
- T4 dateline: both passes "5.31[?]", volunteers "5.30". Kept "5.31[?]"; the OR gives no time. Line 5: A
  "this[?]", B "this". Kept "this".
- T5 dateline: both passes "10[?]" (could be 11); the OR date is 10 Feb. Kept "10[?]".
- T7 line 9 last word: A "Proof[?]", B "Hoof", volunteers "hoof". Kept "Hoof" (OR "hoof"). Line 14: A and
  volunteers "One P M", B "One P 711[?]". Kept "One P M".
- T8 line 3 and 8: A "Brmen[?]", B and volunteers "Bremen". Kept "Bremen". Line 7: B "co operation", A and
  volunteers "cooperation". Kept "cooperation". Line 29: A "unnathe[?] mn[?]", B "unalte[?] run[?]",
  volunteers "wreathe run"; the OR has "gunboat run" and the ledger uses "wreathe" for gunboat elsewhere
  (p.[26], [34], [49], [57]). Kept "wreathe[?] run".
- T9 line 5: A "Betta[?] at onco[?]", B and volunteers "Better at once". Kept "Better at once". Line 7:
  A "Andy[?]", B "Andq[?]", volunteers "Andes"; the image shows a hasty "Andes" with a descender. Kept
  "Andes[?]". A "Dorathy[?]", B and volunteers "Dorothy". Kept "Dorothy".
- T10: no disagreement.

Passes A and B agreed on every code word except the three flagged above (Bremen, wreathe, Andes), all of
which the third witness and the printed text resolve.
