### T1 | Page [9] | 4964 | February 5th 1862, McClellan to Halleck
February 5th 1862
For Alden have Telegraphed Buell to
communicate with you[?] and suggested demonstration
on Sermon have just moved eight
Regts from Ohio into western Merlin,
cannot well spare more thence from
Alvord unless absolutely necessary please communicate
fully with Alvord and with me
signed <del>G</del> Andes B <ins>Im</ins> McClellan <ins>for</ins> Maj <ins>the.</ins> Genl <ins>union</ins>

### T2 | Page [15] | 4970 | Feb 7 '62 7 PM, McClellan to Halleck
Feb 7 '62 7 PM
Alden Number one Dispatch received I
congratulate you upon the result of
your operations they have caused the
utmost satisfaction here I would not
undertake a dash at Saffron now
better devote everything towards turning it
first collecting a sufficient force near
Forts Henry & Donelson to make
success sure Either alvord or yourself
should soon go to the scene
of operations Why not have Alvord
take the line of Lonesome &
operate on Shylock while your troops
turn Saffron those two points gained
a combined movement on Torrent will
be next in order The bridges
at Tuscumbia & Decatur should at
all hazards be destroyed at once
Please number telegraphic dispatches & give
hour of transmittal Thank Bremen Foote
& their commands for me Andes

### T3 | Page [16] | 4971 | Feby 7th 1862 7 1/4 PM, McClellan to Buell
Feby 7th 1862 - 7 1/4 PM
Alvord Number one Why not take
the line of the Lonesome with
Your Command and operate on Shylock
while Alden turns union city and
Saffron I have directed him to
destroy bridge at Decatur if possible
to reach it after Carrying Shylock
and Saffron a Combined attack on
Torrent Could be made it would
easily fall if bridge at Decatur
destroyed Call for all available troops
in Koran Lark and Lather please
number and give hour of transmittal
of telegraphic despatches signed Andes Eugenia

### T4 | Page [19] | 4974 | Feb 9 '62 5.30 PM, Stanton to Buell
<ins>Gen Buell</ins> Feb 9 '62 - 5.31[?] PM
Alvord The President directs me to
say that he has read your
communication to Andes dated the first
and mailed the sixth of this
month and that he approves the
operations you propose therein & believes
if vigrously prosecuted they cannot fail
He desires you and Alden to
cooperate as far as possible &
says that your two heads together
will succeed signed E M Stanton
Secy of War Nancy good evening

### T5 | Page [21] | 4976 | Feb 10 '62, Lincoln to Hunter and Lane
Feb 10[?] '62
For Carroll and General Lane Quotient
<ins>Gen Lane</ins> Quotient My wish has
been & is to avail the
Govt of the services of both
Carroll & Gen Lane & so
far as possible to personally oblige
both Carroll is the senior officer
& must command when they serve
together though in so far as
he can consistently with the public
service & his own honor oblige
Genl Lane he will also oblige
me, If they can not come
to an amicable understanding Genl Lane
must report to Carroll for duty
according to the rules or decline
the service signed Irving Bush please
send far as possible in cipher

### T7 | Page [35] | 4990 | Feb 16 '62 1 PM, McClellan to Buell
Feb 16 '62 - 1 PM.
Alvord If I can send you
motive power & cars to Sermon
by way of Mystic can you
not at once whig on Shylock
in force without waiting for repairs
of road Time is now everything
If Shylock is open the men
could carry their small rations &
bread driving meat on the Hoof
Leave tents & all baggage If
you can occupy Shylock at once
it will end the war in
lonesome signed Andes One P M

### T8 | Page [36] | 4991 | Feb 16 '62, Lincoln to Halleck
Feb 16 '' 62
Alden you have fort Donelson safe
unless Bremen shall be overwhelmed from
outside to prevent which latter will
I think require all the vigilance
energy and skill of yourself and
Alvord acting in full cooperation Saffron
will not get at Bremen but
the force from Sermon will they
hold the railroad from Sermon to
within a few miles of fort
Donelson with the bridge at Clarksville
undisturbed it is unsafe to rely
that they will not dare to
expose Shylock to Alvord a small
part of their force can retire
slowly towards Shylock breaking up the
railroad as they go and keep
Alvord out of that city twenty
days meantime Shylock will will be
abundantly defended by forces from all
south and perhaps from here at
Manassas could not a cavalry force
from Camden on the upper Cumberland
dash across almost unresisted and cut
the railroad at or near Knoxville
lonesome in . the . midst . of . a
bombardment of fort Donelson why could
not a wreathe[?] run up and
destroy the bridge at Clarksville our
success or failure at fort Donelson
is vastly important and I beg
you to put your soul in
the effort I send a copy
of this to Alvord signed Irving

### T10 | Page [56] | 5011 | Feb 21 '62, McClellan to Buell
Feb 21 . 62
Alvord The whig on Shylock is
of the greatest importance If you
can reach it by the line
of the Cumberland more rapidly than
by Sermon at once change your
line of operations & I will
direct Aldens troops on Torrent &
Saffron What is Camden division doing
at Bardstown Rapid movements are now
necessary If Railway to Clarksville is
destroyed take the line of Myrtle
I repeat that both Alden &
yourself keep me too much in
the dark your reports are not
sufficiently numerous or explicit Andes Hannah
