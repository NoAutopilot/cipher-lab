THE ITEM
- Huntington Library, Thomas T. Eckert Papers, mssEC 15 ("War Department Ciphers Sent Feby. 1 to July 21, 1862"), page
  object 4992 (https://hdl.huntington.org/digital/collection/p16003coll11/id/4992), third entry, 16 Feb 1862, as transcribed
  by the Decoding the Civil War volunteers: "For Alvord give me distribution of your troops how many in sermon line and
  where placed those also on all other lines no matter how long a telegram it requires give me the exact state of affairs
  and everything about rebels Andes".
- Our reading: Alvord = Buell, Andes = McClellan (both already published by the Decoding the Civil War blog, 2017);
  Sermon = Bowling Green (from our alignment of this ledger with OR ser. I vol. 7 pp. 584, 624, 626). So: McClellan to
  Buell, Louisville, 16 Feb 1862, asking how many troops are on the Bowling Green line.
- Full files: https://github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/tree/main/ciphers/eckert-1862 (AUDIT.md, key.md, print/residue/).
