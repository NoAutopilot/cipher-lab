New York 14th Augt 80
received the 9th Sept
BODY
Monsieur Terney arrived the
twelfth ultimo at Rhode Island
with seven sail of the line three
Frigates and about five
thousand Troops who are
said to be sickly - there is little
probability of their attempting
Canada this year, their
principal Efforts being intended
against this Place -/
