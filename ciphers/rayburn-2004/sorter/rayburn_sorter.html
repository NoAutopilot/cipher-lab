Rayburn Sign Sorter

The Rayburn sheet (2004; Schneier's 2006 image, byte-identical to ours), 80 signs: 10 written rows (64) and the two margin columns (8 + 8). Each tile is one sign with its own underline or strike-through and any small sign written just under it (k+r, A+m, R+H, a+v). Starting piles are the reconciled reading, capitals and small letters kept apart. Merge piles that are one sign, split a pile that holds two, move a tile to where it belongs, or send it to BAD-CUT / not a letter. The rectangle round rows 1-2 was drawn later by a family member and is left out; so are the vertical word beside the right margin and the circled mark below the grid.

Step 1 · Clean each pileLook along a pile. Tap any sign that is not like the others: it moves to the "Taken out" tray at the bottom. Tap it there to put it back. When a pile looks right, press "This pile is done".
See a sign largerPress and hold a sign (or press "View larger" on a pile): it opens on its line, the sign in brackets, with Next and Previous.
Step 2 · Place the taken-out signsEach taken-out sign is shown large with every other pile below it, closest-looking first. Drag it onto its pile, or just tap the pile. "None of these: new sign" starts a new pile; nothing to type.

Put each taken-out sign where it belongs

Drag the sign onto the pile it matches, or tap the pile's name. The closest-looking piles come first. Tap a small picture inside a pile to open that pile and look at it larger. Drag a small picture onto another pile to move that tile; drag a pile's name (✥) onto another pile to merge the whole pile (on a phone: press a moment, then drag).

Drag an edge of the box, or drag inside it to move the whole box. Drag a round corner handle to move that corner alone (a slanted or uneven box for a wide looped sign). On a phone, the arrows move one edge or the chosen corner 2 pixels a tap. "Erase stray ink": paint over a neighbour's ink inside the box and it is left out of the tile (shown as paper); "Undo stroke" takes back the last stroke. One tile is one box (no "split here"): if the box holds two signs, fit it to the first sign and mark the second with "Bad cut" as now.
