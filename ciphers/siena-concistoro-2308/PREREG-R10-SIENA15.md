# PREREG R10-SIENA15 -- no. 15 against the other 16th-century key sheets of fasc. 1, shape presence (6 Oct 2026, written 09:5x UTC by date -u, pushed before any key image was viewed)

Question: does any 16th-century or undated alphabet key in Concistoro 2308 fasc. 1 (DECODE R4746-R4789) carry the sign families that
no. 15 (R4803, Mario Bandini, Dec 1546 / Jan 1547) uses and R4764 lacks (R9-SIENA15)?

Families (names from Bourdeau's no15v.txt legend): F1 O2 = double circle / bullseye (◎); F2 U = two uprights joined at the foot (⊔);
F3 XT/XH = x with a bar on top, at the foot or through the middle; F4 R = a capital-R-shaped sign used as a cipher sign.

Sheets to examine (fixed now): R4765, R4760, R4767, R4768, R4776, R4789, and the ids absent from Bourdeau's table R4766, R4769, R4774,
R4777, R4784 (whatever they turn out to be; any that is not an alphabet key is logged as such and skipped). Excluded and why: R4764
(R9-SIENA15, 0 of 4 recorded), R4773 (all-numeric), R4785/R4786 (code books), R4787 (titles book), R4788 (genealogy); the 15th-century
keys (Bourdeau's dates) are out of scope.

Criterion (presence, read by eye on the sheet's alphabet, null and doubles rows; a sign in a clear-text label does not count):
- CANDIDATE iff >= 3 of F1-F4 are drawn on the sheet as cipher signs.
- STRONG iff CANDIDATE and >= 10 of no. 15's 16 commonest signs (A U LAM R XT D O2 # H 8 PSI TH DOT 1 MM TO) are drawn on it.
- A sheet with 2 of 4 is logged "partial overlap", not a candidate.

Reader calibration (controls that can fail differently from the targets, done in the same session with the same viewing procedure,
before the candidates are scored):
- negative: R4764 P2 (key image) must read <= 1 of F1-F4 (R9-SIENA15 recorded 0);
- positive: one crop of no. 15 itself (R4803 P2, the L2.15-L2.16 band) must read >= 3 of F1-F4.
If either calibration misses, stop: log "non-test at this reader" and score nothing.

This is a lookup, not a decode: a CANDIDATE only names the sheet and its alphabet rows for a later scored test (a concordance + the
R9-SIENA15 S2 gate with its own controls); no reading, no grade, no key.tsv. If no sheet is a CANDIDATE, no. 15 goes to Remaining gaps
as too-short / no-key-material (no fitting key in fasc. 1).
