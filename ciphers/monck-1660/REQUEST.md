# Copy request: British Library, Add MS 32093 f.423

Take the exact contact route from bl.uk ("Manuscripts" > "Requesting items" / "Copying at the British Library")
rather than from here, since forms and prices change. The BL's online systems have been unreliable since the
2023 cyber attack; expect a slower, quote-based process than a normal digitisation order.

## The order

1. **Add MS 32093, f. 423** ("Letter partly in cipher, undeciphered, relating, apparently to Gen. Monck and his
   movements in 1659 and 1660. Imperfect."). Ask for a high-resolution digitisation of f. 423 (both sides) via
   BL Imaging Services. Say why: to attempt a decipherment of the ciphered portion.
2. Ask for a price quote up front — no fixed reproduction price is published online for this kind of item; get
   the figure before committing.
3. Ask about the "Imperfect" note in the catalogue: whether this means damage, a missing portion, or a loose
   leaf, since that affects what a scan will actually show.

## Route (bl.libguides.com, checked 20 Sept 2026)

- A Reader Pass issued after 21 March 2024 is required to request items or order reproductions of undigitised
  material. If the person does not have one, ask whether Imaging Services can quote and supply a copy without
  an in-person Reader Pass (some libraries allow remote reproduction requests without a reading-room visit;
  confirm with BL Customer Services or Imaging Services directly).
- Copyright clearance for use/publication of the image is the requester's responsibility per BL's copying
  guide; ask what BL needs for private research versus any later publication of a reading.

## Also worth asking about in the same message

- Whether Historical MSS Commission's 1876/1879 description of this collection (5th Report pp.308-320, 7th
  Report pp.428-433 — printed, likely on Google Books/HathiTrust already, so check there first before asking
  BL) says anything more about item 113 than the modern catalogue's one-line note.
