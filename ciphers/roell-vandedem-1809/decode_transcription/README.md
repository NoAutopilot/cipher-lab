# DECODE transcription of R1469 / R1470, as parsed by D. Bourdeau

`R1469_groups.txt` (1,297 groups) and `R1470_groups.txt` (1,288 groups) are copied unchanged on 2 Oct 2026 from
https://github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver/blob/main/targets/roell1809/ (raw.githubusercontent.com, main). They are
Bourdeau's `parse.py` output (MIT) over the DECODE transcription of records R1469 (7 pages, dated 9 Feb 1809) and
R1470 (6 pages, undated) by DECODE transcriber "SofPe", 2 Feb 2020 (per his NOTES.md). Dots separating groups are
dropped; "a few marks and uncertain digits are dropped" (his words), so this is a group list, not a line-faithful
transcription: no line breaks, no marks column (CLAUDE.md "Marks and numerals as drawn"). The DECODE source documents
themselves are account-gated (sources/decode/NOTES.md) and were not fetched. Credit: D. Bourdeau, cyphersolver (code
MIT, text CC BY 4.0); DECODE (de-crypt.org) records R1469/R1470. Highest values 5100 and 7205 (one each) are misreads
per Bourdeau; the next highest is 3264.
