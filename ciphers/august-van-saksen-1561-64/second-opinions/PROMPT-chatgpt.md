# Second opinion request: WVO 126, William of Orange to Elector August of Saxony, Brussels, 16 September 1564

I am checking whether a sixteenth-century cipher passage has already been deciphered or printed. Please answer only
with sources you can cite exactly: author, title, volume, year, and page or letter number. If you are unsure a source
exists, say so. Do not guess.

**The item.** A letter from William of Orange to August, Elector of Saxony, dated Brussels, 16 September 1564.
Original: Sächsisches Hauptstaatsarchiv Dresden, Geheimer Rat (Geheimes Archiv), Locat 8510/5, fol. 138r-140v. The
postscript on fol. 139 is in a symbol cipher. The draft (minute) is in the Koninklijk Huisarchief, The Hague, A 11/XIV
I/4 nr. 26, recorded "with a 'Zeitung'". There is also a 20th-century copy in the KHA's Collectie Japikse. Database
record: Briefwisseling van Willem van Oranje, letter 126
(https://resources.huygens.knaw.nl/wvo/app/brief?nr=126). The clear part of the letter begins "Wiewoll ich in gereumer
zeitt von E. Churf. G. khein schreiben entpfangen".

**Our reading of the cipher postscript (excerpt):** "...das wir seidhero auss Hispanien andere zeitung bekommen haben,
welche vermelden, das die Konnigin so heftig kranck geworden sei, das man ir die adern zwei mahl schlagen und auch
zwei mahl purgieren mussen, dermassen das sie irer frucht erlediget worden sei." (News from Spain: the Queen was bled
twice and purged twice and lost the child she was carrying. This is Elisabeth of Valois, August 1564.)

**What to look for.**
1. Any edition, calendar or study that prints or summarises this letter of 16 Sept 1564, especially its postscript
   or the Spanish news in it: Groen van Prinsterer, Gachard, Rachfahl, Kluckhohn, von Weber, Böttiger, Kretzschmar,
   Japikse, Demandt, P.J. Blok, Kervyn de Lettenhove, and works on Anna of Saxony.
2. Any printed text of the KHA minute or its 'Zeitung'.
3. Any study of the ciphers used between Orange and the Saxon court in 1561-1564.

Give exact citations only.
