THE ITEMS
- Reports in French of the Saxon envoy Ernst Christoph von Manteuffel from Berlin to the Saxon court (Flemming),
  October-November 1712: Sächsisches Hauptstaatsarchiv Dresden, 10026 Geheimes Kabinett, Loc. 694/08, digitised
  frames 0390, 0391, 0485 (images: https://www.archiv.sachsen.de/digitalisate/3a83f921-9a43-485f-874b-34653ed59b68/fullsize/0390.jpg,
  likewise .../0391.jpg and .../0485.jpg). Mostly clear French with short cipher groups. We applied someone else's key, a manuscript code table
  made by Dr. Krauske in 1893 (Loc. 694/10), not a period key and not our own.
- Frame 0390, letter of 10 Oct 1712: someone "avoit parlé touchant la trêve non seulement au roi de Prusse, qui se
  feroit un fort grand plaisir de pouvoir l'effectuer, mais aussi à" a person whose name reads "F r i b e n d" (perhaps
  the Swedish envoy Friesendorff; not settled).
- Frame 0391, postscript of 13 Oct 1712: Heusch tells Manteuffel the contents of a packet for Fabrice about how the
  Queen of England "s'est expliquée par ses ministres au sujet des affaires du nord": Oxford "a dit en gros" that the
  Queen was too far away to meddle in pacifying the North and would defer to Hanover's views on execution; but
  Bolingbroke's declaration "a été bien plus violente": the Queen wants absolutely to make peace in the North and, to
  get it more easily, the King of Denmark would be detached "bon gré malgré" from his allies.
- Frame 0485, letter of about 12 Nov 1712: Manteuffel read to the British envoy (the name reads "Breton", i.e. William
  Breton) the passage of the court's last letter concerning him, "et il en a été charmé"; "le point de la Courl[ande]
  iroit toujours bien".
- Our audit and search log: https://github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/blob/main/ciphers/sachsstaatsarchiv-manteuffel-1712/AUDIT.md
  (section "AUDIT (V-MANT08)"). Searched so far: Briefwisseling van Anthonie Heinsius XIV (Huygens full text),
  Droysen, Geschichte der preußischen Politik IV.1.
