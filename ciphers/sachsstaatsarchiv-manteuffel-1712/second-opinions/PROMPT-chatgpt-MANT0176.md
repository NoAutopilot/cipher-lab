THE ITEM
- A report in French of the Saxon envoy Ernst Christoph von Manteuffel from Berlin to Field Marshal Count Flemming,
  dated "a Berl. le 2 Juil. 1712": Sächsisches Hauptstaatsarchiv Dresden, 10026 Geheimes Kabinett, Loc. 694/08, digitised
  frame 0176 (image: https://www.archiv.sachsen.de/digitalisate/3a83f921-9a43-485f-874b-34653ed59b68/fullsize/0176.jpg); the
  letter continues on frame 0177. Mostly clear French with five short cipher runs. We applied someone else's key, a manuscript
  table made by Dr. Otto Krauske in 1893 (Loc. 694/10), not a period key and not our own.
- The clear text says Manteuffel had the commandant of Küstrin ordered, as if on his own motion, to hold back "la bibliotheque de
  Maier" (probably the library of the theologian Johann Friedrich Mayer, d. 1712), in case Prince Menshikov asked about it. The
  cipher runs read, with Krauske's table, only fragments: a name 'Maier' or 'Maierf' twice (the one who "enverroit deux colonels
  ... jusqu'a Bernau" and whose "certificat" is asked for), two colonels '(Sp)ald' and 'Horn', and the town 'Bernau'. One of our
  verifiers suggests, as an unproven guess, that 'Maierf' is the Swedish governor-general at Stettin, Meyerfeldt (Meijerfelt).
- Our audit and search log: https://github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/blob/main/ciphers/sachsstaatsarchiv-manteuffel-1712/AUDIT.md
  (sections "AUDIT (V-MANT0176)" and "AUDIT 2 (AUD2-MANT3)"). Searched so far: Acta Borussica, Behördenorganisation I (1894);
  Droysen, Geschichte der preußischen Politik IV.1 and IV.2; Berner (1901), Aus dem Briefwechsel König Friedrichs I. (full-text
  search only); Google Books, Internet Archive full text, OpenAlex. Not read: Sten Bonnesen, Studier över August II:s
  utrikespolitik 1712-1715, del I (Lund 1918); Europäische Fama and Mercure historique et politique for July 1712.
