SECOND OPINION REQUEST, label SO-WVO11008-CERTAIN

We are an open cipher-research repository (github.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab). We have read the short cipher passages
in one 1572 letter of William of Orange and want you to try to prove that the letter, or its cipher passages, were
already printed or deciphered before us, and to find mistakes in our reading. Be adversarial: we would rather learn now
that it is in print than claim it wrongly later.

THE ITEM
- William of Orange (writing as the merchant "George Certain") to his brother Louis of Nassau ("Lambert Certain",
  "estant pour le present a Tournay"), Cologne (Keulen), 12 August 1572. Koninklijk Huisarchief, The Hague, A 11/XI 15,
  original. Catalogue record and image: https://resources.huygens.knaw.nl/wvo/app/brief?nr=11008 and
  https://resources.huygens.knaw.nl/media/wvo/images/11000-11999/11008.pdf (WVO no. 11008; it gives no printed edition).
- Incipit: "J'ay recheu ce jourduy les deux vostres du 5 et 7 du courant, vous remerchiant".
- Five runs of numbers in the French clear text. Under the printed 1572 Orange-Nassau table (a=3, b=6 ... multiples of 3;
  other numbers nulls; printed by Nepveu tot Ameyde in the Algemeene Konst- en Letterbode) they read "le duc de holstein"
  (followed in clear by "lequel a faict charge pour ... 2000 escus"), "ermuyden" (Arnemuiden) and "ulgssinghen"
  (= vlissinghen, one code off). Two short runs are damaged or unread.
- Our files: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/main/ciphers/wvo-11008-certain-1572/NOTES.md
  (transcription, key, control), reading
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/main/ciphers/wvo-11008-certain-1572/reading.txt, search log
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NoAutopilot/cipher-lab/main/ciphers/wvo-11008-certain-1572/AUDIT.md

WHAT TO RETURN: for every place you find this letter or its cipher passages (printed, deciphered or discussed), the full
citation, page, and a link; any error you find in the reading or the key; and, if you find nothing, the list of what you
searched. Please do not describe our reading as new or first; we only want to know whether it is already in print.
